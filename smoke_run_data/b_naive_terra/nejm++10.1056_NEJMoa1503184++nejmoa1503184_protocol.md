# Clinical Study Protocol and Statistical Analysis Framework

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Background and Rationale

[Provided] GRIPHON evaluates selexipag (ACT-293987), a non-prostanoid IP-receptor agonist, against placebo in pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH), including morbidity/mortality, exercise capacity, and safety.

### 1.2 Objectives

[Provided] **P1:** Demonstrate the effect of selexipag versus placebo on time to first CEC-confirmed morbidity/mortality event in PAH.

[Provided] **S1:** Evaluate selexipag versus placebo for exercise capacity and other secondary/exploratory efficacy endpoints in PAH.

[Provided] **S2:** Evaluate safety and tolerability of selexipag in PAH in AC-065A302 and AC-065A303.

[Provided] Exploratory objectives are not separately labelled. Exploratory efficacy endpoints and exploratory dose-response, pharmacoeconomic, pharmacokinetic (PK), and safety analyses are enumerated.

[Clarification Required] Specify the individual secondary and exploratory endpoints and their assessment times. These are needed to define the corresponding analyses.

## 2. Study Methods

### 2.1 Trial Design

[Provided] AC-065A302/GRIPHON is a prospective, multicenter, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled, parallel-group, Phase 3, event-driven study. AC-065A303 is an open-label, uncontrolled extension for long-term safety and tolerability.

[Provided] Allocation is 1:1 placebo:selexipag. The planned enrollment is 1,150 patients, with 575 per group.

[Provided] The Day-1 evening dose is selexipag 200 µg twice daily or matching placebo. Weekly increments of 200 µg twice daily reach an individual maximum tolerated dose up to 1,600 µg twice daily; reductions are permitted for tolerability. From Week 12, the dose is held through Week 26; later increases are allowed at scheduled visits.

[Provided] Treatment continues until 331 CEC-confirmed events, with a maximum of 4.3 years.

[Clarification Required] Define whether the 4.3-year maximum applies to individual treatment duration or to study duration. This determines how the treatment limit is implemented.

### 2.2 Randomization

[Provided] An independent centralized IWRS/IVRS assigns a unique randomization number. The randomization code remains confidential until unblinding. Randomization is stratified by site.

[Clarification Required] Specify any blocking or minimization procedure and the unblinding procedures. These affect allocation implementation and protection of blinding.

### 2.3 Sample Size

[Provided] The final Version-7 specification plans 1,150 randomized patients and 331 first CEC-confirmed morbidity/mortality events. The earlier Version 1 allowed a blinded sample-size adaptation up to 1,150 patients.

[Provided] The calculation assumes constant event hazards of 0.2231/year for placebo and 0.145/year for selexipag, a target hazard ratio of 0.65, and a constant dropout hazard of 0.051/year in both groups. No separate numerical sample-size inflation for dropout was reported.

[Provided] The calculation assumes a one-sided unstratified log-rank test in a group-sequential design, variance under the alternative, one-sided overall α=0.005, and 90% power to reject the null. Superiority is the comparison; no non-inferiority or equivalence margin applies. The trial is individually randomized and non-clustered, so no clustering design effect applies.

[Provided] Under the stated assumptions, 331 events were expected to provide 90% power, and recruitment of up to 1,150 patients randomized 1:1 was expected to yield that target. EAST 5.3 was used with its variance-under-alternative option.

[Provided] The planned probability of rejecting the null at interim was approximately 19%. Under HR=1, the probability of accepting the null at interim was approximately 50%.

### 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

[Provided] For the primary endpoint, the one-sided null states that selexipag survival for remaining free of a first CEC-confirmed event is no greater than placebo at all times. The alternative states that it is no worse at all times and greater at some time. The primary comparison is superiority.

[Proposed] Report the selexipag-to-placebo hazard ratio as a summary of the treatment effect. **Rationale:** It complements the time-to-event comparison. **Assumption:** A single hazard ratio is an adequate summary; assess proportional hazards.

### 2.5 Statistical Interim Analyses

[Provided] The sample-size calculation uses a group-sequential design with one-sided overall α=0.005 and the interim operating characteristics stated in Section 2.3.

[Clarification Required] Specify the number and timing of interim analyses, information fractions, efficacy and futility boundaries, and the procedure for applying them. These decisions are necessary to implement the group-sequential design and interpret its overall error rate.

[Clarification Required] Specify who reviews interim results and the confidentiality, unblinding, and continuation procedures. These decisions protect trial integrity.

### 2.6 Timing of Analyses

[Provided] The final A302 analysis includes Treatment and post-treatment observation data, excluding Treatment Extension, with a cutoff of 27-Apr-2014. A303 safety data through 10-Mar-2014 are included.

[Provided] Screening occurs within 28 days before Day 1; baseline and randomization occur on Day 1. Titration continues through Week 8; visits occur at Weeks 12, 16, and 26, then every 6 months, followed by end of study (EOS) and safety follow-up.

[Provided] Study closure prompts EOS or a post-treatment observation closure visit (PTOCV) within four weeks. Treatment Extension runs from EOS to end of treatment extension (EOTE) after unblinding.

[Provided] Six-minute walk test (6MWT) and Borg assessments occur at baseline and scheduled follow-ups. Week-26 endpoint assessments occur before the morning dose, at trough.

## 4. Trial Population

### 4.1 Screening Data

[Provided] Screening is within 28 days before Day 1, and screening 6-minute walk distance (6MWD) must be 50–450 m.

[Proposed] Summarize the numbers screened, eligible, randomized, and not randomized, with reasons where recorded. **Rationale:** This describes selection into the trial. **Assumption:** Screening disposition and reasons are collected.

### 4.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

[Provided] Eligible patients are aged 18–75 years and have symptomatic Group-1 idiopathic, heritable, drug/toxin-induced, connective-tissue-disease-associated, repaired-shunt congenital-heart-disease-associated, or HIV-associated PAH.

[Provided] Documented right heart catheterization must show mean pulmonary arterial pressure ≥25 mmHg, pulmonary vascular resistance ≥400 dyn·s·cm−5, and pulmonary capillary wedge pressure or left ventricular end-diastolic pressure ≤15 mmHg.

[Provided] Women of childbearing potential require contraception with a failure rate <1%/year through one month after the final dose. Endothelin receptor antagonists and phosphodiesterase-5 inhibitors are permitted if stable for at least three months before baseline through Week 26.

[Provided] Exclusions include pulmonary hypertension Groups 2–5; prostacyclin or analogue treatment within one month or planned during the trial, except a single administration for acute vasodilator testing; and recent investigational drug use. Prostacyclin/analogues and investigational drugs are prohibited.

[Provided] Exclusions include obstructive disease with FEV1/FVC <70% **and** FEV1 <65% predicted; restrictive disease with total lung capacity <70% predicted; Child-Pugh B/C impairment; left ventricular ejection fraction <45%; severe renal dysfunction; and body mass index <18.5.

[Provided] Exclusions include inability to comply or perform the 6MWT; rehabilitation completed less than eight weeks before screening or planned; impaired consent or adherence capacity; life expectancy less than 12 months; and pregnancy or lactation.

[Clarification Required] Define “severe renal dysfunction” and “recent investigational drug,” including applicable thresholds or time windows. These are needed for reproducible eligibility decisions.

## 5. Analysis

### 5.1 Primary Outcomes

[Provided] The primary outcome is time to first CEC-confirmed morbidity/mortality event, comparing selexipag with placebo.

### 5.2 Secondary Outcomes

[Provided] Baseline and scheduled follow-up assessments include 6MWT and Borg assessments; Week-26 endpoint assessments are at trough.

### 5.3 Analysis Methods

[Proposed] Use an unstratified log-rank comparison for the primary endpoint, consistent with the sample-size calculation, and display event-free survival by randomized group using Kaplan–Meier estimates. **Rationale:** These methods address the event-time comparison. **Assumption:** The final analysis is intended to use the same unstratified test as the calculation.

[Proposed] Estimate the selexipag-to-placebo hazard ratio with a Cox model and assess proportional hazards. **Rationale:** This provides an interpretable effect estimate and checks its suitability. **Assumption:** Event times and censoring are defined and sufficiently observed.

[Clarification Required] Confirm whether the primary test or model must account for site stratification, and specify the final group-sequential decision rule. These choices affect the primary p-value and inference.

[Clarification Required] Specify methods and covariates for each secondary endpoint. Their choice depends on the endpoint definitions and intended comparisons.

### 5.4 Missing Data
