# Clinical Study Protocol and Statistical Analysis Framework

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Background and Rationale

**[Provided]** Empagliflozin (BI 10773) is evaluated in a Phase III programme to establish long-term cardiovascular safety in adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM), insufficient glycaemic control and increased cardiovascular (CV) risk. The study supports ongoing CV safety assessment and regulatory submission.

### 1.2 Objectives

**[Provided]** The primary objective is to demonstrate non-inferiority of pooled empagliflozin 10 mg and 25 mg once daily versus placebo for time to first 3-point major adverse cardiovascular event (3P-MACE), using a hazard-ratio (HR) margin of 1.3.

**[Provided]** Secondary objectives are to assess the adjudicated 4-point composite of 3P-MACE plus hospitalisation for unstable angina within the confirmatory hierarchy; and occurrence or time to silent MI, heart-failure hospitalisation, new albuminuria, new macroalbuminuria, new or worsening nephropathy and a composite microvascular outcome.

**[Provided]** Further secondary objectives are to assess CV death, fatal or nonfatal MI and stroke, unstable-angina hospitalisation, all-cause mortality, transient ischaemic attack (TIA) and coronary revascularisation; and change from baseline in HbA1c, fasting plasma glucose (FPG), weight, waist circumference and blood pressure (BP), the HbA1c/systolic BP (SBP)/weight composite and rescue-therapy use.

**[Provided]** Exploratory objectives are to describe pharmacokinetic (PK) trough concentrations, adverse events (AEs), hypoglycaemia, adverse events of special interest (AESIs), laboratory data, ECG, vital signs and optional pharmacogenomics. Further TSAP/Publication-SAP analyses include recurrent CV events, renal and microvascular endpoints, rescue therapy, risk factors and correlations of SBP and HbA1c with urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (UACR).

## 2. Study Methods

### 2.1 Trial Design

**[Provided]** This is an international, multicentre, randomised, parallel-group, double-blind, placebo-controlled, event-driven Phase III CV-safety study, with study drug added to standard care.

**[Provided]** Screening is followed by a 2-week open-label placebo run-in, then randomisation 1:1:1 to oral once-daily empagliflozin 10 mg, empagliflozin 25 mg or matched placebo. Double-blind treatment continues until the required primary events, followed by 30-day follow-up. Planned treatment is approximately 6–8 years, up to approximately 420 weeks, conditional on event accrual.

**[Provided]** During the first 12 post-randomisation weeks, pre-consent background antidiabetic therapy should remain unchanged where possible; rescue is permitted by criteria. After week 12, background therapy may be adjusted for clinical care.

**[Clarification Required]** The rescue criteria and procedures for maintaining or breaking the blind must be specified to operationalise treatment and protect trial integrity.

### 2.2 Randomization

**[Provided]** A third-party phone/web IVRS/IWRS assigns treatment using a computer-generated, pseudorandom, reproducible and non-predictable sequence. Randomisation is blocked and balanced within strata; no minimisation is reported.

**[Provided]** Strata are screening HbA1c <8.5% versus ≥8.5%; randomisation BMI <30 versus ≥30 kg/m²; North America, Latin America, Europe, Africa or Asia; and screening eGFR ≥90, 60–89 or 30–59 mL/min. For the TSAP primary model, North America includes Australia and New Zealand.

**[Provided]** Baseline tables compare planned IVRS strata with actual CRF strata. Analyses use actual CRF information, with IXRS values as a stated fallback for missing HbA1c or eGFR.

### 2.3 Sample Size

**[Provided]** The planned sample is 7,000: 2,333 assigned empagliflozin 10 mg, 2,333 empagliflozin 25 mg and 2,334 placebo. The event calculation pools active arms for a 2:1 active-to-placebo comparison.

**[Provided]** For time to first 3P-MACE, identical active and placebo hazards and an HR margin of 1.3 are assumed. The event table gives 516 and 690 events for 80% and 90% non-inferiority power, respectively, without interim adjustment; the interim-adjusted minimum is 691 adjudicated primary events.

**[Provided]** Separately, 691 events provide at least 80% power to detect an HR of 0.785, corresponding to a 21.5% risk reduction. The calculation uses a one-sided .025 significance level and a log-rank test in EAST version 5, stated to be equivalent to the Cox proportional-hazards score test. Variability is not applicable to this time-to-event calculation.

**[Provided]** No fixed placebo event rate is used for the event requirement. Duration scenarios use annual event rates of 1.0%, 1.5% and 2.0% and accrual periods of 2 or 2.5 years. With 7,000 patients, 24-month accrual at 3,500/year and a 1.5% annual event rate, duration is just under 8 years.

**[Provided]** The event count is independent of accrual, follow-up and annual event rate; the number randomised and duration depend on them. No numeric dropout or non-adherence inflation factor is reported; a clustering design effect is not applicable to this individually randomised trial.

**[Provided]** A blinded overall event-rate review may change the recruitment goal. A protocol amendment is required if assumptions or accrual require more recruitment or another change. The stopping basis is at least 691 adjudicated primary events, not 691 key-secondary events.

### 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

**[Provided]** Pooled empagliflozin versus placebo follows this fixed hierarchy, each test at one-sided alpha .025:

1. **[Provided]** Primary 3P-MACE non-inferiority: H0: HR ≥1.3.
2. **[Provided]** Key-secondary 4-point composite non-inferiority: H0: HR ≥1.3.
3. **[Provided]** Primary 3P-MACE superiority: H0: HR ≥1.
4. **[Provided]** Key-secondary 4-point composite superiority: H0: HR ≥1.

**[Provided]** Proceed only if the preceding 95% confidence interval (CI) upper bound is below 1.3 for non-inferiority or below 1.0 for superiority.

### 2.5 Statistical Interim Analyses

**[Provided]** The sample-size section states that one-sided alpha .0001 is spent at interim and .0249 remains for final analysis. Formal interim timing is not specified.

**[Clarification Required]** The interim timing, hypotheses, boundary, decision rules, access to unblinded results and oversight must be specified; these determine how the stated alpha spending is implemented.

### 2.6 Timing of Analyses

**[Provided]** Final analysis occurs after at least 691 adjudicated primary events. Patients are followed to trial termination; the end is last patient out. Data cutoff, database lock and other data-readiness requirements are not specified.

**[Provided]** Post-randomisation visits occur at weeks 4, 8, 12, 16, 28, 40 and 52, then every 14 weeks. HbA1c, FPG, weight, waist circumference and BP are assessed at weeks 12 and 52, annually and at end of study.

**[Provided]** The final/end-of-study visit includes drug collection and compliance, physical examination, laboratory assessment and ECG. Visit 98 occurs 30 days after final/end-of-study and includes BP and pulse, weight and waist, blood and urine safety laboratories, AEs and endpoints, and concomitant therapy.

**[Clarification Required]** The cutoff, lock and adjudication-readiness procedures must be determined to define the final analysis dataset and verify the event requirement.

## 3. Statistical Principles

### 3.1 Significance and Confidence Intervals

**[Provided]** Confirmatory tests use one-sided alpha .025 and the hierarchical 95% CI upper-bound rules. The stated interim and final alpha allocations are .0001 and .0249, respectively.

**[Clarification Required]** Reconcile the nominal 95% CI hierarchy rule with the .0249 final alpha and specify compatible boundaries or adjusted intervals; this affects confirmatory decisions and overall error control.

**[Proposed]** Report effect estimates with two-sided 95% CIs for non-confirmatory analyses without claiming controlled significance. This supports interpretation; it assumes no additional multiplicity scheme is adopted.

### 3.2 Protocol Deviations

**[Proposed]** Document and classify deviations before unblinding, including eligibility, treatment and assessment deviations, and summarise them by assigned group. This supports interpretation; it assumes an agreed deviation review process.

**[Clarification Required]** Define important deviations and their consequences for analysis populations; these decisions affect sensitivity analyses.

### 3.3 Analysis Populations

**[Proposed]** Analyse efficacy primarily by randomised assignment and safety by treatment received. This preserves the randomised comparison and describes exposure-related harms; it assumes treatment and exposure records are reliable.

**[Clarification Required]** Define eligibility for each analysis set, including randomised but untreated patients, and any per-protocol set. These definitions affect denominators and non-inferiority interpretation.

## 4. Trial Population

### 4.1 Screening Data

**[Proposed]** Summarise numbers screened, entering run-in, randomised and not randomised, with available reasons for exclusion. This describes selection; it assumes screening disposition is collected.

### 4.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

**[Provided]** Inclusion requires T2DM before consent, diet and exercise, consent, BMI ≤45 kg/m², and either no antidiabetic treatment for 12 weeks or stable background therapy, including insulin dose within 10% for 12 weeks. Screening HbA1c must be 7.0%–10.0%, or 7.0%–9.0% if drug-naïve.

**[Provided]** Age must be ≥18 years, except Japan ≥20 years and India 18–65 years. Qualifying high CV risk comprises prior MI; specified multivessel or single-vessel coronary artery disease or unstable angina; prior stroke; or peripheral arterial disease. Women of child-bearing potential require acceptable contraception and periodic pregnancy testing.

**[Provided]** Exclusions include run-in fasting glucose >240 mg/dL confirmed on a second day; liver enzymes or alkaline phosphatase >3× upper limit of normal; planned cardiac procedure within 3 months; eGFR <30; malabsorption surgery; dyscrasia or haemolysis; cancer within 5 years except basal-cell carcinoma; contraindicated background therapy; and unstable weight intervention.

**[Provided]** Further exclusions include systemic steroids or uncontrolled endocrinopathy; pregnancy, nursing or unsuitable contraception; alcohol or drug abuse or poor compliance; recent or ongoing investigational trial; an unsafe condition; and acute coronary syndrome, stroke or TIA within 2 months. Canada and South Africa have local exclusions whose details are not specified.

**[Clarification Required]** Specify the qualifying coronary disease criteria, acceptable contraception, local exclusions and other operational definitions needed for consistent eligibility assessment.

## 5. Analysis

### 5.1 Primary Outcomes

**[Provided]** The primary outcome is time to first 3P-MACE for pooled empagliflozin 10 mg and 25 mg versus placebo, with HR non-inferiority margin 1.3.

**[Clarification Required]** Specify the 3P-MACE components, time origin, event ascertainment, adjudication and censoring rules. These are necessary to construct and analyse the endpoint.

### 5.2 Secondary Outcomes

**[Provided]** The key secondary outcome is the adjudicated 4-point composite of 3P-MACE plus hospitalisation for unstable angina, tested within the confirmatory hierarchy. Other secondary outcomes are the CV, renal, microvascular and diabetes-related outcomes listed in Section 1.2.

**[Clarification Required]** Specify endpoint definitions, including renal thresholds, microvascular and HbA1c/SBP/weight composite components, rescue criteria and whether each outcome is analysed as occurrence, time to event or both. These choices determine the estimands and analyses.

### 5.3 Analysis Methods

**[Proposed]** Estimate pooled active versus placebo HRs and CIs using a Cox proportional-hazards model, with treatment and the actual stratification factors; apply the stated IXRS fallback for missing HbA1c or eGFR. This aligns the analysis with randomisation and the stated Cox/log-rank framework; it assumes model specification and covariate handling are confirmed.

**[Proposed]** Display event counts and Kaplan–Meier curves for the primary and key-secondary endpoints, and assess proportional hazards. This aids interpretation; it assumes event and censoring dates are available.

**[Proposed]** Summarise binary outcomes by group and analyse continuous changes from baseline with models accounting for baseline value and scheduled time. This accommodates differing outcome types; it assumes suitable measurements and model assumptions.

**[Clarification Required]** Specify the primary estimand, covariate parameterisation, handling of treatment discontinuation, rescue and death, and the detailed models for secondary outcomes. These choices affect the treatment effect being estimated.

### 5.4 Missing Data

**[Proposed]** Summarise missing assessments and follow-up by group and reason, and assess sensitivity to plausible departures from primary assumptions. This makes missing-data risks visible; it assumes reasons can be recorded.

**[Clarification Required]** Define event censoring, missing covariate handling beyond the stated IXRS fallback, and endpoint-specific missing-data and sensitivity methods. These decisions can materially affect estimates and non-inferiority conclusions.

**[Provided]** TSAP/Publication-SAP further analyses include recurrent CV events, renal and microvascular endpoints, rescue therapy, risk factors, and correlations of SBP and HbA1c with UACR. Exploratory analyses also describe PK trough concentrations and optional pharmacogenomics.
