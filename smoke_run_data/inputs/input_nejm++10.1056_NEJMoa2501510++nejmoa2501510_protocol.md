# Evaluation Input

## 1.1 Background and Rationale

C3G and IC-MPGN are complement-driven glomerular diseases that may occur in native kidneys or recur after transplant. Pegcetacoplan inhibits C3 and C3b to limit inappropriate complement activation and glomerular C3-deposit injury. Reduction in proteinuria is the expected early renal signal.

## 1.2 Objectives

### Primary Objectives

- **P1:** Assess twice-weekly SC pegcetacoplan versus placebo in primary C3G/IC-MPGN on the basis of proteinuria reduction.
  - **P1 Outcome:** Proteinuria reduction with twice-weekly SC pegcetacoplan versus placebo.

### Secondary/Exploratory Objectives

**Secondary**

- **S1:** Assess the effect of pegcetacoplan on eGFR.
  - **S1 Outcome:** eGFR.
- **S2:** Assess the effect of pegcetacoplan on additional C3G/IC-MPGN disease-related parameters.
  - **S2 Outcome:** Additional C3G/IC-MPGN disease-related parameters.
- **S3:** Evaluate safety of pegcetacoplan over 52 weeks of treatment.
  - **S3 Outcome:** Safety over 52 weeks of treatment.

**Exploratory**

- **S4:** Characterize additional clinical, laboratory, and histologic findings in response to pegcetacoplan.
  - **S4 Outcome:** Additional clinical, laboratory, and histologic findings in response to pegcetacoplan.

## 2.1 Trial Design

### Design Type

Phase 3, randomized, multicenter, placebo-controlled, double-blind study comprising:
- 10-week screening.
- 26-week randomized controlled period (RCP).
- 26-week open-label period (OLP).
- 8-week follow-up for participants not entering the long-term extension.

### Allocation Ratio

1:1 pegcetacoplan to placebo in the RCP.

### Intervention Summary

SC infusion twice weekly:
- Pegcetacoplan group: pegcetacoplan in RCP and OLP.
- Placebo group: matching placebo in RCP, then pegcetacoplan in OLP.
- Adults and adolescents ≥50 kg: 1080 mg/20 mL twice weekly.
- Adolescents 35 to <50 kg: 648 mg first, then 810 mg/15 mL twice weekly.
- Adolescents 30 to <35 kg: 540 mg for the first 2 doses, then 648 mg/12 mL twice weekly.

The RCP is blinded; all participants receive open-label pegcetacoplan in the OLP. Participants, sponsor, investigators, and site staff remain blinded to RCP allocation through completion of all week-26 assessments and RCP database lock. Central evaluations, including central pathology, are blinded. Only non-site drug-supply/logistics personnel are unblinded.

## 2.2 Randomization

**Method&nbsp;**

Centrally performed computer-generated randomization schedule using stratified permuted blocks.

Stratification factors:
- Posttransplant recurrence versus nontransplant.
- Then baseline renal biopsy available, from screening or historically within 28 weeks, versus unavailable.

Minimization: Not specified.

## 2.3 Sample Size

### Planned Sample Size

Approximately 80–100 randomized participants, 40–50 per arm; at least 78 native-kidney participants and at least 63 with native-kidney C3G.

## 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

The primary null hypothesis is no between-group difference in week-26 log-transformed uPCR ratio to baseline; the alternative is a difference.

Comparative key/additional hypotheses separately test no difference versus a difference in:
- Composite renal response.
- ≥50% uPCR response.
- eGFR change.
- Histologic activity-score change.
- C3c response.
- Proteinuria <1 g/day.
- Albumin normalization.
- C3 normalization.
- FACIT-Fatigue change.
- KDQOL change.

Primary, key secondary, and additional secondary endpoints are tested in a fixed sequence to control trial-wise type I error.

## 2.5 Timing of Analyses

The formal SAP is finalized before database lock. RCP analysis follows:
- Completion or discontinuation of all participants.
- Entry, review, cleaning, and finalization of corresponding data.
- Lock of the week-26 analysis database.
- Unblinding.

Primary and secondary endpoints are assessed at week 26 and evaluated at week 52 as exploratory. Primary uPCR uses an equal-weighted average over weeks 24, 25, and 26. Twenty-four-hour urine collections occur at weeks 24 and 48.

## 3.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

### Inclusion Criteria

- Primary C3G/IC-MPGN.
- Active renal disease.
- Proteinuria ≥1 g/day and FMU uPCR ≥1000 mg/g.
- eGFR ≥30 mL/min/1.73 m².
- Stable renal regimen.
- Required vaccination status.
- Reproductive safeguards.
- Capacity to consent/assent and administer drug, or caregiver support.
- Adults; where approved, adolescents aged 12–17 years weighing ≥30 kg.

### Exclusion Criteria

- Prior pegcetacoplan.
- Improving disease.
- Relevant secondary disease, infection, or malignancy.
- Prohibited complement therapies.
- Pregnancy or breastfeeding.
- Severe infection.
- Hereditary fructose intolerance.
