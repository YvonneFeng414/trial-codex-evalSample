# Evaluation Input

## 1.1 Background and Rationale

Noninfectious intermediate-, posterior-, and pan-uveitis may threaten vision, while sustained corticosteroid exposure has ocular and systemic risks. This final analysis specification evaluates steroid-sparing maintenance adalimumab against placebo in active disease.

## 1.2 Objectives

### Primary Objectives

- **P1:** Evaluate efficacy and safety of adalimumab (80 mg SC at Baseline, then 40 mg SC every other week from Week 1) versus placebo as maintenance therapy in subjects requiring high-dose corticosteroids for active noninfectious intermediate-, posterior-, or pan-uveitis.
  - **P1 Outcome:** Efficacy and safety of adalimumab versus placebo as maintenance therapy.

### Secondary/Exploratory Objectives

- The protocol does not separately label secondary objectives; it defines ranked secondary efficacy endpoints and their confirmatory testing sequence.
- The protocol does not separately label exploratory objectives; other efficacy, sensitivity, and subgroup analyses are specified as exploratory analyses.

## 2.1 Trial Design

### Design Type

Phase 3, randomized, double-masked, placebo-controlled, multicenter parent study. Analyses cover the non-Japan parent study, with a separate Japan sub-study.

### Allocation Ratio

1:1.

### Intervention Summary

All continuing subjects received a standardized 60-mg/day prednisone burst at entry with mandatory taper/discontinuation by Week 15; topical corticosteroids were tapered per protocol.

- **Arm 1:** Adalimumab 80 mg SC loading dose at Baseline, then 40 mg SC every other week beginning Week 1.
- **Arm 2:** Matching placebo loading and every-other-week doses.

## 2.2 Randomization

**Method&nbsp;**

Eligible subjects were randomized by IVRS/IWRS; the AbbVie Deutschland Statistics Department prepared the schedule.

- **Stratification:** Baseline immunosuppressant (IMM) usage; Japanese subjects were randomized in a separate stratum without IMM stratification.
- **Blocking:** Randomization was not stratified by site; an adequate block size was used.

## 2.3 Sample Size

### Planned Sample Size

Approximately 234 patients to obtain 138 treatment-failure events.

- **Outcome used for calculation:** Time to treatment failure at 6 months.
- **Outcome type:** Time-to-event treatment failure.
- **Assumed control-arm value:** 70% placebo treatment-failure rate at 6 months.
- **Assumed treatment-arm value:** 50% adalimumab treatment-failure rate at 6 months.
- **Target difference / effect size:** Absolute 20-percentage-point difference in assumed 6-month failure rates.
- **Variability assumption:** Failures were conservatively assumed to begin after 2 months as prednisone reached lower doses.
- **Alpha:** Two-sided 5%.
- **Power:** 90%.
- **Statistical test assumed:** Log-rank test.
- **Required number of events:** 138 treatment-failure events.
- **Non-inferiority / equivalence margin:** Not applicable: superiority comparison.
- **Dropout / non-adherence inflation:** Pooled 35% dropout over 12 months.
- **Design effect / clustering:** Not applicable to the individually randomized sample-size calculation.
- **Basis for assumptions:** Event, recruitment, and dropout assumptions; accrual averaged 4 subjects/month for first 30 months then 7/month.
- **Sample size re-estimation / amendments:** Not specified.
- **Software used for calculation:** East5 Version 5.2.0.0.
- **Rationale:** Calculations across sample sizes using stated assumptions indicated approximately 234 patients to achieve 138 events.

## 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

Primary time-to-treatment-failure and ranked secondary endpoints are tested two-sided at 0.05. Ranked-secondary testing begins only if the primary endpoint is significant and proceeds in the specified hierarchy; each preceding endpoint must be significant to test the next, controlling the two-sided 5% multiple significance level. Other, sensitivity, and subgroup analyses are exploratory.

## 2.5 Timing of Analyses

- **Final analysis timing:** Final efficacy value is the treatment-failure visit, Early Termination Visit, or Week 80 Visit if no failure; analyses occur after parent-study termination.
- **Outcome assessment schedule:** Baseline, Week 1, Week 4, then every 4 weeks with Week 27 replacing Week 28; treatment failure is assessed at Week 6 and later visits. Analysis windows run through Week 80 by Rx day; closest assessment to nominal day is used, ties use latest.
- **Data cutoff, database-lock requirements, and data readiness:** Not specified.

## 3.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

### Inclusion Criteria

- Age ≥18 years.
- Noninfectious intermediate-, posterior-, or pan-uveitis.
- Active disease in either eye despite ≥2 weeks of stable prednisone ≥10 mg/day (or equivalent), defined by active chorioretinal/retinal vascular lesion, OCT macular edema with intraocular inflammation, AC cells ≥2+, or vitreous haze ≥2+.
- Appropriate contraception/negative pregnancy tests.
- Screening health evaluation: history, laboratory profile, physical examination, chest radiograph and ECG.
- Injection capability, consent/compliance, and TB testing/therapy conditions.

### Exclusion Criteria

- Isolated anterior or infectious uveitis; inadequate response/intolerance to high-dose steroids.
- POHS, ocular masquerade/serpiginous disease, pupil-dilation limitation, fundus-obscuring opacity/cataract need, specified glaucoma/BCVA/demyelination/retinopathy/macular disease/vitreoretinal-interface or recent/planned eye-surgery conditions.
- Prior anti-TNF/biologic, cyclophosphamide/chlorambucil or steroid implant exposure.
- Unstable, multiple, prohibited, or excessive immunosuppression.
- Local steroids within 90 days; recent investigational drugs.
- Prohibited infections/TB/hepatitis B/HIV/immunodeficiency or anti-infective treatment; positive syphilis/Lyme tests.
- Selected cardiovascular/neurologic history, hypersensitivity, pregnancy/breastfeeding, malignancy, drug/alcohol abuse, significant screening laboratory abnormalities, and investigator unsuitability.
