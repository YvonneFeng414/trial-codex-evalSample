# Evaluation Input

## 1.1 Background and Rationale

Patients with recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer progressing after platinum-based chemotherapy have poor outcomes and no widely accepted subsequent systemic standard. This pivotal phase 3 study compares cemiplimab with investigator’s-choice (IC) chemotherapy.

## 1.2 Objectives

### Primary Objectives

- **P1 Objective:** Compare overall survival (OS), first among patients with squamous cell carcinoma (SCC), then in the overall eligible population (SCC or adenocarcinoma/adenosquamous carcinoma), for cemiplimab versus IC chemotherapy.
- **P1 Outcome:** OS for cemiplimab versus IC chemotherapy, first in SCC patients, then in the overall eligible population.

### Secondary/Exploratory Objectives

- **S1 Objective:** Compare progression-free survival (PFS), objective response rate (ORR) by RECIST 1.1, duration of response (DoR), adverse-event profiles, and EORTC QLQ-C30 global-health-status/quality-of-life (GHS/QoL) and physical-functioning mean change from baseline, in SCC and overall populations.
- **S1 Outcome:** PFS, ORR by RECIST 1.1, DoR, adverse-event profiles, and EORTC QLQ-C30 GHS/QoL and physical-functioning mean change from baseline, in SCC and overall populations.
- **S2 Objective (Exploratory):** Characterize serum cemiplimab PK and immunogenicity; explore associations of efficacy with pretreatment-tumor molecular features and peripheral-blood pharmacodynamic activity.
- **S2 Outcome:** Serum cemiplimab PK and immunogenicity; associations of efficacy with pretreatment-tumor molecular features and peripheral-blood pharmacodynamic activity.

## 2.1 Trial Design

### Design Type

Open-label, randomized, multicentre phase 3 trial with screening, treatment, follow-up and subsequent survival follow-up; no treatment crossover.

### Allocation Ratio

1:1 cemiplimab versus IC chemotherapy.

### Intervention Summary

Treatment is planned for up to 96 weeks.

- **Cemiplimab:** 350 mg IV every 3 weeks.
- **IC chemotherapy:** Pemetrexed, topotecan, irinotecan, gemcitabine, or vinorelbine.
  - **Pemetrexed:** 500 mg/m² IV day 1 every 21 days, with folate/vitamin B12 support.
  - **Topotecan:** 1 mg/m² IV daily for 5 days every 21 days; may increase to 1.25 mg/m² after first course without significant toxicity.
  - **Irinotecan:** 100 mg/m² IV weekly for 4 doses, then 10–14 days’ rest (≥14 days in Japan); may increase to 125 mg/m² after first course without significant toxicity.
  - **Gemcitabine:** 1000 mg/m² IV days 1 and 8 every 21 days.
  - **Vinorelbine:** 30 mg/m² IV days 1 and 8 every 21 days.

Cemiplimab and IC treatment continue to 96 weeks or RECIST 1.1 progression, unacceptable toxicity, death, or consent withdrawal. Cemiplimab may continue beyond RECIST progression only under protocol criteria. In follow-up, a patient with PD may be retreated with the same originally assigned drug; IC selection and each drug’s dose/schedule are protocol specified.

## 2.2 Randomization

**Method&nbsp;**

- IWRS treatment assignment after investigator deems eligibility.
- **Stratification factors:** SCC analysis: geographic region (North America, Asia-Pacific, rest of world [ROW]). Overall analysis: region and histology (SCC versus adenocarcinoma; adenosquamous classified as adenocarcinoma).
- **Minimization or blocking:** Not specified.
- After Amendment 5, only SCC patients were randomized. Before Amendment 5, patients were stratified by SCC versus adenocarcinoma/adenosquamous histology; this historical distinction is retained in the overall-population analysis.

## 2.3 Sample Size

### Planned Sample Size

- **Planned enrollment:** 460 randomized SCC patients; approximately 590 total patients projected.
- **Outcome used for calculation:** OS in SCC patients, a time-to-event survival outcome.
- **Assumed control-arm value:** Median OS 7 months for IC chemotherapy.
- **Assumed treatment-arm value:** Median OS 10 months for cemiplimab.
- **Target effect:** HR 0.70, corresponding to approximately 42.8% increase in median OS under exponential distributions.
- **Distribution assumption:** Exponential OS distributions in both groups.
- **Alpha:** Overall one-sided 0.025.
- **Power:** Approximately 90% for HR 0.70 with 340 SCC OS events.
- **Statistical test assumed:** Group-sequential, stratified log-rank OS test with Lan-DeMets (O’Brien-Fleming) alpha spending.
- **Required events:** 340 OS events in SCC patients.
- **Non-inferiority/equivalence margin:** Not applicable; superiority hypothesis.
- **Dropout assumption:** 10% per year.
- **Design effect/clustering:** Not applicable; individually randomized trial.
- **Basis for assumptions:** Phase 2 IC median OS reports of 6.5–8.1 months; enrollment assumptions of 2/month during months 1–5, 9/month during months 6–16, 20/month during months 17–23, then 22/month.
- **Amendments:** SAP v2.0 updated sample size, interim analysis, target population, and enrollment rate for protocol amendments through Amendment 6. A formal sample-size re-estimation procedure is not specified.
- **Calculation software:** East version 6.4.1.
- **Rationale:** 460 SCC patients are expected to yield 340 events about 42 months after first SCC randomization; overall-population power is projected above 90% if HR = 0.70.

## 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

H0: Cemiplimab and IC chemotherapy have the same OS survival curve.

H1: Cemiplimab is superior.

OS is tested first in SCC and, only if significant, in the overall population.

## 2.5 Timing of Analyses

- **Final analysis:** Final OS cutoff is when 340 SCC OS events are observed. Events through cutoff are included; otherwise censor at the earlier of last-known-alive date or cutoff.
- **Treatment timing:** Treatment starts within 5 days after randomization (within 10 days for pemetrexed) in 6-week cycles.
- **Imaging:** CT/MRI on day 42 (±7 days) of cycles 1–4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, and 16. After treatment, imaging is every 90 days (±10) until PD for those without prior PD.
- **QLQ-C30:** At screening and day 1 of treatment cycles (may be at another visit in the same cycle), before procedures; also at post-treatment follow-up visits 1 and 2.
- **Follow-up:** Visit 1 is approximately 30 days (±10) after last dose; visit 2 is approximately 90 days (±10) later. Survival ascertainment then occurs every 90 days (±10) until death or completion.
- **Database-lock and data-readiness requirements:** Not specified.

## 3.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

### Inclusion Criteria

- Women ≥18 years with recurrent/persistent/metastatic noncurable cervical SCC; adenocarcinoma/adenosquamous allowed before Amendment 5.
- Progression after platinum specifically for recurrent/persistent/metastatic cancer, not curative chemoradiation.
- RECIST 1.1 measurable lesion ≥10 mm by CT/MRI/caliper or ≥20 mm by chest radiograph; nodes >15 mm short axis.
- ECOG ≤1.
- Bilirubin ≤1.5× ULN, or ≤3× with liver metastases.
- Transaminases ≤3× ULN, or ≤5× with liver metastases.
- Alkaline phosphatase ≤2.5× ULN, or ≤5× with liver/bone metastases.
- Creatinine ≤1.5× ULN or clearance >45 mL/min.
- Hemoglobin ≥9 g/dL, ANC ≥1.5×10⁹/L, and platelets ≥75×10⁹/L.
- Life expectancy >12 weeks.
- Ability to comply and complete questionnaires.
- Consent.
- Required prior bevacizumab/paclitaxel status or exceptions.

### Exclusion Criteria

- Significant systemic-treatment autoimmune disease.
- Prior PD-1/PD-L1 therapy.
- Immune-modulator within 4 weeks or recent associated toxicity.
- Active/unstable CNS metastasis.
- >10-mg prednisone-equivalent immunosuppression within 4 weeks.
- Active serious infection/HIV/HBV/HCV.
- Pneumonitis within 5 years.
- Anticancer therapy within 30 days; radiation within 14 days.
- Relevant allergy.
- Concurrent/recent malignancy.
- Transplant.
- Pregnancy/breastfeeding.
- Failure to use required contraception.
