# Evaluation Input

## 1.1 Background and Rationale

TIMELESS evaluates whether IV tenecteplase improves clinical outcomes versus placebo in imaging-selected acute ischemic stroke presenting 4.5–24 hours after last-known-well time. The rationale is to achieve earlier reperfusion in patients with salvageable tissue and ICA/MCA occlusion, including patients needing transfer for endovascular therapy.

## 1.2 Objectives

### Primary Objectives

- **P1:** Compare efficacy of tenecteplase with placebo using ordinal Day-90 modified Rankin Scale (mRS).
  - **P1 Outcome:** Ordinal Day-90 mRS, tenecteplase versus placebo.

### Secondary/Exploratory Objectives

- **S1 — Secondary:** Evaluate efficacy using Day-90 functional independence, angiographic reperfusion, NIHSS, Barthel Index, Glasgow Outcome Scale, 24-hour reperfusion and 24-hour recanalization; evaluate safety using sICH, adverse events, mortality, and PH2.
  - **S1 Outcome:** Day-90 functional independence, angiographic reperfusion, NIHSS, Barthel Index, Glasgow Outcome Scale, 24-hour reperfusion, 24-hour recanalization, sICH, adverse events, mortality, and PH2.
- **S2 — Exploratory:** Evaluate final infarct volume/growth, mRS subgroups, thrombectomy-not-performed outcomes, 30-day readmission, one-pass EVT, Neuro-QoL, and Day-90 survival.
  - **S2 Outcome:** Final infarct volume/growth, mRS subgroups, thrombectomy-not-performed outcomes, 30-day readmission, one-pass EVT, Neuro-QoL, and Day-90 survival.

## 2.1 Trial Design

### Design Type

Phase III, prospective, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial in imaging-eligible 4.5–24-hour AIS with ICA or MCA (M1/M2) occlusion.

### Allocation Ratio

1:1.

### Intervention Summary

Both groups receive guideline-based standard care; ICA/M1 patients may receive adjuvant EVT if guideline criteria are met.

- **Tenecteplase:** 0.25 mg/kg IV, maximum 25 mg, one bolus over 5 seconds.
- **Placebo:** One bolus injection over 5 seconds.

## 2.2 Randomization

**Method&nbsp;**

IWRS assigns treatment kit and randomizes eligible consented patients; treat as soon as possible.

- **Stratification factors:** Age ≤70/>70 years; occlusion ICA/M1 versus M2; baseline NIHSS ≤15/>15; ECC versus nECC.
- **Blocking:** Permuted blocks provide approximately 1:1 balance within each stratum.

## 2.3 Sample Size

### Planned Sample Size

- **Planned enrollment:** 456 patients, approximately 228 per group; 432 evaluable after 5% dropout.
- **Outcome used for calculation:** Day-90 ordinal mRS, with seven levels (0–6).
- **Assumed control-arm distribution, mRS 0–6:** 13%, 13%, 12%, 14%, 18%, 12%, 18%.
- **Assumed treatment-arm distribution, mRS 0–6:** 17%, 20%, 16%, 15%, 13%, 7%, 12%.
- **Target effect size:** Common odds ratio at least 1.76, tenecteplase versus placebo.
- **Variability assumption:** Assumed ordinal mRS distributions; alternative historical scenarios gave >99% and >88% power.
- **Significance level:** Two-sided 0.049 after adjustment for one efficacy interim analysis.
- **Power:** At least 90%.
- **Statistical test assumed for calculation:** Wilcoxon–Mann–Whitney test.
- **Required number of events:** Not applicable; not an event-driven design.
- **Non-inferiority/equivalence margin:** Not applicable; superiority design.
- **Dropout inflation:** 5%.
- **Design effect/clustering:** Not applicable; individually randomized trial with no cluster design.
- **Basis for assumptions:** Assumed mRS distributions and alternative scenarios derived from prior stroke studies.
- **Sample-size re-estimation/amendments:** Not specified.
- **Software:** EAST 6.4.1.
- **Rationale:** 456 provides 90% power to detect the specified mRS distribution difference after 5% dropout.

## 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

All efficacy endpoints are assessed for superiority. For the primary endpoint, H0 is common odds ratio = 1 versus two-sided H1 not equal to 1. The odds ratio represents odds of lower (better) mRS for tenecteplase versus placebo.

## 2.5 Timing of Analyses

- **Final analysis:** After all patients complete/discontinue, all data are in the database, and database lock.
- **Assessment timing:**
  - 24-hour imaging/NIHSS: 18–30 hours.
  - 72–96-hour NIHSS: 60–96 hours; imaging: 60–120 hours.
  - Day 5 mRS/GOS/BI: 4–6 days.
  - Day 30 clinical outcomes: 15–60 days.
  - Day 90 clinical outcomes: 61–120 days.

## 3.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

### Inclusion Criteria

- Pre-stroke mRS 0–2.
- NIHSS ≥5 both at baseline and immediately pre-randomization.
- Qualifying CTA/MRA plus CTP/MRP target mismatch: core <70 mL, ratio ≥1.8, mismatch ≥15 mL.
- Capacity to comply.

### Exclusion Criteria

- Bleeding/coagulopathy/anticoagulants.
- Prior thrombolysis.
- Relevant intracranial pathology.
- Uncontrolled hypertension.
- Glucose extremes.
- Prior recent stroke/surgery.
- Pregnancy.
- Prohibitive imaging findings.
