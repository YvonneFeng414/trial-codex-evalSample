# Evaluation Input

## 1.1 Background and Rationale

Oral factor Xa (FXa) inhibitors are widely used, but acute intracranial hemorrhage (ICrH) while anticoagulated has high mortality and disability. Preventing hematoma expansion is a key treatment goal. Andexanet alfa is a recombinant, catalytically inactive FXa analogue intended to reverse FXa inhibition. ANNEXA-I compares andexanet with usual care in this setting.

## 1.2 Objectives

### Primary Objectives

- **P1:** Compare andexanet with usual care for effective hemostasis at 12 hours post-randomization.
  - **Objective-level outcome P1:** Effective hemostasis at 12 hours post-randomization, comparing andexanet with usual care.

### Secondary/Exploratory Objectives

- **S1 — Secondary:** Compare percent change in anti-fXa activity from baseline to nadir during the first 2 hours post-randomization.
  - **Objective-level outcome S1:** Percent change in anti-fXa activity from baseline to nadir during the first 2 hours post-randomization.
- **S2 — Exploratory efficacy:** Additional efficacy objectives are thrombin generation, neurologic deterioration, mRS, NIHSS/GCS, imaging-only hemostasis, rescue therapy, anti-fXa/hemostasis correlation, EQ-5D-5L, and utility-weighted mRS.
  - **Objective-level outcome S2:** Thrombin generation, neurologic deterioration, mRS, NIHSS/GCS, imaging-only hemostasis, rescue therapy, anti-fXa/hemostasis correlation, EQ-5D-5L, and utility-weighted mRS.
- **S3 — Exploratory safety:** Safety objectives cover thrombotic events (TEs), mortality, invasive procedures, hospital use, adverse events (AEs), vital signs, and immunogenicity.
  - **Objective-level outcome S3:** TEs, mortality, invasive procedures, hospital use, AEs, vital signs, and immunogenicity.

## 2.1 Trial Design

### Design Type

Phase 4, prospective, multicenter, randomized, open-label clinical trial; the EAC and imaging core laboratory are blinded.

### Allocation Ratio

1:1 andexanet:usual care.

### Intervention Summary

Usual care is investigator-selected treatment, including no treatment, other than andexanet initiated within 3 hours.

Andexanet has no crossover and comprises:

- **Low dose:** 400-mg IV bolus over about 15 minutes, then 480 mg at 4 mg/min for 120 minutes.
- **High dose:** 800-mg IV bolus over up to about 30 minutes, then 960 mg at 8 mg/min for 120 minutes.

Regimen selection uses the preceding FXa inhibitor, its last dose and its timing. Patients qualifying through local anti-fXa >100 ng/mL with last dose >15 hours/unknown receive high dose. Andexanet should start no later than 30 minutes after randomization and preferably within 2 hours of baseline brain imaging.

## 2.2 Randomization

**Method&nbsp;**

Sponsor-generated randomization scheme after consent, eligibility/baseline assessments, and documentation of intended usual care.

- **Stratification factors:** Intended usual-care agent (plasma-derived coagulation-factor concentrate versus other) and symptom-onset-to-baseline-imaging time (<180 versus ≥180 minutes).
- **Minimization or blocking:** Not specified.

## 2.3 Sample Size

### Planned Sample Size

Approximately 900 participants, 450 per group; preplanned interim sample size re-estimation (SSR) could increase total enrollment to 1200.

- **Outcome used for calculation:** Rate of effective hemostasis.
- **Outcome type:** Binary proportion.
- **Assumed control-arm value:** 70% effective hemostasis.
- **Assumed treatment-arm value:** 80% effective hemostasis.
- **Target difference / effect size:** 10 percentage points, representing a 33% reduction in risk of not achieving effective hemostasis.
- **Variability assumption:** Not applicable; the calculation is for a binary proportion and reports arm rates.
- **Alpha:** Overall two-sided 0.05, accounting for the interim analysis.
- **Power:** Approximately 90%.
- **Statistical test assumed:** CMH comparison of effective-hemostasis proportions, stratified by symptom-onset-to-baseline-imaging time.
- **Required number of events:** Not applicable; the endpoint is a binary proportion rather than time-to-event.
- **Non-inferiority / equivalence margin:** Not applicable; the stated hypothesis is a superiority comparison.
- **Dropout / non-adherence inflation:** The calculation accounts for early discontinuation; no separate numerical inflation is specified.
- **Design effect / clustering:** Not applicable; this is individually randomized, not cluster-randomized.
- **Basis for assumptions:** The andexanet rate derives from ANNEXA-4 efficacy results; 10% was considered clinically meaningful.
- **Sample size re-estimation / amendments:** At interim, conditional power of 30%–90% permits a DSMB-recommended increase up to 1200; no increase if conditional power is <30% or >90%.
- **Software used for calculation:** Not specified.
- **Sample size rationale:** The design targets a clinically meaningful difference while accounting for early discontinuation and interim type-I error.

## 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

The primary hypothesis is H0: πUC−πandexanet=0 versus HA: πUC−πandexanet≠0.

The primary objective succeeds when the andexanet effective-hemostasis proportion is higher under the prespecified alpha-spending thresholds. The secondary endpoint is tested only after a significant primary endpoint.

## 2.5 Timing of Analyses

Final analyses occur after study completion and final database lock. If stopped after interim, DCO1 supports confirmatory efficacy and DCO2 captures all participants for safety and efficacy sensitivity analyses.

Assessment timing:

- **Anti-fXa:** Baseline, 1 and 2 hours.
- **Thrombin generation:** Baseline, 1 and 12 hours.
- **TFPI:** Baseline, 1, 2 and 12 hours and Day30.
- **CT/MRI:** Baseline and about 12 hours.
- **NIHSS:** Baseline and 2, 3, 6, 12, 24 and 72 hours, with 12/24-hour assessments blinded.
- **GCS:** Baseline and 2, 3, 6, 12, 24 and 72 hours.
- **Premorbid mRS:** Baseline.
- **mRS/EQ-5D-5L:** Day30 or early termination.
- **Follow-up:** Day 7 and 14 calls and Day30 (+7) follow-up; an additional approximately Day120 visit for positive anti-andexanet antibodies.

## 3.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

### Inclusion Criteria

- Adults with acute intracerebral bleeding of specified volume.
- CT/MRI within 2 hours before randomization.
- Oral apixaban, rivaroxaban or edoxaban at protocol-specified doses within 15 hours.
- Symptom onset-to-baseline scan <6 hours.
- Randomization outside 15 hours or after unknown last-dose time requires standard-of-care local anti-fXa >100 ng/mL within 2 hours before consent and mandates high-dose andexanet.
- Consent may be participant, LAR/proxy, or emergency consent where lawful.

### Exclusion Criteria

- Planned hematoma-affecting surgery within 12 hours.
- GCS<7, with specified non-neurologic sedation exception.
- NIHSS>35.
- Hematoma volume ≤0.5 or ≥60 mL.
- Inability to use the same CT/MRI modality.
- Expected survival <1 month.
- Recent thrombotic/cardiovascular events.
- Cardiogenic shock.
- Severe sepsis/septic shock.
- Pregnancy/lactation, with required pregnancy testing/contraception precautions.
- Restricted anticoagulants/blood products.
- Prior/planned andexanet.
- Investigational drug.
- Tumor-related bleeding.
- Hypersensitivity.
