# Evaluation Input

## 1.1 Background and Rationale

IPM 027 is a pivotal Phase III evaluation of whether a 25-mg dapivirine silicone-elastomer vaginal matrix ring (Ring-004), replaced every 4 weeks, safely prevents HIV infection in healthy HIV-negative women compared with an indistinguishable placebo ring. The program addresses the need for woman-controlled HIV-prevention options and evaluates a monthly product intended for sustained use independent of sexual activity.

## 1.2 Objectives

### Primary Objectives

- **P1:** To determine the efficacy of dapivirine ring use every 4 weeks for approximately 24 months (104 weeks) in preventing HIV-1 infection among healthy, sexually active HIV-negative women.
  - **Outcome P1:** HIV-1 infection prevention with dapivirine ring use every 4 weeks for approximately 24 months (104 weeks).
- **P2:** To assess the safety of dapivirine ring use on that schedule.
  - **Outcome P2:** Safety of dapivirine ring use every 4 weeks for approximately 24 months (104 weeks).

### Secondary/Exploratory Objectives

**Secondary**

- **S1:** Compare HIV-2 incidence; curable STI incidence and vaginal-flora changes; and determine pregnancy incidence.
  - **Outcome S1:** HIV-2 incidence; curable STI incidence; vaginal-flora changes; pregnancy incidence.
- **S2:** Assess reported 4-week ring-use adherence and acceptability.
  - **Outcome S2:** Reported 4-week ring-use adherence and acceptability.
- **S3:** Assess frequency and type of HIV-1 resistance in women acquiring HIV-1 while using investigational product (IP).
  - **Outcome S3:** Frequency and type of HIV-1 resistance in women acquiring HIV-1 while using IP.

**Exploratory**

- **S4:** Evaluate HSV-2/HIV-1 association; contraception method, pregnancy and HIV-seroconversion relationships; drug concentrations versus HIV-1 seroconversion/viral resistance; and drug concentrations versus self-reported adherence.
  - **Outcome S4:** HSV-2/HIV-1 association; relationships between contraception method, pregnancy and HIV seroconversion; drug concentrations versus HIV-1 seroconversion/viral resistance; drug concentrations versus self-reported adherence.

## 2.1 Trial Design

### Design Type

Multi-centre, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled pivotal Phase III safety-and-efficacy trial.

### Allocation Ratio

2:1 dapivirine:ring placebo.

### Intervention Summary

Both arms use a ring continuously, with self-insertion/replacement at 4-week intervals for approximately 104 weeks and exit follow-up 6 weeks after ring discontinuation.

- **Dapivirine arm:** Ring-004, a silicone-elastomer vaginal matrix ring containing 25 mg dapivirine.
- **Placebo arm:** Placebo vaginal ring.

## 2.2 Randomization

**Method&nbsp;**

An automated IXRS (Interactive Voice/Web Response System) assigns a unique participant identification number and treatment; a master randomization list links participant identification number to assignment.

- **Stratification:** Research centre.
- **Blocking:** A pre-specified block size is used.
- **Minimization:** Not specified.

## 2.3 Sample Size

### Planned Sample Size

- **Planned enrollment:** Approximately 1,950 women: 1,300 dapivirine and 650 placebo. A total of 1,959 were enrolled and accrual closed.
- **Amendment:** Amendment 3 increased planned enrollment from 1,650 to approximately 1,950 to mitigate product nonadherence and preserve power, following high nonadherence at one research centre. This was an amendment, not a prospectively described re-estimation algorithm.
- **Calculation outcome:** HIV-1 seroconversion incidence rate in the two treatment arms.
- **Outcome type:** Time-to-event incidence rate under proportional hazards.
- **Assumed control-arm value:** Average annual placebo-arm HIV incidence >4%, allowing for reduction from risk-reduction counselling.
- **Assumed treatment-arm value:** Microbicide efficacy ≥50% in preventing HIV-1 infection.
- **Target effect:** 50% reduction in HIV-1 seroconversion rate; scenarios also evaluate 40% and 60% efficacy.
- **Variability assumption:** Proportional hazards between groups.
- **Significance level:** Two-sided 0.05 for the calculation.
- **Statistical test:** Two-sided log-rank test.

| Efficacy scenario | Power | Expected HIV-1 seroconversions |
|---|---|---|
| 50% | 81% | 96 |
| 40% | 61% | 104 |
| 60% | 94% | 88 |

- **Required-event target:** Not specified; the reported seroconversions are expected events.
- **Early discontinuation/loss to follow-up:** ≤10% assumed.
- **Non-inferiority/equivalence margin:** Not applicable; superiority efficacy trial.
- **Design effect/clustering:** Not applicable; individual randomization with centre stratification rather than cluster randomization.
- **Software:** Not specified.
- **Additional rationale:** With 1,950 participants, the trial also has 95% probability to detect an AE occurring at ≥0.3% among those assigned IP.

## 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

The primary efficacy null hypothesis is no difference in HIV-1 seroconversion rate/population survival curves between dapivirine and placebo; the design tests superiority. Primary safety analysis is descriptive.

## 2.5 Timing of Analyses

- **Final analysis:** Final analyses occur only after database lock. Blinded review, including deviation classification, occurs before lock; treatment codes are released after lock.
- **Screening:** Up to 28 days.
- **IP use and exit:** Approximately 104 weeks of IP use, with exit 6 weeks after last product use.
- **On-IP visit windows:** ±7 days.
- **Every 4 weeks:** HIV and pregnancy testing, AE/concomitant review, diary/adherence data and ring exchange.
- **Every 12 weeks:** Pelvic/STI/safety laboratory assessments.
- **Acceptability:** Every 24 weeks from week 24, also at week 4.
- **Cervical cytology:** Screening, week 52 and last product-use visit.

## 3.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

### Inclusion Criteria

- Women aged 18–45 years.
- Able to consent and attend procedures.
- Sexually active: ≥1 penetrative penile-vaginal act/month in the prior 3 months.
- HIV-negative at screening/enrolment.
- Using stable contraception unless postmenopausal or surgically sterilized.
- Asymptomatic for genital infection at enrolment.
- Locatable.
- Not participating in conflicting investigational studies.

### Exclusion Criteria

- Current/recent intended pregnancy or breastfeeding.
- Specified injection-drug/research/vaccine histories.
- Untreated significant urogenital infection or grade ≥2 pelvic finding.
- Specified genital/medical/allergy conditions.
- Clinically important baseline laboratory abnormalities.
- Disqualifying cytology.
- Investigator-judged risk or inability to adhere.
