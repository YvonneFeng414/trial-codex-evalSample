# Evaluation Input

## 1.1 Background and Rationale

Pediatric type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM) has limited glucose-lowering options. This Phase 3 study evaluates whether once-weekly dulaglutide added to diet and exercise, with or without metformin and/or basal insulin, improves glycemic control versus placebo. HbA1c was selected as an accepted endpoint for pediatric T2DM glycemic trials.

## 1.2 Objectives

### Primary Objectives

- **P1:** Test superiority of pooled dulaglutide 0.75 mg and 1.5 mg SC once weekly versus placebo for change in central-laboratory HbA1c from baseline to Week 26 in children and adolescents with inadequately controlled T2DM.
  - **P1 Outcome:** Change in central-laboratory HbA1c from baseline to Week 26, pooled dulaglutide versus placebo.

### Secondary/Exploratory Objectives

- **S1 — Secondary efficacy:** Compare individual 0.75-mg and 1.5-mg doses and, where specified, pooled dulaglutide with placebo for individual-dose HbA1c change, FBG change, HbA1c <7.0%, and BMI change at Week 26; assess HbA1c/FBG/BMI changes and HbA1c <7.0% at Week 52.
  - **S1 Outcome:** Individual-dose HbA1c change, FBG change, HbA1c <7.0%, and BMI change at Week 26 versus placebo, including pooled dulaglutide where specified; HbA1c/FBG/BMI changes and HbA1c <7.0% at Week 52.
- **S2 — Secondary safety and PK/PD:** Assess safety at Week 26 versus placebo and Week 52 within dulaglutide groups, covering self-reported hypoglycemia/rescue; adjudicated pancreatitis/pancreatic enzymes; thyroid TEAEs/calcitonin; and ADA, allergic/hypersensitivity, and injection-site reactions. Characterize PK and exposure relationships with safety/efficacy.
  - **S2 Outcome:** Self-reported hypoglycemia/rescue; adjudicated pancreatitis/pancreatic enzymes; thyroid TEAEs/calcitonin; ADA, allergic/hypersensitivity, and injection-site reactions at Week 26 versus placebo and Week 52 within dulaglutide groups; PK and exposure relationships with safety/efficacy.
- **S3 — Exploratory:** At Weeks 26 and 52 unless stated otherwise, assess HbA1c ≤6.5%; HbA1c <7.0% and HbA1c ≤6.5% without severe, documented symptomatic (<70 mg/dL), or probable hypoglycemia; weight, BMI SDS, height/height SDS, waist, morning prolactin/IGF-1/estradiol/testosterone in males/LH/cortisol, lipids, Tanner stage, EQ-5D-Y VAS and domain levels, and basal-insulin-dose change. Assess HbA1c change at Week 13 and insulin resistance, beta-cell function, and adiponectin at Weeks 13 and 26.
  - **S3 Outcome:** HbA1c ≤6.5%; HbA1c <7.0% and HbA1c ≤6.5% without severe, documented symptomatic (<70 mg/dL), or probable hypoglycemia; weight, BMI SDS, height/height SDS, waist, morning prolactin/IGF-1/estradiol/testosterone in males/LH/cortisol, lipids, Tanner stage, EQ-5D-Y VAS and domain levels, and basal-insulin-dose change at Weeks 26 and 52; HbA1c change at Week 13; insulin resistance, beta-cell function, and adiponectin at Weeks 13 and 26.

## 2.1 Trial Design

### Design Type

Phase 3, multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-arm superiority trial with a 26-week open-label extension and 30-day safety follow-up.

### Allocation Ratio

1:1:1 at baseline (Visit 2).

### Intervention Summary

SC study treatment is administered weekly:

- Dulaglutide 0.75 mg/week during double blind and open label.
- Dulaglutide 1.5 mg/week during double blind and open label.
- Placebo/week during double blind, switching to dulaglutide 0.75 mg/week at Week 26.

Week-26 reporting labels are All Dula (pooled 1.5/0.75 mg), Dula 1.5, Dula 0.75, and Placebo; placebo is reference.

Post-Week-26 sequence labels are All Dula (0.75/0.75 and 1.5/1.5 pooled), Dula 1.5/1.5, Dula 0.75/0.75, and Placebo/Dula 0.75. No between-group inference is made after Week 26. Volumes/adjuvant content: Not specified.

## 2.2 Randomization

**Method&nbsp;**

Computer-generated random sequence implemented through IWRS after eligibility confirmation at Visit 2.

- **Stratification factors:** Screening HbA1c <8.0% or ≥8.0%, metformin use yes/no, and insulin use yes/no.
- **Minimization or blocking:** Not specified.

## 2.3 Sample Size

### Planned Sample Size

- **Initial plan:** Approximately 150 enrolled (50/arm) to obtain 120 Week-26 completers; approximately 750 screened, based on an estimated 80% screen-failure rate.
- **Calculation outcome:** Mean change in HbA1c from baseline to Week 26, pooled dulaglutide versus placebo; continuous percentage-point HbA1c change.
- **Absolute control- and treatment-arm values:** Not specified; the calculation reports a between-arm difference.
- **Initial target difference:** −0.65% mean HbA1c-change difference for pooled dulaglutide versus placebo.
- **Initial variability:** SD 1.0%.
- **Alpha:** Two-sided 0.05; SSR does not require alpha adjustment.
- **Initial power:** At least 90% pooled and 80% for each dose.
- **Dropout assumption:** 20%.
- **SSR scenario:** SD 1.3%; up to 189 enrolled (63/arm) gives at least 80% pooled and 69% each-dose power.
- **SSR procedure:** SAC could recommend variability-only SSR after about 100 enrolled, bounded at 150–189; Lilly decides implementation. Blinded Week-26 SD is estimated using REML MMRM containing baseline HbA1c, insulin/metformin, treatment, visit, and treatment-by-visit.
- **2019 SSR:** Used SAC-estimated HbA1c SD 1.4%, assumed difference −0.8%, and 10% Week-26 HbA1c missingness; confirmed approximately 150.
- **Statistical test assumed:** Superiority comparison of HbA1c change.
- **Required number of events:** Not applicable; the powered endpoint is continuous HbA1c change, not time-to-event.
- **Non-inferiority/equivalence margin:** Not applicable; superiority trial.
- **Design effect/clustering:** Not applicable; individually randomized parallel-arm trial, with no clustering described.
- **Basis for assumptions:** Limited pediatric GLP-1 RA experience, broad baseline HbA1c values, and inclusion of metformin and/or basal-insulin users.
- **Software:** Not specified.
- **Rationale:** Preserve power while limiting placebo exposure and accommodating uncertainty in pediatric endpoint variability.

## 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

Two primary estimands compare pooled dulaglutide with placebo for Week-26 HbA1c:

- Efficacy: ITT, post-rescue censored.
- Treatment regimen: ITT regardless of rescue/adherence.

Each has full two-sided .05. After H1 pooled-HbA1c success, the graphical procedure transfers alpha among H2–H3 individual HbA1c, H4–H6 HbA1c <7%, H7–H9 FBG, and H10–H12 BMI hypotheses (pooled, 1.5 mg, 0.75 mg versus placebo).

The scheme is separately run for treatment-regimen washout-MI, efficacy, and EMA-restricted efficacy populations and may be revised using blinded Week-26 correlations before lock.

## 2.5 Timing of Analyses

- **Primary database lock:** After all randomized patients complete Week 26/Visit 9. Sponsor unblinds after primary lock to analyze/report primary data.
- **Final database lock:** After all complete the 4-week safety follow-up/Visit 801.
- **Baseline:** Visit 2.
- **Week 13:** Fasting, with pre-dose and 1-to-12-hour post-dose PK.
- **Week 26:** Fasting, pre-dose PK, central laboratories/ECG/EQ-5D-Y; ±6-day window.
- **Week 39:** PK permitted ≤2 days postdose.
- **Week 52:** Fasting, with PK/ECG/EQ-5D-Y; ±7-day window.
- **Visit 801/Week 56:** Approximately 30 days later, with safety follow-up and PK.
- **EQ-5D-Y:** Baseline, Weeks 26/52, or early termination.

## 3.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

### Inclusion Criteria

- Male/female, age 10–<18 years, with IDF-ISPAD T2DM.
- Lifestyle measures, metformin, and basal insulin stable for ≥8 weeks before screening; metformin ≥1000 mg/day or highest tolerated/local-approved dose.
- Screening HbA1c >6.5% to ≤11.0%, or ≤9.0% if newly diagnosed/lifestyle only.
- BMI >85th percentile.
- Weight ≥50 kg.
- Assent/parent consent.

### Exclusion Criteria

- T1DM/antibodies.
- Ketoacidosis/hyperosmolar syndrome.
- GI/pancreatic/thyroid/renal/cardiovascular risks.
- Hb variants.
- Pregnancy/breastfeeding or ineffective contraception.
- Unstable psychiatric treatment.
- Prohibited medicines/other trials.
- Other antihyperglycemics require ≥3-month washout.
- Bolus insulin requires ≥6-week washout except ≤14-day acute-condition rescue.
