# Evaluation Input

## 1.1 Background and Rationale

FOXTROT is a phase IIa study of BAY 1213790, a fully human IgG1 anti-factor XIa antibody that binds FXIa near its catalytic center, in patients undergoing elective primary unilateral total knee arthroplasty (TKA). TKA carries substantial VTE risk, whereas established thromboprophylaxis entails bleeding risk. Selective FXIa inhibition is being assessed for VTE prevention with a potentially favorable bleeding profile. Dose selection (0.3–1.8 mg/kg) was informed by preclinical work, Phase 1 population PK/PD, and systems pharmacology.

## 1.2 Objectives

### Primary Objectives

- **P1:** Assess safety and efficacy of different BAY 1213790 doses versus enoxaparin in elective primary unilateral TKA.
  - **Objective-level outcome P1:** Safety and efficacy of different BAY 1213790 doses versus enoxaparin.

### Secondary/Exploratory Objectives

- **S1 — Secondary:** Compare safety and efficacy of BAY 1213790 with apixaban.
  - **Objective-level outcome S1:** Safety and efficacy of BAY 1213790 compared with apixaban.
- **S2 — Exploratory:** Assess BAY 1213790 dose-response for efficacy and safety.
  - **Objective-level outcome S2:** BAY 1213790 dose-response for efficacy and safety.
- **S3 — Exploratory:** Characterize BAY 1213790 PK/PD and their relationship.
  - **Objective-level outcome S3:** BAY 1213790 PK/PD and their relationship.
- **S4 — Other objective:** Further characterize BAY 1213790 by evaluating additional PD markers, and investigate thrombosis mechanisms using additional PD markers.
  - **Objective-level outcome S4:** Additional PD markers for BAY 1213790 characterization and investigation of thrombosis mechanisms.

## 2.1 Trial Design

### Design Type

Randomized, active-comparator-controlled, multicenter phase IIa study; open-label for treatment assignment, observer-blinded for BAY 1213790 dose, with blinded CIAC adjudication. Part A administers BAY after surgery; sequential Part B administers BAY before surgery, with no parallel recruitment.

### Allocation Ratio

5:1:1 pooled BAY 1213790:enoxaparin:apixaban, adaptable after arm closure/opening. BAY recipients are assigned 1:1:1 among Part A doses and 1:1 in Part B, subject to adjustment after arm closure/opening.

### Intervention Summary

- **Part A, post-surgery:** BAY 1213790 0.3, 0.6, or 1.2 mg/kg IV once after adequate hemostasis on the day after TKA, preferably morning and ≤24 hours after wound closure. A 1.8-mg/kg arm may open.
- **Part B, pre-surgery:** BAY 1213790 0.3 or 1.2 mg/kg IV once in the second half of pre-surgery Day 1. IDMC/SC may select 0.3/0.6/1.2/1.8 mg/kg or add an arm. Part B begins only after Part A completion and review of ≥75% of Part A primary assessments.
- **Comparators in both parts:** Enoxaparin 40 mg SC daily begins the pre-TKA evening or 6–8 hours after TKA and continues ≥10 days to venography. Apixaban 2.5 mg orally twice daily begins after TKA, ≤24 hours from closure, and continues ≥10 days to venography.

## 2.2 Randomization

**Method&nbsp;**

IxRS randomization to BAY 1213790, enoxaparin, or apixaban, followed by IxRS randomization of BAY recipients to dose arm.

- **Stratification factors:** Not specified.
- **Minimization or blocking:** Not specified.

## 2.3 Sample Size

### Planned Sample Size

- **Initial target:** 700 patients: 500 BAY recipients across five intended arms and 100 each receiving enoxaparin or apixaban.
- **Maximum:** 900 patients.
- **Arm targets:** 100 per BAY and comparator arm; comparator enrollment at least 60 in Part A and at least 40 in Part B.
- **Calculation outcome:** Primary efficacy composite VTE endpoint at Day 12+3; binary event proportion.
- **Assumed event proportions:** Enoxaparin 25%; apixaban 10%; BAY 10% and 4% in the respective scenarios.
- **Effect measure and margin:** Risk difference; non-inferiority margin 5%.
- **Significance level:** One-sided 5%.
- **Power scenarios:**
  - BAY 10% versus enoxaparin 25%: 81% superiority and 97% non-inferiority.
  - BAY 4% versus enoxaparin 25%: 99% superiority and 99% non-inferiority.
  - Apixaban scenarios are descriptive calculation context: 0%/29% and 43%/88% superiority/non-inferiority.
- **Assumed tests:** One-sided non-inferiority and superiority tests using the lower bound of a two-sided 90% normal-approximation CI for risk difference.
- **Variability assumption:** Not applicable; calculation is for binary event proportions.
- **Required events:** Not applicable; powered on proportions rather than an event-driven target.
- **Unavailable/non-evaluable venography allowance:** Approximately 20% due to quality or dropout, yielding 80 evaluable patients per arm.
- **Design effect/clustering:** Not applicable; individually randomized, non-clustered study.
- **Basis for assumptions:** Event-rate scenarios and the 20% non-evaluable venography assumption.
- **Re-estimation/amendments:** After approximately 75% of Part A Visit 7 assessments, pooled unavailable venography >30% may trigger a sample increase to regain 80% superiority power. Arm/dose enrollment may also change on IDMC/SC advice, within 900 patients.
- **Software:** Not specified.
- **Rationale:** Phase II comparisons aim to characterize dose safety/efficacy; examples demonstrate non-inferiority/superiority precision versus enoxaparin.

## 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

For each BAY dose/time arm versus enoxaparin, test non-inferiority first, then superiority, each one-sided at global α=5%. The fixed sequence stops after the first unrejected null.

Non-inferiority requires the lower 90% CI bound for BAY minus enoxaparin to be below 5%; superiority requires it below 0.

No hypothesis testing is planned versus apixaban; provide descriptive proportions and 90% CIs.

## 2.5 Timing of Analyses

- **Primary completion:** All patients assessed at Visit 7.
- **Study end:** Last patient’s last visit.
- **Database blinding:** Continues through final database lock after LPLV and authorization of release.
- **Assessment schedule:** Screening Day -13 to 1; randomization Day 1; TKA Day 2; treatment visits Days 3, 4, 6±1, and 12+3; bilateral venography/primary outcomes at Visit 7, Day 12+3; post-treatment visits Day 30±5, Day 90±7, and Day 150±7.

## 3.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

### Inclusion Criteria

- Provide consent.
- Age ≥18 years.
- Elective primary unilateral TKA.
- Meet reproductive/contraception requirements.
- Able to follow instructions.
- Part B requires general anesthesia.

### Exclusion Criteria

- Clinically significant bleeding risk, including specified hematologic, coagulation, hepatic, hypertension, surgery, or bleeding-disorder conditions.
- Prior DVT.
- Weight >135 kg.
- CrCl <60 mL/min by MDRD.
- Recent MI/stroke.
- Active cancer, with specified exceptions.
- Comparator contraindication.
- Full anticoagulation/dual antiplatelets.
- Compression/stimulator use.
- Contrast allergy.
- Prior assignment.
- Recent investigational treatment.
- Site affiliation.
- **Part A:** Postoperative epidural analgesia.
- **Part B:** Regional anesthesia/epidural analgesia.
