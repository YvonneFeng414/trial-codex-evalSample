# Evaluation Input

## 1.1 Background and Rationale

J2T-DM-KGAB (DRM06-AD04; ADvocate1) is a phase 3 study of lebrikizumab monotherapy versus placebo in adolescents and adults with moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis (AD).

## 1.2 Objectives

### Primary Objectives

- **P1:** Evaluate safety and efficacy of lebrikizumab compared with placebo in moderate-to-severe AD. For FDA, the primary endpoint is Week-16 IGA 0/1 with ≥2-point improvement; for EMA, the Week-16 IGA endpoint and EASI-75 are co-primary.
  - **P1 Outcome:** Safety and efficacy compared with placebo; FDA: Week-16 IGA 0/1 with ≥2-point improvement; EMA: Week-16 IGA endpoint and EASI-75.

### Secondary/Exploratory Objectives

- **S1 (Secondary):** Evaluate induction and maintenance efficacy, health outcomes/quality of life, rescue use, and serum lebrikizumab concentration.
  - **S1 Outcome:** Induction and maintenance efficacy, health outcomes/quality of life, rescue use, and serum lebrikizumab concentration.
- **S2 (Exploratory):** Provide supportive analyses of additional efficacy and patient-reported outcomes, and descriptive acceptability/tolerability results from modified SQAAQ in participating EU adolescents.
  - **S2 Outcome:** Additional efficacy and patient-reported outcomes; acceptability/tolerability from modified SQAAQ in participating EU adolescents.

## 2.1 Trial Design

### Design Type

International randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group phase 3 study comprising screening, 16-week double-blind induction, 36-week maintenance, escape treatment where applicable, and approximately 12-week safety follow-up after the last injection for early terminators or those not entering the extension.

### Allocation Ratio

- Induction: 2:1 (LEB250Q2W:placebo).
- Week-16 eligible responders: rerandomized 2:2:1 (LEB250Q2W:LEB250Q4W:placebo).

### Intervention Summary

All injections are subcutaneous.

- **Induction LEB250Q2W:** 500 mg at baseline and Week 2 (two PFS-NSD loading doses), then 250 mg every 2 weeks through Week 14.
- **Induction placebo:** 4 mL at baseline and Week 2, then 2 mL every 2 weeks through Week 14.
- **Maintenance responders:** Lebrikizumab 250 mg Q2W, lebrikizumab 250 mg Q4W, or placebo Q2W; placebo injections maintain blinding.
- **Escape:** Week-16 nonresponders, induction rescue users, or rerandomized patients losing EASI-50 at Weeks 24/32/40/48 receive open-label lebrikizumab 250 mg Q2W to Week 52. Those without EASI-50 after 8 escape weeks discontinue.

## 2.2 Randomization

**Method&nbsp;**

Electronic data-capture (EDC) allocation at baseline after eligibility. EDC rerandomizes maintenance patients according to Week-16 IGA/EASI response and induction rescue use. Protocol Amendment 1 replaced the earlier IWRS wording with EDC.

- **Stratification factors:** Geographic region (US, EU, rest of world), age (12 to <18 versus ≥18 years), and baseline disease severity (IGA 3 versus 4).
- **Minimization or blocking:** Not specified.

## 2.3 Sample Size

### Planned Sample Size

Approximately 400 total, randomized 2:1 lebrikizumab:placebo. The 96:48 calculation was increased to approximately 400 to collect safety information and ensure sufficient Week-16 responders for maintenance.

- **FDA calculation:** Week-16 IGA 0/1, assuming 34.7% for LEB250Q2W versus 7.7% for placebo. With 96 LEB250Q2W and 48 placebo patients, power is >95% using a two-group continuity-corrected chi-square test at two-sided α = 0.05.
- **EMA calculation:** The same 96:48 allocation provides >95% power at two-sided α = 0.05 for each co-primary endpoint, Week-16 IGA 0/1 and EASI-75. Assumed EASI-75 rates are 48.0% for lebrikizumab versus 11.5% for placebo. Overall power for both co-primary endpoints is ≥90%.
- **Outcome type:** Binary response proportions.
- **Stated target differences:** 27.0 percentage points for IGA 0/1 and 36.5 percentage points for EASI-75, calculated from the stated Phase 2b assumptions.
- **Variability assumption:** Binomial response proportions as stated above; separate variance parameter not specified.
- **Basis for assumptions:** DRM06-AD01/J2T-DM-KGAF Phase 2b rescue-medication nonresponse sensitivity results.
- **Required number of events:** Not applicable; calculation is based on fixed-time binary response proportions rather than event accrual.
- **Non-inferiority/equivalence margin:** Not applicable; superiority comparison.
- **Dropout/non-adherence inflation:** Not specified.
- **Design effect/clustering:** Not applicable; individually randomized, non-clustered design.
- **Sample-size re-estimation/amendments:** Not specified.
- **Calculation software:** Not specified.

## 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

The induction superiority null is equality of LEB250Q2W and placebo for Week-16 IGA 0/1 with ≥2-point improvement. EMA additionally tests equality for Week-16 EASI-75.

FDA’s graphical family contains IGA01 W16 followed by EASI-75 W16, EASI-90 W16, Pruritus NRS-4 at Weeks 16/4/2, IGA01 W4, adult IGA01 W16, and sleep-loss response W16.

EMA’s induction graphical family starts with co-primary IGA01 W16 and EASI-75 W16 and includes EASI-90 W16/W4, EASI percent change W16, pruritus percent change and response Weeks 16/4/2, DLQI response/change W16, and sleep-loss response/change W16.

For EMA maintenance, Q2W and Q4W each follow the prespecified ordered sequence:

1. Persistence of EASI-75.
2. Persistence of IGA response.
3. Persistence of ≥4-point pruritus response.
4. Percent EASI change at Week 52.

Maintenance inferential comparisons are LEB-responder Q2W versus placebo and LEB-responder Q4W versus placebo.

## 2.5 Timing of Analyses

- **Final analysis:** Final DBL follows safety follow-up, early discontinuation, or extension enrollment; it may be combined with Week-52 DBL depending on the regulatory timeline.
- **Induction assessments:** Baseline and Weeks 2/4/6/8/10/12/14/16, with ±3-day windows.
- **Maintenance assessments:** Weeks 20/24/28/32/36/40/44/48/52, with ±5-day windows.
- IGA/EASI/BSA are scheduled through Week 16. IGA/EASI/BSA/SCORAD and intermittent PRO/safety assessments continue in maintenance, with Week-52 end-of-study/ETV assessments and a 12-week safety follow-up when required.
- Pruritus NRS and sleep-loss daily diaries are mapped to baseline and Weeks 1, 2, 4, then scheduled visits through Week 52. Baseline requires ≥4 of 7 daily reports; postbaseline weekly values are prorated if ≥1 report is available.
- Out-of-window assessments are retained unless stated otherwise.

## 3.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

### Inclusion Criteria

No prospective enrollment waiver is permitted.

- Adult or adolescent 12 to <18 years weighing ≥40 kg.
- AD by American Academy of Dermatology criteria for ≥1 year.
- Baseline EASI ≥16, IGA ≥3/4, and AD BSA ≥10%.
- Inadequate topical response or medical inadvisability.
- Stable nonmedicated moisturizer twice daily for ≥7 days.
- ≥4 of 7 baseline pruritus/sleep diary entries.
- Ability to comply.
- Specified WOCBP highly effective contraception/abstinence during treatment plus 18 weeks, and male barrier contraception for 18 weeks.
- Consent/assent.

### Exclusion Criteria

- Prior lebrikizumab.
- Anaphylaxis.
- Recent topical AD treatment, dupilumab/tralokinumab, systemic immunomodulator/phototherapy, investigational product/biologic, prescription moisturizer, tanning, or live vaccine.
- Uncontrolled chronic disease/severe asthma.
- Recent/active infection, hepatitis/cirrhosis, parasitic infection risk, immunosuppression, or HIV.
- Significant screening laboratory findings.
- Assessment-interfering skin disease.
- Malignancy within 5 years, except fully treated in-situ cervical or resolved nonmetastatic basal/squamous skin cancer.
- Severe illness/psychological condition.
- Pregnancy, breastfeeding, or pregnancy planning.
