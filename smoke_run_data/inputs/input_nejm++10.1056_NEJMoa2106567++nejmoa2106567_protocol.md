# Evaluation Input

## 1.1 Background and Rationale

EFC14153 evaluates subcutaneous dupilumab in children aged 6 to <12 years with uncontrolled persistent asthma receiving background controller therapy.

## 1.2 Objectives

### Primary Objectives

- **P1:** Evaluate efficacy of dupilumab in children aged 6 to <12 years with uncontrolled persistent asthma.
  - **Objective-level outcome P1:** Efficacy.

### Secondary/Exploratory Objectives

- **S1 — Secondary:** Assess safety/tolerability; PROs including HRQoL; systemic exposure and anti-drug antibodies; and the association of treatment with pediatric immune responses to tetanus, diphtheria, pertussis and/or seasonal trivalent/quadrivalent influenza vaccination.
  - **Objective-level outcome S1:** Safety/tolerability; PROs including HRQoL; systemic exposure; anti-drug antibodies; association of treatment with pediatric immune responses to tetanus, diphtheria, pertussis and/or seasonal trivalent/quadrivalent influenza vaccination.
- **S2 — Exploratory:** Explore biomarker prediction/association with response and optional genetic profiles; evaluate ICS/second-controller step-up and additional PROs.
  - **Objective-level outcome S2:** Biomarker prediction/association with response; optional genetic profiles; ICS/second-controller step-up; additional PROs.

## 2.1 Trial Design

### Design Type

Multinational, multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group study. Screening is 4±1 weeks, treatment is 52 weeks, and a 12-week post-treatment period applies to participants not entering the one-year extension.

### Allocation Ratio

Dupilumab:matching placebo 2:1.

### Intervention Summary

Subcutaneous Q2W dupilumab or matching placebo on background medium-dose ICS plus a second controller, or high-dose ICS alone/with a second controller.

- Baseline weight ≤30 kg: dupilumab 100 mg Q2W/matching placebo (0.67 mL).
- Baseline weight >30 kg: dupilumab 200 mg Q2W/matching placebo (1.14 mL).
- Investigators and patients are unblinded to dose level because volumes differ.

Treatment is for a maximum of 52 weeks. Background controllers continue. Temporary ICS increase up to fourfold for ≤10 days is allowed; permanent controller step-up may follow at least two severe exacerbations. Scheduled visits use a ±5-day window; ETD/EOT assessment and telephone follow-up exceptions are specified.

## 2.2 Randomization

**Method&nbsp;**

Central IVRS/IWRS permuted-block randomization after screening.

Stratification factors:
- Screening ICS dose: medium/high.
- Screening eosinophils: <0.3/≥0.3 Giga/L.
- Region:
  - Latin America: Argentina, Brazil, Colombia, Chile, Mexico.
  - Eastern Europe: Poland, Hungary, Romania, Lithuania, Russia, Ukraine, Turkey.
  - Western Countries: Australia, Canada, Italy, South Africa, Spain, USA.

These strata are distinct from pooled-country regional analysis. Minimization: Not specified.

## 2.3 Sample Size

### Planned Sample Size

At least 402 overall: 268 dupilumab and 134 placebo.

The three powered populations are retained separately:

| Population | Sample size, dupilumab/placebo | Assumed placebo rate | Assumed dupilumab rate | Relative-risk reduction | Power |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Type 2 inflammatory phenotype: eosinophils ≥0.15 Giga/L or FeNO ≥20 ppb | Approximately 345, 230/115 | 0.7/year | 0.322/year | 54% | 94% |
| Eosinophils ≥0.15 Giga/L | 327, 218/109 | 0.7/year | 0.322/year | 54% | 93% |
| Eosinophils ≥0.3 Giga/L | 255, 170/85 | 0.8/year | 0.32/year | 60% | 96% |

Calculation assumptions:
- Outcome: annualized rate of severe exacerbations during 52 weeks.
- Outcome type: negative-binomial count/rate.
- Statistical test assumed: dupilumab-placebo comparison of a negative-binomial severe-exacerbation rate.
- Dispersion parameter: 1.5.
- Allocation: 2:1.
- Two-sided alpha: 0.05.
- Linear discontinuation: 20% at one year.
- Average exposure: 0.9 year.
- Projected prevalences: 86% Type 2, 81% eosinophils ≥0.15 Giga/L, and 64% eosinophils ≥0.3 Giga/L.
- Relative-risk reductions were based on Phase 3 EFC13579 (QUEST).
- Required number of events: Not specified.
- Non-inferiority/equivalence margin: Not applicable; superiority comparison.
- Design effect/clustering: Not applicable; individually randomized trial.
- Calculation software: Not specified.

Sizing assured target power across the three biomarker-defined populations under 2:1 allocation.

Protocol versions:

- Amendment 3 revised the primary populations and final calculation to 402.

## 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

Superiority hypotheses compare dupilumab with placebo for severe-exacerbation rate and sequentially tested efficacy endpoints. The final SAP uses regional hierarchical sequences to control two-sided Type I error at 5%.

**US/reference sequence, orders 1–24:**
1. Severe-exacerbation rate in ≥0.3, ≥0.15, Type 2.
2. Week-12 pre-BD FEV1 in ≥0.3, ≥0.15, Type 2.
3. Week-24 ACQ-7 in ≥0.3, ≥0.15, Type 2.
4. The same three endpoints in FeNO ≥20.
5. Severe-exacerbation rate and FEV1 in full ITT.
6. Severe-exacerbation rate in high-ICS ≥0.3, ≥0.15, Type 2, FeNO ≥20, full ITT.
7. Week-12 FeNO in ≥0.3, ≥0.15, Type 2, FeNO ≥20, full ITT.

**EU/reference sequence:** Uses the same ordered endpoint groups but starts the first three, FEV1 and ACQ-7 sequences Type 2, ≥0.15, ≥0.3, and retains that order for high-ICS and FeNO.

## 2.5 Timing of Analyses

Final database lock occurs after every randomized participant completes Week 52 or discontinues earlier. Analyses through lock are final CSR analyses.

The originally planned two locks were integrated into one final lock. Treatment of post-lock data is decided for the CSR/addendum. Calendar date: Not specified.

Assessment timing:
- Primary rate: through Week 52.
- Key FEV1: Week 12.
- Spirometry: screening, randomization and subsequent visits.
- Post-bronchodilator assessments include Weeks 2, 4, 8, 12, 24, 36, 52 and 64.

## 3.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

### Inclusion Criteria

- Age 6 to <12 years.
- Physician-diagnosed persistent asthma for ≥12 months.
- Uncontrolled persistent asthma.
- Medium-dose ICS plus one controller, or high-dose ICS alone/with a second controller, for ≥3 months, with stable dose ≥1 month before screening; third controller not permitted.
- Pre-BD FEV1 ≤95% predicted or FEV1/FVC <0.85 at screening/baseline.
- ≥10% reversibility, with stated alternatives.
- Prior-year SCS-treated exacerbation or hospital/emergency care.
- Screening uncontrolled-asthma evidence: ACQ-5 ≥1.5, reliever use, sleep awakening, or symptoms.
- Ability to comply, caregiver comprehension, required assent/consent and questionnaire completion.

### Exclusion Criteria

- Age <6 or ≥12 years; weight <16 kg.
- Chronic lung disease or life-threatening asthma.
- Interfering disease, malignancy, or inability to follow procedures.
- Timing-restricted omalizumab/other biologic or immunosuppressant, allergen immunotherapy, investigational medicines, or prohibited concomitants.
- Prior dupilumab study or site-staff relationship.
- <80% diary-recorded background adherence.
- Specified corticosteroid exposure, including prohibited SCS/topical steroid.
- Clinically significant illness, including stated tuberculosis provisions, or major medical disease.
- Pregnancy/contraception restrictions.
- Parasite, HIV, infection, immunosuppression, autoimmune disease, biologic hypersensitivity, or viral-hepatitis risk.
- Liver injury or specified liver/laboratory abnormalities, including stated CPK, platelet, eosinophil and creatinine thresholds; numerical thresholds: Not specified.
- Consent withdrawal or study-level enrollment cessation.
