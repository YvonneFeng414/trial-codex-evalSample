# Evaluation Input

## 1.1 Background and Rationale

Empagliflozin (BI 10773) is evaluated in this Phase III programme to establish long-term cardiovascular safety in adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM), insufficient glycaemic control, and increased cardiovascular (CV) risk. The study is integral to ongoing CV safety assessment and regulatory submission.

## 1.2 Objectives

### Primary Objectives

- **P1:** Demonstrate non-inferiority of pooled empagliflozin 10 mg and 25 mg once daily versus placebo for time to first 3-point major adverse cardiovascular event (3P-MACE), using a hazard-ratio (HR) margin of 1.3.
  - **Outcome P1:** Time to first 3P-MACE, pooled empagliflozin 10 mg and 25 mg once daily versus placebo; HR non-inferiority margin 1.3.

### Secondary/Exploratory Objectives

**Secondary**

- **S1:** Assess the key secondary adjudicated 4-point composite (3P-MACE plus hospitalisation for unstable angina) within the confirmatory hierarchy.
  - **Outcome S1:** Adjudicated 4-point composite of 3P-MACE plus hospitalisation for unstable angina.
- **S2:** Assess occurrence/time to silent MI, heart-failure hospitalisation, new albuminuria, new macroalbuminuria, new/worsening nephropathy and the composite microvascular outcome.
  - **Outcome S2:** Occurrence/time to silent MI, heart-failure hospitalisation, new albuminuria, new macroalbuminuria, new/worsening nephropathy and the composite microvascular outcome.
- **S3:** Assess further CV events: CV death, fatal/nonfatal MI and stroke, unstable-angina hospitalisation, all-cause mortality, TIA and coronary revascularisation.
  - **Outcome S3:** CV death, fatal/nonfatal MI and stroke, unstable-angina hospitalisation, all-cause mortality, TIA and coronary revascularisation.
- **S4:** Assess diabetes-related efficacy: change from baseline in HbA1c, FPG, weight, waist and BP; HbA1c/SBP/weight composite and rescue-therapy use.
  - **Outcome S4:** Change from baseline in HbA1c, FPG, weight, waist and BP; HbA1c/SBP/weight composite and rescue-therapy use.

**Exploratory**

- **S5:** Describe PK trough concentrations, safety (AEs, hypoglycaemia, AESIs, laboratory data, ECG, vital signs), and optional pharmacogenomics.
  - **Outcome S5:** PK trough concentrations; AEs, hypoglycaemia, AESIs, laboratory data, ECG and vital signs; optional pharmacogenomics.
- **S6:** Conduct TSAP/Publication-SAP further analyses, including recurrent CV events, renal/microvascular endpoints, rescue therapy, risk factors, and correlations of SBP/HbA1c with UACR.
  - **Outcome S6:** Recurrent CV events, renal/microvascular endpoints, rescue therapy, risk factors, and correlations of SBP/HbA1c with UACR.

## 2.1 Trial Design

### Design Type

Phase III, international, multicentre, randomised, parallel-group, double-blind, placebo-controlled, add-on-to-standard-care, event-driven CV-safety study.

Screening is followed by a 2-week open-label placebo run-in, double-blind treatment until the required primary events, and 30-day follow-up. Planned treatment is approximately 6 to 8 years, up to approximately 420 weeks, conditional on accrual of the required events.

### Allocation Ratio

1:1:1.

### Intervention Summary

Oral once-daily study drug in addition to background therapy:

- Empagliflozin (BI 10773) 10 mg.
- Empagliflozin (BI 10773) 25 mg.
- Matched placebo.

All patients undergo a 2-week open-label placebo run-in before randomisation. During the first 12 post-randomisation weeks, pre-consent background antidiabetic therapy should remain unchanged where possible; rescue is permitted by criteria. After week 12, background therapy may be adjusted for clinical care.

## 2.2 Randomization

**Method&nbsp;**

Third-party phone/web IVRS/IWRS assigns treatment from a computer-generated pseudorandom, reproducible and non-predictable sequence. Randomisation is blocked and balanced within strata; no minimisation is reported.

Stratification factors:

- Screening HbA1c <8.5%/≥8.5%.
- Randomisation BMI <30/≥30 kg/m².
- North America, Latin America, Europe, Africa or Asia.
- Screening eGFR ≥90, 60–89, or 30–59 mL/min.

For the TSAP primary model, North America includes Australia and New Zealand. Baseline-characteristic tables compare planned IVRS strata with actual CRF strata. Analyses use actual CRF information, with IXRS values only as a stated fallback for missing HbA1c/eGFR.

## 2.3 Sample Size

### Planned Sample Size

- **Total:** 7000 patients: 2333 empagliflozin 10 mg, 2333 empagliflozin 25 mg, and 2334 placebo.
- **Calculation allocation:** The two active arms are pooled, giving 2:1 active:placebo.
- **Calculation outcome:** Time to first 3P-MACE; time-to-event HR non-inferiority endpoint.
- **Assumed hazards:** Identical hazards for pooled empagliflozin and placebo for the non-inferiority event calculation.
- **Control event rate:** No fixed placebo event rate is used for the event requirement. Duration scenarios use yearly event rates of 1.0%, 1.5%, and 2.0%.
- **Non-inferiority margin:** HR 1.3.
- **Non-inferiority power and events:** 90% power requires 690 events without interim adjustment. The event table gives 516/690 events for 80%/90% power.
- **Interim-adjusted event requirement:** Minimum 691 adjudicated primary events.
- **Separate superiority calculation:** 691 events provide at least 80% power to detect HR 0.785, a 21.5% risk reduction.
- **Significance level:** One-sided .025; .0001 is spent at interim and .0249 remains for final analysis.
- **Assumed test:** Log-rank test in EAST Software, version 5, stated equivalent to the Cox proportional-hazards score test.
- **Variability assumption:** Not applicable to the time-to-event event calculation.
- **Dropout/non-adherence inflation:** No numeric inflation factor is reported.
- **Design effect/clustering:** Not applicable; individually randomised trial.
- **Duration assumptions:** 24-month accrual at 3500/year, a 1.5% yearly event rate and 7000 patients yield just under 8 years. Duration scenarios also use 1.0% and 2.0% annual event rates and 2- or 2.5-year accrual.
- **Re-estimation/amendments:** Blinded overall event-rate review may change the recruitment goal. If assumptions/accrual require more recruitment or change, a protocol amendment is required.
- **Stopping basis:** At least 691 adjudicated primary events, not 691 key-secondary events.

The event count is independent of accrual, follow-up and annual event rate; the number randomised and duration depend on them.

## 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

Pooled empagliflozin versus placebo follows a fixed four-step hierarchy:

1. Non-inferiority for primary 3P-MACE: H0 HR≥1.3.
2. Non-inferiority for key-secondary 4P-MACE: H0 HR≥1.3.
3. Superiority for primary 3P-MACE: H0 HR≥1.
4. Superiority for key-secondary 4P-MACE: H0 HR≥1.

Each is tested at one-sided alpha .025. Proceed only if the preceding 95% CI upper bound is below 1.3 for non-inferiority or 1.0 for superiority.

## 2.5 Timing of Analyses

- **Final analysis:** After at least 691 adjudicated primary events. Patients are followed to trial termination; the end is last patient out.
- **Data cutoff/database lock and other data-readiness requirements:** Not specified.
- **Formal interim analysis timing:** Not specified. The sample-size section states that .0001 alpha is spent at interim and .0249 remains for final analysis.
- **Blinded event-rate review:** May change the recruitment goal.
- **Post-randomisation visits:** Weeks 4, 8, 12, 16, 28, 40 and 52, then every 14 weeks.
- **Efficacy assessments:** HbA1c, FPG, weight, waist circumference and BP at weeks 12, 52, annually and end of study. Weight and waist are additionally assessed at 30-day follow-up.
- **Final/EOS visit:** Drug collection/compliance, physical examination, laboratory assessment and ECG.
- **Follow-up:** Visit 98 occurs 30 days after final/EOS and includes BP/pulse, weight/waist, blood/urine safety labs, AEs/endpoints and concomitant therapy.

## 3.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

### Inclusion Criteria

- T2DM before consent and diet/exercise.
- Drug-naïve, with no antidiabetic treatment for 12 weeks, or stable background therapy, including insulin dose within 10% for 12 weeks.
- Screening HbA1c 7.0%–10.0%, or 7.0%–9.0% if drug-naïve.
- Age ≥18 years; Japan ≥20 years; India 18–65 years.
- BMI ≤45 kg/m².
- Consent.
- Qualifying high CV risk: prior MI; specified multivessel/single-vessel CAD or unstable angina; prior stroke; or PAD.
- Women of child-bearing potential require acceptable contraception and periodic pregnancy testing.

### Exclusion Criteria

- Run-in fasting glucose >240 mg/dL confirmed on a second day.
- Liver enzymes/alkaline phosphatase >3×ULN.
- Planned cardiac procedure within 3 months.
- eGFR <30.
- Malabsorption surgery.
- Dyscrasia/haemolysis.
- Cancer within 5 years, except basal-cell carcinoma.
- Contraindicated background therapy.
- Unstable weight intervention.
- Systemic steroids/uncontrolled endocrinopathy.
- Pregnancy/nursing or unsuitable contraception.
- Alcohol/drug abuse or poor compliance.
- Recent/ongoing investigational trial.
- Unsafe condition.
- Acute coronary syndrome/stroke/TIA within 2 months.
- Canada and South Africa have stated local exclusions; details are not specified.
