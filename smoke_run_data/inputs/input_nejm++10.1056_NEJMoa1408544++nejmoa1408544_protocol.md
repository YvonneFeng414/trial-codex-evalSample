# Evaluation Input

## 1.1 Background and Rationale

CAPiTA is a phase 4 trial of 13-valent pneumococcal conjugate vaccine (13vPnC) to prevent vaccine-serotype pneumococcal community-acquired pneumonia (CAP) and invasive pneumococcal disease (IPD) in older adults in the Netherlands. The final controlling protocol is Amendment 9 (01 October 2013).

## 1.2 Objectives

### Primary Objectives

- **P1:** Demonstrate 13vPnC efficacy in prevention of a first episode of confirmed vaccine-type (VT) pneumococcal CAP.
  - **P1 Outcome:** First episode of confirmed VT pneumococcal CAP.

### Secondary/Exploratory Objectives

- **S1 — Secondary:** Demonstrate efficacy against first confirmed nonbacteremic/noninvasive (NB/NI)-VT pneumococcal CAP and first VT-IPD.
  - **S1 Outcome:** First confirmed NB/NI-VT pneumococcal CAP and first VT-IPD.

- **S2 — Exploratory:** Evaluate prevention of all confirmed VT-CAP; first confirmed pneumococcal, NB/NI pneumococcal, CAP, and IPD; first/all confirmed VT-CAP by individual 13vPnC serotype; first/all NVT and pneumococcal CAP; first/all NB/NI-VT-CAP and NB/NI-VT-CAP by individual serotype; first/all culture-confirmed VT, pneumococcal, and NVT CAP; and first/all probable VT, pneumococcal, NVT and possible pneumococcal CAP.
  - **S2 Outcome:** All confirmed VT-CAP; first confirmed pneumococcal, NB/NI pneumococcal, CAP, and IPD; first/all confirmed VT-CAP by individual 13vPnC serotype; first/all NVT and pneumococcal CAP; first/all NB/NI-VT-CAP and NB/NI-VT-CAP by individual serotype; first/all culture-confirmed VT, pneumococcal, and NVT CAP; and first/all probable VT, pneumococcal, NVT and possible pneumococcal CAP.

- **S3 — Exploratory:** Evaluate all CAP; all VT-IPD, first/all NVT-IPD, all IPD; deaths from confirmed VT pneumococcal CAP/VT-IPD, pneumococcal CAP/IPD and all causes; hospitalization with CAP plus influenza A/B and specified respiratory viruses; age efficacy trends and economic effect.
  - **S3 Outcome:** All CAP; all VT-IPD, first/all NVT-IPD, all IPD; deaths from confirmed VT pneumococcal CAP/VT-IPD, pneumococcal CAP/IPD and all causes; hospitalization with CAP plus influenza A/B and specified respiratory viruses; age efficacy trends and economic effect.

- **S4 — Exploratory:** Evaluate VT, all, NVT and individual-serotype S. pneumoniae carriage and H. influenzae, M. catarrhalis, and S. aureus carriage at 6, 12, 24 months; safety objectives are SAE incidence, subset reactogenicity/AEs and deaths; immunogenicity objective is serotype IgG/OPA by age and sex at baseline and approximately months 1, 12, 24.
  - **S4 Outcome:** VT, all, NVT and individual-serotype S. pneumoniae carriage and H. influenzae, M. catarrhalis, and S. aureus carriage at 6, 12, 24 months; SAE incidence, subset reactogenicity/AEs and deaths; serotype IgG/OPA by age and sex at baseline and approximately months 1, 12, 24.

## 2.1 Trial Design

### Design Type

Phase 4, parallel-group, randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blind trial conducted at multiple sites/regions in the Netherlands by the Julius Center.

### Allocation Ratio

1:1, 13vPnC versus placebo.

### Intervention Summary

One 0.5-mL intramuscular deltoid dose at Visit 1.

- **13vPnC:** Serotypes 1, 3, 4, 5, 6A, 6B, 7F, 9V, 14, 18C, 19A, 19F, and 23F conjugated to CRM197; 2 micrograms each except 6B 4 micrograms, pH 5.8, 5 mM succinate, 0.85% sodium chloride, 0.02% polysorbate 80, and 0.125 mg aluminum phosphate.
- **Placebo:** pH 5.8, 5 mM succinate, 0.15 M sodium chloride, 0.02% polysorbate 80, and 0.125 mg aluminum per 0.5 mL.

## 2.2 Randomization

**Method&nbsp;**

Sponsor/designee randomization schedule; eligible consented subjects receive the next available syringe, whose PAC identifies allocation. Randomized block design with vaccine groups randomly ordered within blocks; several blocks assigned at a time.

Age stratification applies to the immunogenicity subset: 65–69, 70–79, and ≥80 years, with at least 300 in each. Trial-wide randomization stratification is not specified.

## 2.3 Sample Size

### Planned Sample Size

- **Total:** Approximately 85,000 participants, 42,500/group.
- **Immunogenicity subset:** 2,000 participants, 1,000/group.
- **Calculation outcome:** First confirmed per-protocol VT-CAP; supplementary power statements address NB/NI-VT-CAP and VT-IPD.
- **Outcome type:** Time-to-first confirmed clinical event, analyzed as incidence rate/relative risk.
- **Assumed control-arm rates:** VT-CAP incidence 1.2/1000/year; placebo CAP, confirmed pneumococcal CAP, and confirmed VT-CAP rates of 10, 2.5, and 1.2/1000/year, respectively.
- **Assumed efficacy:** True VT-CAP efficacy at least 52%; specificity-adjusted efficacy 48.4% in the revised model.
- **Target effect:** Vaccine efficacy >0 based on the lower confidence bound; interim clinical-significance threshold is a VT-CAP lower bound >0.20.
- **Specificity assumptions:** Test specificity at least 97% initially; SSUAD subsequently validated at 98%.
- **Alpha:** Two-sided 0.05 experiment-wise.
- **Power:** 90% for primary VT-CAP; ≥80% for NB/NI-VT-CAP under stated assumptions; ≥90% for VT-IPD if true efficacy ≥90%.
- **Subset precision:** IgG precision 0.10–0.16 for 95% CI differences.
- **Statistical basis:** Breslow-Day conditional binomial derivation for the revised primary event target; exact conditional confidence interval analysis with O'Brien-Fleming adjustment.
- **Original event target:** 156 primary events.
- **Revised event targets:** 65 interim and 130 final first confirmed per-protocol VT-CAP events. DMC administrative review led to revision after observed incidence was lower than predicted.
- **VT-IPD event requirement:** 20 events for the stated power.
- **Non-inferiority/equivalence margin:** Not applicable; efficacy/superiority framework.
- **Loss to follow-up:** 5% assumed.
- **Design effect/clustering:** Not applicable; individually randomized, non-clustered trial.
- **Basis for assumptions:** Clinical feasibility, one enrollment season, ≥2 years follow-up, incidence assumptions, assay specificity, DMC review, and an external consultant-supported revised model.
- **Calculation software:** Not specified.
- **Rationale:** 85,000 participants were selected to accrue required primary events; the 2,000-person subset was based on six-month safety regulatory requirements and feasibility.

## 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

The primary inference is vaccine efficacy = 1 − relative risk for first confirmed VT-CAP, with success when the adjusted two-sided 95% CI lower bound exceeds 0.

NB/NI-VT-CAP is hierarchically tested only following clinically significant primary efficacy.

VT-IPD is evaluated once at study conclusion and is efficacious if its two-sided 95% CI exceeds 0.

## 2.5 Timing of Analyses

- **Final analysis:** If no interim efficacy declaration, final efficacy inference occurs after investigator sign-off of ≥130 per-protocol first confirmed VT-CAP events, cleaning, unblinding, and database freeze.
- **Interim-declared efficacy:** Uses the interim dataset for inference, with overrunning data supplementary.
- **CAP/IPD surveillance:** Runs through the case-acquisition stopping rule.
- **Immunogenicity blood collection:** Baseline and months 1, 12, 24.
- **Carriage assessments:** Baseline and months 6, 12, 24.
- **Safety assessments:** 7-day reactogenicity and 28-day unsolicited AE windows. SAE assessment windows: Not specified.

## 3.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

### Inclusion Criteria

- Adults ≥65 years at vaccination.
- Registered with a referring GP.
- Able to meet study requirements.
- Subset subjects must complete e-diary procedures.

### Exclusion Criteria

- Prior pneumococcal vaccination.
- Institutional/semiskilled care.
- Vaccine contraindication.
- Recent investigational product.
- Severe vaccine reaction.
- Defined immune deficiency/suppression.
- For subset subjects, influenza vaccination in the preceding 7 days.
- Acute febrile illness ≥38.0 C or recent acute illness delays enrollment.
