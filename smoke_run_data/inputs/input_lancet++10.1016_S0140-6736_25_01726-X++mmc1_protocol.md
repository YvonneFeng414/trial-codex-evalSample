# Evaluation Input

## 1.1 Background and Rationale

POInT is a phase IIb primary-prevention trial in genetically at-risk infants. It tests whether sustained oral exposure to insulin during the usual period of initiation of beta-cell autoimmunity induces immune tolerance and reduces beta-cell autoimmunity and diabetes.

## 1.2 Objectives

### Primary Objectives

- **P1:** Determine whether daily oral insulin from age 4–7 months to age 3 years reduces cumulative incidence of beta-cell autoantibodies and diabetes in childhood.
  - **P1 Outcome:** Cumulative incidence of beta-cell autoantibodies and diabetes in childhood.

### Secondary/Exploratory Objectives

- **S1 — Secondary:** Compare development of any persistent confirmed beta-cell autoantibody, persistent confirmed IAA, persistent confirmed GADA, and dysglycaemia or diabetes.
  - **S1 Outcome:** Development of any persistent confirmed beta-cell autoantibody, persistent confirmed IAA, persistent confirmed GADA, and dysglycaemia or diabetes.
- **S2 — Exploratory:** Assess treatment effects in subgroups and in immunologic, transcriptomic, metabolic, and viral-antibody longitudinal measures; assess type 1 diabetes and progression from seroconversion to clinical type 1 diabetes.
  - **S2 Outcome:** Immunologic, transcriptomic, metabolic, and viral-antibody longitudinal measures; type 1 diabetes; progression from seroconversion to clinical type 1 diabetes.

## 2.1 Trial Design

### Design Type

Investigator-initiated, multicentre, multinational, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, phase IIb primary-prevention study.

### Allocation Ratio

1:1 oral insulin:placebo.

### Intervention Summary

Daily parent-administered oral capsule, preferably 07:00–10:00. Treatment ends the day before the age-3 visit or with clinical diabetes.

- **Oral insulin:** 7.5 mg for 2 months, then 22.5 mg for 2 months, then 67.5 mg until age 3 years. Human recombinant insulin crystals plus microcrystalline cellulose to a total capsule weight of 200 mg, in a 0.5-mL hard gelatin capsule.
- **Placebo:** Matching microcrystalline-cellulose capsule daily until age 3 years.

## 2.2 Randomization

**Method&nbsp;**

Package-number randomization by the study-drug manufacturer; a separate randomization list assigns multiples to the same arm.

- **Stratification factors:** Not specified.
- **Minimization or blocking:** Not specified.

## 2.3 Sample Size

### Planned Sample Size

1,050 infants allocated 1:1. The original design projected 1,040; completed enrollment was 1,050.

**Original calculation**

- **Calculation outcome:** Persistent confirmed multiple beta-cell autoantibodies or diabetes.
- **Outcome type:** Time-to-event/hazard-rate outcome.
- **Control assumption:** 7.5% event probability at 3.5 years, corresponding under an exponential distribution to hazard 0.02227.
- **Treatment assumption:** Hazard halved versus placebo, to 0.011135.
- **Target effect:** 50% risk reduction/hazard-rate reduction.
- **Distribution assumption:** Exponential time-to-event distribution.
- **Significance level:** Two-sided overall 0.05.
- **Power:** 80%.
- **Test:** Two-sided log-rank test of equal hazard rates.
- **Required complete follow-up:** 832 children, uniformly randomized over 3.5 years and followed another 3.5 years.
- **Dropout allowance:** 20%, yielding 1,040 children to be randomized.
- **Basis:** Expected primary-outcome frequency, exponential hazard model, accrual and follow-up assumptions, and anticipated dropout.
- All randomized children, including those contributing observation time to dropout, contribute to the primary analysis.

**October 2021 blinded update**

- Used observed blinded dropout, accrual, and event-rate information.
- Placebo hazard used in the re-estimate: 0.02456; halved treatment-hazard scenario: 0.01228.
- Projected power: 81.3% under the observed total hazard and halved treatment hazard.
- Extrapolated dropout: 12% (95% CI 8–15%).
- Completed accrual was 3.17 years with 3.25-year follow-up.
- Updated calculation used PROC POWER, SAS 9.4, based on the Lakatos formula.
- No power issue was judged to prevent trial end six months after last treatment.

**Other sample-size information**

- **Required number of events:** Not specified.
- **Non-inferiority/equivalence margin:** Not applicable; superiority framework.
- **Design effect/clustering:** Not applicable; individually randomized trial.

## 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

Primary and secondary endpoints are superiority endpoints. The primary null compares equal hazard rates. The primary confirmatory comparison is oral insulin versus placebo for persistent confirmed multiple beta-cell autoantibodies or diabetes.

## 2.5 Timing of Analyses

- **Final analysis:** After the observation period defined as six months after the last participant’s last treatment day, expected June 2024, final data export, and quality control.
- **Database lock:** October 23, 2024.
- **Assessment schedule:** Eight intervention visits from baseline through age 3 years and up to nine post-age-3 follow-up visits; blood for autoantibodies at every visit.
- Persistent-autoantibody-positive children undergo OGTTs from age 3 years at six-month intervals.

## 3.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

### Inclusion Criteria

- Age 4–7 months.
- >10% risk of developing multiple beta-cell autoantibodies by age 6 years.
- Solid food introduced.
- Parental written consent.
- Without first-degree type 1 diabetes: DR3/DR4-DQ8 or DR4-DQ8/DR4-DQ8 plus genetic-risk score >14.4.
- With a first-degree history: HLA DR4 and DQ8 without DRB1*1501 or DQB1*0503.

### Exclusion Criteria

- Interfering disease/treatment.
- A condition associated with poor compliance.
- Unsafe medical condition.
- Diabetes at recruitment.
- Another clinical trial.
