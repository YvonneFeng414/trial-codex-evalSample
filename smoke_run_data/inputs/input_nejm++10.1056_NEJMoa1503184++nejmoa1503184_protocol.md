# Evaluation Input

## 1.1 Background and Rationale

GRIPHON evaluates selexipag (ACT-293987), a non-prostanoid IP-receptor agonist, in pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH), with morbidity/mortality, exercise capacity, and safety evaluated against placebo.

## 1.2 Objectives

### Primary Objectives

- **P1:** Demonstrate the effect of selexipag versus placebo on time to first CEC-confirmed morbidity/mortality event in PAH.
  - **Objective-level outcome:** Time to first CEC-confirmed morbidity/mortality event in PAH, comparing selexipag versus placebo.

### Secondary/Exploratory Objectives

- **S1:** Evaluate selexipag versus placebo for exercise capacity and other secondary/exploratory efficacy endpoints in PAH.
  - **Objective-level outcome:** Exercise capacity and other secondary/exploratory efficacy endpoints in PAH, comparing selexipag versus placebo.
- **S2:** Evaluate safety and tolerability of selexipag in PAH (AC-065A302 and AC-065A303).
  - **Objective-level outcome:** Safety and tolerability of selexipag in PAH.

Exploratory objectives are not reported as separately labelled objectives. Exploratory efficacy endpoints and exploratory dose-response, pharmacoeconomic, PK, and safety analyses are enumerated.

## 2.1 Trial Design

### Design Type

Prospective, multicenter, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled, parallel-group Phase 3, event-driven study AC-065A302/GRIPHON. AC-065A303 is an open-label, uncontrolled extension for long-term safety/tolerability.

### Allocation Ratio

Placebo:selexipag 1:1; 1,150 planned patients, 575 per group.

### Intervention Summary

Day-1 evening dose is 200 µg twice daily selexipag or matching placebo. Weekly 200-µg twice-daily increments reach an individual maximum tolerated dose up to 1,600 µg twice daily, with reductions for tolerability. From Week 12, the dose is held to Week 26; later increases are allowed at scheduled visits. Treatment continues until 331 CEC-confirmed events, with a maximum of 4.3 years.

- **Selexipag:** Initially 200 µg twice daily, titrated to an individual tolerated dose ≤1,600 µg twice daily.
- **Placebo:** Matching ACT-293987 placebo twice daily under the same titration schedule.

## 2.2 Randomization

**Method&nbsp;**

Independent centralized IWRS/IVRS assigns a unique randomization number; the code remains confidential until unblinding.

- **Stratification:** Site.
- **Minimization or blocking:** Not specified.

## 2.3 Sample Size

### Planned Sample Size

**Version 7**

- **Planned enrollment:** 1,150 randomized patients, 575 per group.
- **Calculation outcome:** Time to first CEC-confirmed morbidity/mortality event.
- **Outcome type:** Time-to-event.
- **Control-arm constant event hazard:** 0.2231/year.
- **Treatment-arm constant event hazard:** 0.145/year.
- **Target hazard ratio:** 0.65.
- **Variance assumption:** Variance under the alternative hypothesis.
- **Constant dropout hazard:** 0.051/year in both arms; no separate numerical sample-size inflation reported.
- **Significance level:** One-sided overall α=0.005 in the group-sequential design.
- **Power:** 90% to reject the null hypothesis.
- **Assumed test:** One-sided unstratified log-rank test in a group-sequential setting.
- **Required events:** 331 first CEC-confirmed events.
- **Non-inferiority/equivalence margin:** Not applicable; superiority comparison.
- **Design effect/clustering:** Not applicable; individually randomized, non-clustered trial.
- **Basis for assumptions:** Event and dropout hazards, target hazard ratio, group-sequential design, and variance under the alternative.
- **Software:** EAST 5.3, using its variance-under-alternative option.
- **Rationale:** Under the stated hazards, 331 events achieve 90% power. Recruitment of up to 1,150 patients randomized 1:1 was expected to yield the target.
- **Interim operating characteristics:** Planned probability of rejecting the null at interim was approximately 19%; under HR=1, the probability of accepting the null at interim was approximately 50%.

**Earlier Version 1**

A blinded adaptation of sample size up to 1,150 patients was allowed.

The SAP uses the final Version-7 specifications of 1,150 planned patients and 331 events.

## 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

For the primary endpoint, the one-sided null is that selexipag survival for remaining free of a first CEC-confirmed event is no greater than placebo at all times. The alternative is no worse at all times and greater at some time. The primary comparison is superiority.

## 2.5 Timing of Analyses

- **Final analysis:** A302 includes Treatment and post-treatment observation data, excluding Treatment Extension; cutoff 27-Apr-2014. A303 safety data through 10-Mar-2014 are included.
- **Assessment schedule:** Screening ≤28 days before Day 1; baseline/randomization on Day 1; titration through Week 8; visits at Week 12, Week 16, Week 26, then every 6 months; EOS and safety follow-up.
- **Study closure:** Prompts EOS or PTOCV within four weeks.
- **Treatment Extension:** Runs from EOS to EOTE after unblinding.
- **6MWT/Borg assessments:** Baseline and scheduled follow-ups.
- **Week-26 endpoint assessments:** Before the morning dose (trough).
- **Database-lock and data-readiness requirements:** Not specified.

## 3.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

### Inclusion Criteria

- Age 18–75 years.
- Symptomatic Group-1 idiopathic, heritable, drug/toxin-induced, connective-tissue-disease-associated, repaired-shunt congenital-heart-disease-associated, or HIV-associated PAH.
- Documented RHC: mPAP ≥25 mmHg, PVR ≥400 dyn·s·cm−5, and PCWP/LVEDP ≤15 mmHg.
- Screening 6MWD 50–450 m.
- Women of childbearing potential require contraception with failure rate <1%/year through one month after the final dose.
- ERAs/PDE5 inhibitors are permitted if stable ≥3 months before baseline through Week 26.

### Exclusion Criteria

- PH Groups 2–5.
- Prostacyclin/analogue treatment within one month or planned during the trial, except a single administration for acute vasodilator testing.
- Obstructive disease: FEV1/FVC <70% and FEV1 <65% predicted.
- Restrictive disease: TLC <70% predicted.
- Child-Pugh B/C impairment.
- LVEF <45%.
- Severe renal dysfunction.
- BMI <18.5.
- Recent investigational drug.
- Inability to comply or perform 6MWT.
- Rehabilitation completed <8 weeks before screening or planned.
- Impaired consent/adherence capacity.
- Life expectancy <12 months.
- Pregnancy/lactation.

Prostacyclin/analogues and investigational drugs are prohibited.
