# Evaluation Input

## 1.1 Background and Rationale

ING114467 is a Phase III study in antiretroviral therapy (ART)-naïve adults with HIV-1 evaluating once-daily dolutegravir (DTG; GSK1349572) plus abacavir/lamivudine (ABC/3TC) versus fixed-dose efavirenz/emtricitabine/tenofovir (Atripla). The study aims to establish non-inferior antiviral activity and characterize longer-term safety, tolerability, resistance, and health outcomes. DTG 50 mg once daily was selected from Phase 2b dose-ranging data.

## 1.2 Objectives

### Primary Objectives

- **P1 Objective:** To demonstrate antiviral activity of DTG plus ABC/3TC FDC once daily versus Atripla over 48 weeks in HIV-1 infected, ART-naïve subjects.
  - **P1 Outcome:** Antiviral activity of DTG plus ABC/3TC FDC once daily versus Atripla over 48 weeks.

### Secondary/Exploratory Objectives

- **S1 Objective:** Demonstrate antiviral activity through Week 96.
  - **S1 Outcome:** Antiviral activity through Week 96.
- **S2 Objective:** Evaluate antiviral activity through Week 144.
  - **S2 Outcome:** Antiviral activity through Week 144.
- **S3 Objective:** Compare tolerability, long-term safety, antiviral and immunologic activity over time.
  - **S3 Outcome:** Tolerability, long-term safety, antiviral and immunologic activity over time.
- **S4 Objective:** Assess development of viral resistance in subjects experiencing virologic failure.
  - **S4 Outcome:** Development of viral resistance in subjects experiencing virologic failure.
- **S5 Objective:** Assess change in symptom bother count.
  - **S5 Outcome:** Change in symptom bother count.
- **S6 Objective:** Evaluate incidence of HIV-associated conditions over time.
  - **S6 Outcome:** Incidence of HIV-associated conditions over time.
- **S7 Objective:** Explore the impact of gender, race and/or HIV-1 subtype on response.
  - **S7 Outcome:** Response by gender, race and/or HIV-1 subtype.
- **S8 Objective:** Explore change in utility and health-related quality of life.
  - **S8 Outcome:** Change in utility and health-related quality of life.

Exploratory objectives are not reported; all stated objectives are designated primary or secondary. Exploratory analyses are described as analyses rather than objectives.

## 2.1 Trial Design

### Design Type

Phase III, multicentre, randomized, stratified, double-blind, double-dummy, active-controlled, parallel-group, fully powered non-inferiority study.

The double-blind randomized phase runs from Day 1 through Week 96. Amendment 05 provides an open-label randomized phase through Week 144 and a DTG continuation phase thereafter.

### Allocation Ratio

1:1; approximately 800 randomized subjects, approximately 400 per arm.

### Intervention Summary

Once daily, double-dummy, preferably fasting/at bedtime:

- **DTG + ABC/3TC:** DTG 50 mg tablet plus ABC/3TC 600/300 mg tablet and Atripla placebo.
- **Atripla:** EFV/FTC/TDF 600/200/300 mg tablet plus DTG and ABC/3TC placebos.

## 2.2 Randomization

**Method&nbsp;**

Central computer-generated randomization after entry criteria confirmation, using GSK validated RANDALL/RandAll software to generate the schedule.

Stratification factors are screening plasma HIV-1 RNA ≤100,000 versus >100,000 c/mL and screening CD4+ count ≤200 versus >200 cells/mm3, creating four strata.

A fixed block size is used and remains confidential. Minimization: Not specified.

## 2.3 Sample Size

### Planned Sample Size

- **Recruitment target:** Approximately 800 randomized subjects, approximately 400 per arm.
- **Required evaluable sample:** 394 subjects per arm.
- **Calculation outcome:** Proportion with plasma HIV-1 RNA <50 c/mL at Week 48.
- **Outcome type:** Binary virologic response rate.
- **Control-arm assumption:** 75% Week-48 response in the Atripla (TDF/FTC/EFV) arm.
- **Treatment-arm assumption:** Same 75% response rate for non-inferiority planning; a distinct DTG value is not specified.
- **Target difference/non-inferiority margin:** −10 percentage points for DTG minus Atripla response rate.
- **Variability:** Binomial response-rate calculation; separate variance assumption not specified.
- **Alpha:** One-sided 2.5%.
- **Power:** 90%.
- **Assumed statistical assessment:** Lower bound of a two-sided 95% CI for the difference in proportions compared with −10%.
- **Required events:** Not applicable; powered on a binary response rate rather than an event-driven time-to-event design.
- **Dropout/non-adherence inflation:** No numerical inflation specified. The 75% planning response incorporates historical approximately 15% drug-related and 14% non-drug-related failure context.
- **Design effect/clustering:** Not applicable; individual randomized parallel-group trial without a cluster design.
- **Basis for assumptions:** Historical Atripla-controlled and GSK trials, with approximate control response of 71%, recent EFV-arm range of 71%–82%, and anticipated better non-drug compliance.
- **Sample-size re-estimation:** None will be performed.
- **Calculation software:** Not specified.

The stated rationale is that 394 evaluable subjects per arm provides 90% power with a 75% control response, a 10% non-inferiority margin, and one-sided 2.5% significance level.

## 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

- **Null hypothesis:** H0: pDTG − pAtripla ≤ −10%.
- **Alternative hypothesis:** H1: pDTG − pAtripla > −10%.
- **Non-inferiority decision:** DTG is non-inferior if the lower bound of the two-sided 95% CI for the response-rate difference is greater than −10%.
- **Superiority sequence:** If ITT-E and PP analyses demonstrate non-inferiority, ITT-E superiority is tested two-sided at 5% and declared if the lower 95% CI bound exceeds 0.
- **Conditional secondary testing:** Conditional on non-inferiority, key secondary superiority tests are generalized Wilcoxon for time to suppression, repeated-measures mixed model for Week-48 CD4, and ANCOVA for Week-4 SDM bother, in the specified fallback sequence.

## 2.5 Timing of Analyses

- **Final analysis:** Once all subjects have completed or discontinued; Week 48 is primary.
- **RNA assessments in the final amended protocol:** Screening, Day 1, Weeks 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48, and every 12 weeks thereafter through Week 144.
- **CD4/CD8, SDM, EQ-5D, and safety assessment timing:** Not specified.
- Amendment 05 documents Week-144 open-label/continuation conduct; actual dates allocate analysis windows.
- **Data cutoff, database-lock requirements, and data-readiness requirements:** Not specified.

## 3.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

### Inclusion Criteria

- Able to understand and comply, expected to complete the study as planned, and appropriate candidates for oral investigational therapy.
- HIV-1 infected adults ≥18 years.
- Screening HIV-1 RNA ≥1000 c/mL.
- ART-naïve, defined as ≤10 prior ART days after diagnosis.
- HLA-B*5701 negative.
- Dated written informed consent.
- Women must be non-child-bearing—post-menopausal with 12 months amenorrhea and ≥45 years, or documented sterilization—or have negative screening and Day-1 pregnancy tests and use protocol-specified contraception consistently from two weeks before IP through two weeks after it. Permitted methods include abstinence, double barrier, qualifying IUD/other <1%-failure method, or hormonal contraception plus barrier method.
- French enrollment additionally requires social-security affiliation/beneficiary status.

One re-screen with a new number and one repeat test per analyte/assessment are permitted. Genotype retesting is not permitted.

### Exclusion Criteria

- Breastfeeding.
- Active CDC Category C disease, except cutaneous Kaposi not requiring systemic therapy; historical/current CD4 <200 is not exclusionary.
- Hepatic impairment.
- HBsAg positivity or anticipated HCV therapy.
- Recent GI bleed.
- Allergy/intolerance to study drugs or class.
- Recent/ongoing malignancy, except specified skin/Kaposi exceptions.
- Recent HIV immunotherapeutic vaccine, radiation, cytotoxic chemotherapy, immunomodulator, HIV-active agent, or experimental drug/vaccine, including a France-specific restriction.
- Primary resistance.
- Verified Grade-4/acute disqualifying laboratory abnormality.
- ALT/bilirubin thresholds; numerical thresholds not specified.
- CrCl <50 mL/min by Cockcroft–Gault.
