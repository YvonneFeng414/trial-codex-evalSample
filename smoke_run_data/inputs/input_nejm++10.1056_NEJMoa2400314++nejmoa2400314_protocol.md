# Evaluation Input

## 1.1 Background and Rationale

Acute ischemic stroke (AIS) produces neurologic impairment, disability and mortality. This phase III study evaluates genetically engineered recombinant human tissue-type plasminogen activator derivative (reteplase), a bolus-administered thrombolytic, against alteplase for AIS treated within 4.5 hours of symptom onset.

## 1.2 Objectives

### Primary Objectives

- **P1:** Evaluate reteplase versus alteplase effectiveness for AIS within 4.5 hours, operationalized as the difference in the proportion with mRS 0–1 at day 90.
  - **P1 Outcome:** Difference in the proportion with mRS 0–1 at day 90 between reteplase and alteplase.

### Secondary/Exploratory Objectives

- **S1:** Evaluate safety and secondary functional and neurologic outcomes of reteplase versus alteplase, including favorable function, mRS distribution, NIHSS improvement, Barthel Index, hemorrhage, AE/SAE and death outcomes.
  - **S1 Outcome:** Favorable function, mRS distribution, NIHSS improvement, Barthel Index, hemorrhage, AE/SAE and death outcomes for reteplase versus alteplase.

Exploratory objectives were not reported as a separately labelled objective.

## 2.1 Trial Design

### Design Type

Multicenter, prospective, randomized, open-label with blinded endpoint assessment, positive active-controlled parallel-group phase III noninferiority trial in Chinese patients with AIS within 4.5 hours of onset.

### Allocation Ratio

1:1 reteplase:alteplase.

### Intervention Summary

Thrombolysis must begin within 4.5 hours of last known well.

- **Reteplase:** 18 mg IV bolus followed by a second 18 mg IV bolus 30±5 minutes after completion of the first; each injection lasts at least 2 minutes.
- **Alteplase:** 0.9 mg/kg IV (maximum 90 mg), 10% as a bolus within 1 minute and the remainder infused over 60 minutes.

## 2.2 Randomization

**Method&nbsp;**

An independent statistician generates random numbers in SAS 9.4 or higher. After eligibility, the investigator uses IWRS to obtain allocation. Random numbers of withdrawn participants are retained and cannot be replaced or reused.

SAP v1.0 describes simple stratified randomization but names no factors. Final Protocol V2.2 specifies variable-block 1:1 randomization without describing stratification. No individual stratification factor is reported. Initial random-number seed parameters are retained.

## 2.3 Sample Size

### Planned Sample Size

- **Total:** 1,412 participants, 706 per group.
- **Calculation outcome:** Proportion with mRS 0–1 at day 90 after treatment; binary excellent functional outcome.
- **Assumed control-arm value:** Alteplase primary efficacy proportion of 62.5%.
- **Assumed treatment effect:** True reteplase:alteplase efficacy RR of 1.05.
- **Noninferiority margin:** RR 0.93, derived from the historical alteplase versus placebo lower 95% CI RR of 1.15 with f=0.5.
- **Variability assumption:** Not applicable for the binary proportion/RR calculation.
- **Alpha:** One-sided 0.025.
- **Assumed test:** One-sided noninferiority RR test, with noninferiority demonstrated by a lower CI bound above 0.93.
- **Protocol V2.2 and SAP v1.0:** 80% power and approximately 15% dropout/non-adherence inflation.
- **SAP v1.1:** 85% power and 3% dropout/non-adherence inflation. These discrepancies require reconciliation before analysis.
- **Event requirement and safety rationale:** No event-count requirement for the primary binary endpoint. The safety rationale estimates sICH at 1% and death at 5%, with >99% probability of observing at least one death or sICH.
- **Design effect/clustering:** Not applicable to sample-size calculation.
- **Basis for assumptions:** Prior alteplase trials and Phase II reteplase data; SITS-MOST for sICH and NOR-TEST for death.
- **Original Protocol V1.0:** Planned Mehta–Pocock re-estimation conditional on interim efficacy 50%–80%.
- **Protocol V2.2:** Does not retain that interim procedure.
- **SAP v1.0/v1.1:** State no interim efficacy analysis. The version-specific conflict must be settled before execution.
- **Calculation software:** Not specified.
- **Rationale:** The 1:1 sample targets noninferiority for day-90 mRS 0–1 while providing substantial probability of observing key safety events.

## 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

The primary hypothesis is H0: RR≤0.93 versus H1: RR>0.93, where RR is the day-90 mRS 0–1 rate in reteplase divided by that in alteplase.

Noninferiority is concluded at one-sided P<0.025 or lower RR 95% CI bound >0.93. Superiority is then concluded if the lower RR CI bound is >1.

## 2.5 Timing of Analyses

Final analysis is conducted after the end of the entire trial and after database lock, protocol-violation identification and treatment unblinding.

Assessment schedule: Screening/D1; 24h±2h; 72h±6h; D8±1d; D31±3d; and D91±7d.

NIHSS is assessed at baseline, 24h and D8. mRS and Barthel are assessed at D31 and D91, with blinded endpoint evaluation at follow-up.

## 3.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

### Inclusion Criteria

- Age 18–80 years.
- WHO-defined AIS within 4.5 hours of last known well.
- NIHSS 4–25 before thrombolysis.
- Agreement to contraception where applicable.
- Ability to comply.
- Participant or guardian consent.

### Exclusion Criteria

- Non-ascertainable onset.
- Weight <45 or >120 kg.
- Pre-stroke mRS≥2.
- NIHSS consciousness item 1a≥2.
- Hemorrhage/imaging/anticoagulation risks.
- Uncontrolled blood pressure.
- Out-of-range glucose.
- Large infarction.
- Important hepatic or other comorbidity.
- Planned/received endovascular treatment.
- Interfering treatments.
- Recent trial participation.
- Pregnancy/lactation.
- Investigator-determined barriers to compliance.
