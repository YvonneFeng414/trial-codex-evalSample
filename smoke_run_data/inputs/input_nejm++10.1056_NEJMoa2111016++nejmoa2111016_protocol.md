# Evaluation Input

## 1.1 Background and Rationale

ENVISAGE-TAVI AF is a phase 3b trial of edoxaban versus vitamin K antagonist (VKA) in people with atrial fibrillation (AF) requiring chronic oral anticoagulation after transcatheter aortic valve implantation (TAVI). The final SAP v3.0 applies where it adds to or differs from the final protocol.

## 1.2 Objectives

### Primary Objectives

- **P1 Objective:** Compare edoxaban-based versus VKA-based regimens for net adverse clinical events (NACE: all-cause death, myocardial infarction [MI], ischemic stroke, systemic thromboembolism [SEE], valve thrombosis, or ISTH major bleeding).
  - **P1 Outcome:** NACE: all-cause death, MI, ischemic stroke, SEE, valve thrombosis, or ISTH major bleeding, comparing edoxaban-based versus VKA-based regimens.
- **P2 Objective:** Compare the regimens for ISTH major bleeding.
  - **P2 Outcome:** ISTH major bleeding, comparing the regimens.

### Secondary/Exploratory Objectives

**Secondary**

- **S1 Objective:** Compare alternative NACE definitions (TIMI, BARC, GUSTO), MACE, MACCE, cardiovascular mortality, stroke subtypes, SEE, MI, valve thrombosis, and repeat target-lesion revascularization.
  - **S1 Outcome:** Comparison of alternative NACE definitions (TIMI, BARC, GUSTO), MACE, MACCE, cardiovascular mortality, stroke subtypes, SEE, MI, valve thrombosis, and repeat target-lesion revascularization.
- **S2 Objective:** Compare ISTH, TIMI, BARC and GUSTO bleeding endpoints, intracranial/life-threatening/fatal bleeding, mortality, adverse events (AEs), laboratory parameters, ECG and vital signs.
  - **S2 Outcome:** Comparison of ISTH, TIMI, BARC and GUSTO bleeding endpoints, intracranial/life-threatening/fatal bleeding, mortality, AEs, laboratory parameters, ECG and vital signs.

**Exploratory**

- **S3 Objective:** Compare cardiovascular hospital admissions (≥24 hours), length of stay, ICU days, PACT-Q treatment satisfaction, EQ-5D-5L quality of life, and optional biomarkers.
  - **S3 Outcome:** Comparison of cardiovascular hospital admissions (≥24 hours), length of stay, ICU days, PACT-Q treatment satisfaction, EQ-5D-5L quality of life, and optional biomarkers.

## 2.1 Trial Design

### Design Type

Multinational, multicentre, prospective, randomized, open-label, blinded endpoint evaluation (PROBE), parallel-group phase 3b trial. An independent CEC adjudicates events and an independent DSMB monitors safety.

### Allocation Ratio

1:1 edoxaban-based regimen:VKA-based regimen.

### Intervention Summary

Randomized treatment starts within 24 hours of randomization and lasts up to 36 months (minimum 12 months, except last enrolled subjects minimum 6 months), followed by 30 days' observational follow-up.

- **Edoxaban-based regimen:** Edoxaban 60 mg once daily, or 30 mg once daily when dose-reduction criteria in the locally approved label are met.
- **VKA-based regimen:** Locally approved VKA, investigator dose-adjusted to INR 2.0–3.0; in Japan, 1.6–2.6 for age ≥70 and 2.0–3.0 for age <70.
- **Antiplatelet co-therapy:** Predeclared at randomization; no APT or SAPT up to 90 days without another indication; SAPT indefinitely after PCI, with DAPT only up to 3 months after each stent; ASA 75–100 mg/day or clopidogrel 75 mg/day preferred.

## 2.2 Randomization

**Method&nbsp;**

IXRS assigns a unique identifier and treatment; an independent biostatistician generates the schedule under IXRS-vendor oversight. No crossover is permitted.

- **Stratification factors:** TAVI procedure with stenting (yes/no) and need for edoxaban dose adjustment under local label (yes/no).
- **Blocking/minimization:** The schedule contains a randomization block number. Block size and minimization method: Not specified.

## 2.3 Sample Size

### Planned Sample Size

- **Planned enrollment:** Approximately 1400 randomized participants, event-driven.
- **Outcome used for calculation:** NACE, a composite of all-cause death, MI, ischemic stroke, SEE, valve thrombosis, and ISTH major bleeding.
- **Outcome type:** Time-to-first composite event/hazard ratio.
- **Assumed control-arm value:** VKA annual NACE rate 14%.
- **Assumed treatment-arm value:** Assumed observed hazard ratio 0.95.
- **Noninferiority margin:** HR 1.38.
- **Variability assumption:** Not specified. The event-driven calculation reports an annual rate and anticipated median follow-up of two years.
- **Alpha:** 0.05.
- **Power:** 80% for NACE noninferiority.
- **Statistical test assumed:** Noninferiority assessment of the HR using the upper bound of a two-sided 95% CI against 1.38.
- **Required events:** At least 320 NACE events across both arms.
- **Dropout/non-adherence inflation:** Numerical inflation: Not specified. Sample size or follow-up duration may be adjusted for actual versus expected endpoint and dropout rates.
- **Design effect/clustering:** Not applicable: individually randomized parallel-group design.
- **Basis for assumptions:** Published literature and ENGAGE AF-TIMI 48; Executive Committee review for margin acceptability.
- **Sample size re-estimation/amendments:** Possible adjustment of sample size or follow-up based on observed endpoint and dropout rates.
- **Software:** Not specified.
- **Rationale:** With a 14% annual control event rate and two-year median follow-up, approximately 1400 participants yield at least 320 NACE events for 80% power.

## 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

Four confirmatory hypotheses are tested sequentially:

1. NACE noninferiority.
2. ISTH major-bleeding noninferiority.
3. ISTH major-bleeding superiority.
4. NACE superiority.

Steps 1–2 require the upper bound of the two-sided 95% CI for the HR to be <1.38. Steps 3–4 require the upper bound to be <1.00. Failure stops the sequence.

## 2.5 Timing of Analyses

- **Final analysis timing:** Main endpoint analysis is ITT over the overall study period: randomization (or first dose for non-ITT sets) to Month 36/EOT, last visit, or death, whichever occurs first. Incomplete follow-up is censored at last known status.
- **Data cutoff/database lock and data readiness:** Not specified.
- **Assessment schedule:** Baseline/randomization; Months 3, 6, 12, 18, 24, 30 and EOT/Month 36 (±14 days); post-treatment follow-up at one month (±7 days); then 6-monthly follow-up contacts.
- **PACT-Q/EQ-5D-5L assessments:** Baseline, M3, and M12 or EOT, whichever first.

## 3.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

### Inclusion Criteria

- Adults with successful TAVI: correct single-valve position; gradient <20 mmHg, peak velocity <3.0 m/s, regurgitation ≤2; no overt stroke/uncontrolled bleeding.
- Pre-existing or new AF (>30 seconds ECG) requiring chronic OAC.

### Exclusion Criteria

- High bleeding risk.
- Contraindication to either treatment.
- Prolonged DAPT requirement.
- Recent stroke, MI, or major surgery.
- Mechanical valve.
- Severe renal/hepatic disease.
- Instability.
- Active malignancy.
- Specified laboratory abnormalities.
- Pregnancy.
- Investigator-assessed inability to comply.
- Prior randomization.
- Substance dependence.
- Life expectancy <6 months.
