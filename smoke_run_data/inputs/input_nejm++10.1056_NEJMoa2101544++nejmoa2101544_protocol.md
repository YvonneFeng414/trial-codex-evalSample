# Evaluation Input

## 1.1 Background and Rationale

ENSEMBLE (VAC31518COV3001) is a phase 3 study of Ad26.COV2.S, a recombinant replication-incompetent adenovirus type 26 vector encoding SARS-CoV-2 spike protein, to assess prevention of SARS-CoV-2-mediated COVID-19 in adults aged 18 years or older.

## 1.2 Objectives

### Primary Objectives

- **P1 Objective:** Demonstrate efficacy of Ad26.COV2.S versus placebo in preventing first molecularly confirmed moderate-to-severe/critical COVID-19 in SARS-CoV-2-seronegative adults, with onset at least Day 15 and, separately, at least Day 29.
  - **P1 Outcome:** First molecularly confirmed moderate-to-severe/critical COVID-19 with onset at least Day 15 and, separately, at least Day 29, comparing Ad26.COV2.S versus placebo.

### Secondary/Exploratory Objectives

- **S1 Objective:** Evaluate prevention of severe/critical COVID-19; moderate-to-severe/critical COVID-19 regardless of baseline serostatus; disease from Day 2; COVID-19 requiring medical intervention; viral RNA load; mild COVID-19; FDA-harmonized COVID-19; burden of symptomatic disease; asymptomatic/undetected infection; and all SARS-CoV-2 infection.
  - **S1 Outcome:** Severe/critical COVID-19; moderate-to-severe/critical COVID-19 regardless of baseline serostatus; disease from Day 2; COVID-19 requiring medical intervention; viral RNA load; mild COVID-19; FDA-harmonized COVID-19; burden of symptomatic disease; asymptomatic/undetected infection; and all SARS-CoV-2 infection.
- **S2 Objective:** Evaluate SAEs, MAAEs, MAAEs leading to discontinuation, reactogenicity, unsolicited AEs, and immunogenicity.
  - **S2 Outcome:** SAEs, MAAEs, MAAEs leading to discontinuation, reactogenicity, unsolicited AEs, and immunogenicity.
- **S3 Objective (Exploratory):** Explore viral load, immune correlates, functional/molecular antibody characteristics, transcriptomics, patient-reported symptoms, frailty, and clinical disease severity.
  - **S3 Outcome:** Viral load, immune correlates, functional/molecular antibody characteristics, transcriptomics, patient-reported symptoms, frailty, and clinical disease severity.

## 2.1 Trial Design

### Design Type

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 3 study in adults aged ≥18 years.

### Allocation Ratio

1:1 Ad26.COV2.S:placebo.

### Intervention Summary

One Day-1 0.5-mL injection:
- Group 1: Ad26.COV2.S 5×10^10 viral particles, supplied at 1×10^11 vp/mL.
- Group 2: 0.9% sodium chloride placebo.

## 2.2 Randomization

**Method&nbsp;**

Central computer-generated randomization schedule prepared before study under sponsor supervision, using randomly permuted blocks; no minimization reported.

Stratification factors: vaccination unit (site/mobile unit), age (≥18 to <60; ≥60 years), and absence/presence of comorbidities associated or potentially associated with progression to severe COVID-19.

## 2.3 Sample Size

### Planned Sample Size

- **After Protocol Amendment 3:** Approximately 40,000 participants, reduced based on observed/modelled incidence.
- **Previous maximum:** Approximately 60,000 participants.
- **Calculation outcome:** First molecularly confirmed moderate-to-severe/critical COVID-19, separately onset ≥Day 15 and ≥Day 29, in PP participants.
- **Outcome type:** Time-to-first-event/incident case endpoint.
- **Assumed control-arm value:** Null VE 30%.
- **Assumed treatment-arm value:** VE 60%.
- **Target effect:** VE 60% versus null lower bound 30%.
- **Variability assumption:** Not reported as a continuous-outcome variability parameter; event allocation uses an exact conditional binomial/SPRT framework.
- **Significance level:** One-sided 2.5%.
- **Power:** Approximately 90% planned; 86.22% with the truncated SPRT through 154 events at VE 60%.
- **Statistical test assumed:** Truncated sequential probability ratio test, conditional exact binomial framework, beginning at the 20th event.
- **Required events:** 154 total co-primary events.
- **Non-inferiority/equivalence margin:** Not applicable; superiority hypotheses test VE >30% for co-primary endpoints.
- **Dropout/non-adherence inflation:** No numerical inflation reported; epidemiologic uncertainty and potential dropouts informed maximum enrollment.
- **Design effect/clustering:** Not applicable; individually randomized trial.
- **Assumption basis:** 1.4% moderate/severe incidence for the first 3 months, 50% reduction in month 4, 62% reduction thereafter, and 10% seroprevalence. Later epidemiologic modeling supported reduction to approximately 40,000.
- **Software:** gsDesign version 3.1.1 for SPRT boundary code; other sample-size software not specified.
- **Rationale:** Enrollment supports high probability of an efficacy signal within 8 months and sufficient likelihood of five placebo severe cases.

## 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

Co-primary hypotheses are H0: VE≤30% versus H1: VE>30%, each at one-sided 2.5%. Success requires both co-primary tests and, for each timing window, severe/critical VE point estimate ≥50% with at least five placebo severe/critical events.

Conditional on primary success, confirmatory secondary tests use H0: VE≤0% versus H1: VE>0% under graphical multiple-testing control.

The graphical sequence evaluates BOD, medical intervention, all infection, and asymptomatic/undetected infection, subject to their endpoint/population windows. Secondary alpha is based on a single-interim Pocock/Wang–Tsiatis Δ=.5 information fraction derived from Day-29 primary events (154 target). The medical-intervention rationale uses 23 events; all-infection testing requires at least 15,000 available Day-71 samples; asymptomatic testing requires all participants to have ≥6-month follow-up.

## 2.5 Timing of Analyses

- Final analysis follows the last participant’s month-12 visit or earlier discontinuation.
- End-of-study analysis follows all month-24 visits or earlier discontinuation.
- eCOA symptom surveillance occurs at least twice weekly until the later of year 1 or primary analysis, then every 2 weeks. Symptomatic episodes trigger serial testing/procedures.
- Immunogenicity Subset sampling occurs at baseline, Day 29, Day 71, month 6, year 1, month 18, and year 2.
- Asymptomatic-infection serology occurs at baseline, Day 71, month 6, and year 1.
- Co-primary efficacy windows are Day 15 and Day 29.
- A signal before 8-week median follow-up triggers an analysis at that median-follow-up timepoint.
- Data cutoff/database-lock requirements: Not specified.

## 3.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

### Inclusion Criteria

- Adults aged ≥18 years who provide informed consent and can comply with study procedures.
- Stable/well-controlled illness and stable medication dose for ≥12 weeks, where applicable.
- For those of childbearing potential, protocol-compliant contraception from consent through 3 months after vaccination; hormonal contraception initiated ≥28 days before vaccination.
- Negative highly sensitive urine pregnancy tests at screening and immediately before vaccination.
- No blood/bone-marrow donation through 3 months after vaccination.
- Verifiable identity/contactability.
- Ability to complete eCOA questionnaires.

### Exclusion Criteria

- Acute clinically significant illness or temperature ≥38.0°C within 24 hours.
- Serious vaccine/excipient allergy/anaphylaxis.
- Immunologically relevant autoimmune disease/immunodeficiency; chronic/recurrent systemic corticosteroids; antineoplastic/immunomodulating therapy or radiotherapy.
- Immunoglobulin within 3 months or blood products within 4 months.
- Live vaccine within 28 days or non-live vaccine within 14 days.
- Prior coronavirus vaccine.
- Recent investigational drug/device/vaccine.
- Pregnancy, breastfeeding, or pregnancy plan.
- Condition compromising safety/assessments.
- Contraindication to IM injection/blood draw.
- Uncontrolled HIV.
- Serious psychiatric illness/substance abuse.
- Inability to communicate/adhere.
- Stage-specific high-risk comorbidities, recent malignancy, acute polyneuropathy, surgery, or active hepatitis.

Ocular/topical/inhaled steroids and stable non-immunomodulator treatment may be permitted; smoking is not a comorbidity.
