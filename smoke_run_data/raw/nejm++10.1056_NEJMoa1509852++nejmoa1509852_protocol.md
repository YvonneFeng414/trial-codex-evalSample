# Statistical Analysis Plan

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Background and Rationale

Noninfectious intermediate-, posterior-, and pan-uveitis may threaten vision, while sustained corticosteroid exposure has ocular and systemic risks. This final analysis specification evaluates steroid-sparing maintenance adalimumab against placebo in active disease. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Sections 3–4 and 8, pages 195-211 and 282; SAP Amendment 3 Sections 3–4, pages 520-521)*

### 1.2 Objectives

**Primary Objectives**

- Evaluate efficacy and safety of adalimumab (80 mg SC at Baseline, then 40 mg SC every other week from Week 1) versus placebo as maintenance therapy in subjects requiring high-dose corticosteroids for active noninfectious intermediate-, posterior-, or pan-uveitis. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 8, page 282; SAP Amendment 3 Section 4.1, pages 520-521)*

**Secondary Objectives**

- The protocol does not separately label secondary objectives; it defines ranked secondary efficacy endpoints and their confirmatory testing sequence. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 8.1.4, pages 284-285; SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, pages 536-539)*

**Exploratory Objectives**

- The protocol does not separately label exploratory objectives; other efficacy, sensitivity, and subgroup analyses are specified as exploratory analyses. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Sections 11.1 and 11.6-11.7, pages 535 and 540-544)*

## 2. Study Methods

### 2.1 Trial Design

- **Design Type:** Phase 3, randomized, double-masked, placebo-controlled, multicenter parent study; analyses cover the non-Japan parent study, with a separate Japan sub-study/SAP. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Sections 5.1 and 8, pages 210-212 and 282; SAP Amendment 3 Section 3, page 520)*
- **Allocation Ratio:** 1:1. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 8.3, page 286)*
- **Intervention Summary:** All continuing subjects received a standardized 60-mg/day prednisone burst at entry with mandatory taper/discontinuation by Week 15; topical corticosteroids were tapered per protocol. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Sections 5.1 and 6, pages 210-212 and 251-252)*
  - Arm 1: adalimumab 80 mg SC loading dose at Baseline, then 40 mg SC every other week beginning Week 1.
  - Arm 2: matching placebo loading and every-other-week doses. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 4.2, page 521)*

### 2.2 Randomization

- **Method:** Eligible subjects were randomized by IVRS/IWRS; the AbbVie Deutschland Statistics Department prepared the schedule. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 8.3, page 286)*
- **Stratification Factors:** Baseline immunosuppressant (IMM) usage; Japanese subjects were randomized in a separate stratum without IMM stratification. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 5.2, page 523)*
- **Minimization or Blocking:** Randomization was not stratified by site; an adequate block size was used. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 8.3, page 286)*

### 2.3 Sample Size

- **Planned Sample Size:** Approximately 234 patients to obtain 138 treatment-failure events. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 8.2, page 286; SAP Amendment 3 Section 4.3, page 522)*
- **Outcome Used for Calculation:** Time to treatment failure at 6 months. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 8.2, page 286)*
- **Outcome Type:** Time-to-event treatment failure. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Sections 8.1.4 and 8.2, pages 284 and 286)*
- **Assumed Control-Arm Value:** 70% placebo treatment-failure rate at 6 months. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 8.2, page 286)*
- **Assumed Treatment-Arm Value:** 50% adalimumab treatment-failure rate at 6 months. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 8.2, page 286)*
- **Target Difference / Effect Size:** Absolute 20-percentage-point difference in assumed 6-month failure rates. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 8.2, page 286)*
- **Variability Assumption:** Failures were conservatively assumed to begin after 2 months as prednisone reached lower doses. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 8.2, page 286)*
- **Alpha (Significance Level):** Two-sided 5%. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 8.2, page 286)*
- **Power:** 90%. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 8.2, page 286)*
- **Statistical Test Assumed:** Log-rank test. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 8.2, page 286)*
- **Required Number of Events:** 138 treatment-failure events. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 8.2, page 286)*
- **Non-Inferiority / Equivalence Margin:** Not applicable: superiority comparison. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Sections 8.1.4 and 8.2, pages 284 and 286)*
- **Dropout / Non-Adherence Inflation:** Pooled 35% dropout over 12 months. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 8.2, page 286)*
- **Design Effect / Clustering:** Not applicable to the individually randomized sample-size calculation. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 8.2, page 286)*
- **Basis for Assumptions:** Event, recruitment, and dropout assumptions; accrual averaged 4 subjects/month for first 30 months then 7/month. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 8.2, page 286)*
- **Sample Size Re-Estimation / Amendments:** Not reported in protocol. *(Source: pages 1-565 reviewed)*
- **Software Used for Calculation:** East5 Version 5.2.0.0. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 8.2, page 286)*
- **Sample Size Rationale:** Calculations across sample sizes using stated assumptions indicated approximately 234 patients to achieve 138 events. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 8.2, page 286)*

### 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

Primary time-to-treatment-failure and ranked secondary endpoints are tested two-sided at 0.05. Ranked-secondary testing begins only if the primary endpoint is significant and proceeds in the specified hierarchy; each preceding endpoint must be significant to test the next, controlling the two-sided 5% multiple significance level. Other, sensitivity, and subgroup analyses are exploratory. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Sections 8.1.2 and 8.1.4, pages 283-285; SAP Amendment 3 Sections 11.1 and 11.4, pages 535 and 539)*

### 2.5 Statistical Interim Analyses

- **Interim Analyses Planned:** No confirmatory efficacy interim analysis. The IDMC reviews cumulative safety approximately every 6 months or at a frequency it determines. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 4.4, pages 522-523)*
- **Stopping Guidelines:** The IDMC may recommend termination or continuation of the study or an amendment because of safety review. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 4.4, pages 522-523)*
- **Alpha Spending or Adjustment:** Not applicable: no confirmatory efficacy interim analysis. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 4.4, page 522)*

### 2.6 Timing of Analyses

- **Final Analysis Timing:** Final efficacy value is the treatment-failure visit, Early Termination Visit, or Week 80 Visit if no failure; analyses occur after parent-study termination. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Sections 3 and 6.1, pages 520 and 524)*
- **Outcome Assessment Schedule:** Baseline, Week 1, Week 4, then every 4 weeks with Week 27 replacing Week 28; treatment failure is assessed at Week 6 and later visits. Analysis windows run through Week 80 by Rx day; closest assessment to nominal day is used, ties use latest. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 5.1, pages 211-212; SAP Amendment 3 Sections 6.2 and 11.2, pages 524-525 and 535)*

## 3. Statistical Principles

### 3.1 Significance and Confidence Intervals

- **Alpha Level:** All statistical tests are two-sided at 0.05; P values are rounded to three decimals. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 8.1.2, page 283; SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.1, page 535)*
- **Multiplicity Adjustment:** Hierarchical gatekeeping for ranked secondary endpoints after significant primary result controls the two-sided 5% multiple significance level. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 8.1.4, pages 284-285; SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, page 539)*
- **Confidence Intervals:** For efficacy, 95% CIs accompany treatment-failure rates, hazard ratios, parameter estimates and binary proportion differences; 95% CIs also accompany laboratory and vital-sign between-group mean changes. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Sections 11.2 and 11.4-11.6, pages 536 and 538-543; Sections 12.2 and 12.5, pages 553 and 555-556)*

### 3.2 Protocol Deviations

- **Adherence Definition:** Prednisone compliance is total received dose/total planned dose; study-drug compliance is injections received/injections planned. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Sections 9-10, pages 533-534)*
- **Adherence Assessment:** Oral-prednisone duration is last-dose date minus first-dose date plus 1; weekly mean daily dose, duration, total dose and categorized duration are summarized. Corticosteroid eye-drop tapering is analyzed analogously. Study-drug exposure is last-dose date minus first-dose date plus 14 days. These exposure/compliance summaries use n, mean, SD, minimum, quartiles, median and maximum in the safety set. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Sections 9-10, pages 533-534)*
- **Protocol Deviation Definition:** Reportable deviations are enrollment despite unmet entry criteria, failure to withdraw after withdrawal criteria, wrong treatment/incorrect dose, or excluded/prohibited concomitant treatment. Failure to complete final/early termination within 14 days after confirmed treatment failure is also a deviation. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 7, page 282)*
- **Protocol Deviation Reporting:** Counts and percentages with deviations are displayed by treatment group/overall in ITT; wrong treatment/incorrect prednisone dose is a prespecified subgroup factor. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Sections 8 and 11.7, pages 533 and 544)*

### 3.3 Analysis Populations

- **Intention-to-Treat:** ITT is randomized non-Japan subjects excluding Subject 1050205 at Brezin site 39656 for incomplete efficacy source data and Subjects 1202803, 1202804, 1202806, 1202807, and 1202808 at Flynn site 39626 for general compliance issues. Primary efficacy analysis uses ITT, analyzed as randomized. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 5.1, page 523; Section 11.2, page 535)*
- **Per Protocol:** No per-protocol analysis will be done. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 8.1.1, page 283)*
- **Other Populations:** mITT comprises all randomized non-Japan subjects, analyzed as randomized for sensitivity analyses. Safety set comprises non-Japan subjects receiving >=1 study-medication dose, analyzed as treated. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 5.1, page 523)*

## 4. Trial Population

### 4.1 Screening Data

Screening lasts 2–30 days; subjects not eligible may be rescreened once before Baseline. Screening includes consent, eligibility, medical/surgical history, examinations, vital signs, ECG, chest radiograph/TB testing, pregnancy testing, ophthalmic assessments and laboratory testing. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Sections 5.1 and 5.3, pages 211-212 and 239-250)*

### 4.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

Inclusion required age >=18 years; noninfectious intermediate-, posterior-, or pan-uveitis; active disease in either eye despite >=2 weeks of stable prednisone >=10 mg/day (or equivalent), defined by active chorioretinal/retinal vascular lesion, OCT macular edema with intraocular inflammation, AC cells >=2+, or vitreous haze >=2+; appropriate contraception/negative pregnancy tests, screening health evaluation (history, laboratory profile, physical examination, chest radiograph and ECG), injection capability, consent/compliance, and TB testing/therapy conditions. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 5.2.1, pages 213-215)*

Exclusions included isolated anterior or infectious uveitis, inadequate response/intolerance to high-dose steroids; POHS, ocular masquerade/serpiginous disease, pupil-dilation limitation, fundus-obscuring opacity/cataract need, specified glaucoma/BCVA/demyelination/retinopathy/macular disease/vitreoretinal-interface or recent/planned eye-surgery conditions; prior anti-TNF/biologic, cyclophosphamide/chlorambucil or steroid implant exposure; unstable, multiple, prohibited, or excessive immunosuppression; local steroids within 90 days, recent investigational drugs, prohibited infections/TB/hepatitis B/HIV/immunodeficiency or anti-infective treatment, positive syphilis/Lyme tests, selected cardiovascular/neurologic history, hypersensitivity, pregnancy/breastfeeding, malignancy, drug/alcohol abuse, significant screening laboratory abnormalities, and investigator unsuitability. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 5.2.2, pages 215-224)*

### 4.3 Recruitment

Approximately 234 non-Japan patients were anticipated under the final sample-size plan; the protocol describes approximately 60 parent-study sites in North America, Europe, Israel and Australia, with a separate Japanese sub-study. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Sections 5.2 and 8.2, pages 213 and 286)*

### 4.4 Withdrawal and Follow-up

- **Withdrawal Levels:** Subjects may withdraw at any time; investigators/sponsor may discontinue subjects/sites. Treatment failure, safety, eligibility, noncompliance and other stated withdrawal conditions trigger early termination as applicable. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Sections 5.1 and 6, pages 211-212 and 277-281)*
- **Withdrawal Data Collection:** Final/Early Termination visit is performed for treatment failure or other discontinuation; the efficacy Final Visit is the failure visit, Early Termination Visit, or Week 80 Visit if no failure. Safety final observation is the last nonmissing observation on/before last dose plus 70 days. AEs are followed through 70 days after last injection; for rollover subjects, the TEAE window ends before first M11-327 dose. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Sections 5.1 and 6, pages 211-212 and 277-281; SAP Amendment 3 Sections 6.1 and 12.1, pages 524 and 545)*
- **Reasons for Withdrawal:** Premature study-drug discontinuation is summarized by reason overall and by treatment group through Week 6. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 8, page 533)*

### 4.5 Baseline Characteristics

- **Baseline Variables:** Demographics include age and two age-category schemes, sex, race, ethnicity, weight and height; tobacco/alcohol; TB/PPD/QuantiFERON/TB prophylaxis, chest-radiograph, vital-sign and ECG variables; VFQ-25, EQ-5D, HADS, WPAI:SHP and SF-36 with components. Eye-specific variables include OCT edema/CRT, IOP, lesions, AC cells, lens opacity, vitreous haze, logMAR BCVA and refraction. Uveitis duration/type/diagnosis/affected eye/infectious history/flares, medical history, and specified prior/concomitant immunosuppressant/corticosteroid medicines are summarized. No statistical tests are planned for ethnicity, TB/chest-radiograph/ECG and selected categorical ophthalmic variables. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Sections 7.1-7.5, pages 527-532)*
- **Summary Methods:** ITT treatment-group and overall summaries: continuous n, mean, SD, minimum, quartiles, median, maximum; categorical counts/percentages. ANOVA/repeated-measures ANOVA for continuous variables and chi-square/Fisher tests for categorical variables, with clustered-eye adjustment where applicable. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Sections 7 and 7.1-7.2, pages 527-531)*

## 5. Analysis

### 5.1 Primary Outcomes

**Outcome: Time to treatment failure on or after Week 6** *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 8.1.4, page 284; SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.2, pages 535-536)*
- **Definition:** Failure is >=1 criterion in either eye: new active inflammatory chorioretinal/retinal vascular lesions; at Week 6 inability to achieve AC cells <=0.5+ or vitreous haze <=0.5+, and later 2-step increase from best state; or BCVA worsening >=15 ETDRS letters from best state. A two-step increase is 0 to >=2+ or 0.5+ to >=3+. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.2, page 535)*
- **Measurement Units:** Time from first study-drug dose to failure; AC cells/vitreous haze grades and ETDRS letters are endpoint components. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Sections 6.2 and 11.2, pages 524-525 and 535)*
- **Timing of Measurement:** Week 6 and all subsequent scheduled assessments through Week 80/final visit. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Sections 6.2 and 11.2, pages 524-525 and 535)*
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Kaplan–Meier failure rates at 3, 6, 9, 12 and 18 months; failures are events and non-failure dropouts are censored at dropout. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.2, page 536)*

Each of the four treatment-failure components and the number of components met are summarized by counts and percentages. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.2, page 536)*

### 5.2 Secondary Outcomes

Ranked secondary endpoints are part of the primary analysis in the ITT set. For continuous outcomes, descriptive summaries include n, mean, SD, minimum, quartiles, median and maximum at each visit, change from best pre-Week-6 state to each later visit and to Final/Early Termination; data are analyzed by LOCF and as observed. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Sections 11.1 and 11.4, pages 535 and 537-538)*

**Outcome: Change in anterior chamber cell grade** *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, pages 536-538)*
- **Definition:** Ranked endpoint 1: each-eye change from best state achieved before Week 6 to Final/Early Termination Visit. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, pages 536-538)*
- **Measurement Units:** SUN AC-cell grade. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Sections 7.1 and 11.4, pages 529 and 536-538)*
- **Timing of Measurement:** Best pre-Week-6 state versus final/early termination; values at Week 6 and later visits. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, pages 536-538)*
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Change from best pre-Week-6 state; values also summarized categorically. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, page 538)*

**Outcome: Change in vitreous haze grade** *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, pages 537-538)*
- **Definition:** Ranked endpoint 2: each-eye change from best pre-Week-6 state to Final/Early Termination Visit. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, pages 537-538)*
- **Measurement Units:** NEI/SUN vitreous-haze grade. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Sections 7.1 and 11.4, pages 529 and 537-538)*
- **Timing of Measurement:** Best pre-Week-6 state versus final/early termination; values at Week 6 and later visits. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, pages 537-538)*
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Change from best pre-Week-6 state; values also summarized categorically. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, page 538)*

**Outcome: Change in logMAR BCVA** *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, pages 537-538)*
- **Definition:** Ranked endpoint 3: each-eye change from best pre-Week-6 state to Final/Early Termination Visit. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, pages 537-538)*
- **Measurement Units:** logMAR BCVA derived from ETDRS letters; special finger-counting/hand-movement rules apply. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 7.1, pages 529-530)*
- **Timing of Measurement:** Best pre-Week-6 state versus final/early termination; values at Week 6 and later visits. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, pages 537-538)*
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Change categories: worsening >=15, 14–10, 9–5 letters; -4 worsening to +4 improvement; or improvement >=5 letters. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, page 538)*

**Outcome: Time to OCT evidence of macular edema** *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, pages 537-539)*
- **Definition:** Ranked endpoint 4: first OCT evidence of macular edema in >=1 eye on or after Week 6, based on central-reader assessment. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, page 537)*
- **Measurement Units:** Time-to-event. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, pages 538-539)*
- **Timing of Measurement:** On/after Week 6 through final assessment. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, pages 537-539)*
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Event for OCT edema; other dropouts censored at dropout; Kaplan–Meier estimates/curves. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, pages 538-539)*

**Outcome: Percent change in central retinal thickness (CRT)** *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, pages 537-538)*
- **Definition:** Ranked endpoint 5: each-eye percent change from best pre-Week-6 state to Final/Early Termination Visit. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, pages 537-538)*
- **Measurement Units:** Microns and percent change. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Sections 7.1 and 11.4, pages 529-530 and 537-538)*
- **Timing of Measurement:** Best pre-Week-6 state versus final/early termination; Week 6 onward visits. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, pages 537-538)*
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Central-reader map-C center thickness if reliable, otherwise manual-center mean; only same-machine changes/percent changes; analyses adjusted for OCT machine. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Sections 7.1 and 11.4, pages 530 and 537-538)*

**Outcome: VFQ-25 total, distance-vision, near-vision, and ocular-pain change** *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, pages 537-539)*
- **Definition:** Ranked endpoints 6–9: change in total score then distance-vision, near-vision, and ocular-pain subscores from best pre-Week-6 state to Final/Early Termination Visit. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, pages 537-539)*
- **Measurement Units:** VFQ-25 total score and named subscores. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Sections 7.1 and 11.4, pages 529 and 537)*
- **Timing of Measurement:** Best pre-Week-6 state versus final/early termination; Week 6 onward visits. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, pages 537-538)*
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Change from best pre-Week-6 state. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.4, pages 537-538)*

### 5.3 Analysis Methods

- **Primary Analysis Methods:** ITT log-rank comparison and treatment-only proportional-hazards model; Kaplan–Meier curves and 3/6/9/12/18-month rates. Ocular ranked continuous endpoints use clustered-eye ANOVA with treatment factor (CRT also OCT-machine adjusted); VFQ uses ANOVA; OCT edema uses treatment-only PH/log-rank. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Sections 11.2 and 11.4, pages 535-539)*
- **Covariate Adjustment:** Sensitivity PH model includes treatment and baseline IMM; ranked continuous/OCT sensitivity models add IMM, and CRT models additionally adjust for OCT machine. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Sections 11.3 and 11.5, pages 536 and 539-540)*
- **Assumption Checks:** Not reported in protocol. *(Source: pages 1-565 reviewed)*
- **Alternative Methods if Assumptions Fail:** Not reported in protocol. *(Source: pages 1-565 reviewed)*
- **Sensitivity Analyses:** Primary ITT analysis adds baseline IMM to the PH model and reports model HR/95% CI/P value; mITT repeats KM curves/rates and treatment-only PH/log-rank. In ITT, ranked analyses add IMM (and OCT machine for CRT); separate treatment-only ANOVAs analyze worse and better eyes. mITT repeats ranked-secondary methods, including Week-6 dropout exclusions, LOCF and OCT-edema censoring. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Sections 11.3 and 11.5, pages 536 and 539-540)*
- **Subgroup Analyses:** Primary/ranked secondary analyses by sex, age, race, uveitis duration/type/diagnosis/flares, flare timing/dose/duration, baseline OCT edema/IMM/topical corticosteroids, prednisone-dose deviation, and geographic region; no center analysis. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.7, page 544)*

### 5.4 Missing Data

- **Missing Data Assumptions:** Binary missing values are nonresponse under NRI; observed-case analysis uses no missing values. No MAR/MNAR assumption is reported. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 6.3, page 526; pages 1-565 reviewed)*
- **Handling Methods:** Continuous/rating efficacy variables use LOCF without using baseline for post-baseline imputation and carry forward latest nonmissing post-Day-1 value; ranked endpoints exclude Week-6 dropouts and have both LOCF and separate observed analyses. Time-to-failure/OCT outcomes censor non-event dropout at dropout. Other binary endpoints set missing values and values after premature discontinuation or treatment failure to nonresponse under NRI, and exclude subjects who terminate because the planned failure count was reached; HRU is observed only. Missing dates use 01/start day, end-of-month/end day, January 1/start month, and December 31/end month. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Sections 6.3 and 11.4-11.6, pages 526 and 538-543)*

### 5.5 Additional Analyses

Other efficacy analyses include observed-case AUC from worst possible value to discontinuation for AC cells (grade 4), vitreous haze (grade 4), logMAR BCVA (3.0) and VFQ-25 total (0), excluding subjects who terminate because planned failure count was reached. Ocular AUCs use clustered-eye ANOVA with treatment; VFQ AUC and the remaining VFQ subscores, HADS, WPAI:SHP and EQ-5D changes use treatment-factor ANOVA with parameter estimate/95% CI/P value. Component-specific time-to-failure analyses include baseline-restricted lesion/AC-cell/vitreous-haze analyses; quiescence/lack-of-inflammation proportions are assessed Weeks 6–52 and steroid-free versions Weeks 16–52. Binary NRI also applies post-discontinuation/failure and excludes planned-event-count termination; chi-square compares proportions with 95% CI for difference. HRU is observed only: total postbaseline utilization divided by total time from Baseline to last visit with nonmissing HRU. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 11.6, pages 540-543)*

### 5.6 Harms

- **Adverse Event Definitions:** TEAEs start on/after first study dose through 70 days after last dose, or before extension first dose for rollover subjects. Incomplete onset dates are assumed TEAE unless contrary evidence; worsening severity is a new AE. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 10 Section 8.1.5, page 285; SAP Amendment 3 Section 12.1, page 545)*
- **Safety Analysis Methods:** Safety-set overview includes all TEAEs, study-drug/prednisone related AEs, severe/serious AEs, related SAEs, discontinuation/death, investigator/coded uveitis-related and special-interest AEs. Current MedDRA SOC/PT summaries use alphabetical SOC/PT ordering and decreasing-frequency listings for adalimumab incidence >=5% and SAEs; each subject counts once per PT/SOC/overall, using most severe/related event where applicable. Fisher tests display only P<=.100 (3 decimals); pre-treatment SAEs are listed separately. AE rate/100 subject-years = total reported AEs divided by total exposed days/365.25, times 100. Prespecified AE subgroups are sex, age, uveitis type, baseline OCT edema/IMM and named baseline IMM drugs. Laboratories/vitals use descriptive/shift/ANOVA methods with 95% CIs; CTC/liver listings and ocular IOP/lens-opacity shift categories including two-step increase are specified. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Sections 12.1-12.6, pages 545-556)*
  - Uveitis-related AEs: corneal-transparency loss, band keratopathy, synechiae, cataracts, glaucoma/increased IOP, vitreous hemorrhage, macular edema, retinal detachment, epiretinal membrane, vitreomacular traction, retinal ischemia, vision loss and hypotony. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Section 12.1, page 546)*
  - Biologic special-interest AEs: infections (including serious, Legionella, opportunistic, oral candidiasis, TB and parasitic infection), hepatitis-B reactivation/PML; malignancies (lymphoma, HSTCL, NMSC, melanoma, leukemia and other); immune reactions (allergy/angioedema/anaphylaxis, lupus/SLE, vasculitis, sarcoidosis, autoimmune hepatitis); MI/CVA/CHF/pulmonary embolism; interstitial lung disease; perforation/pancreatitis; Stevens-Johnson/erythema multiforme/psoriasis; demyelinating disorders/ALS/RPLS; hematologic disorders; hepatic events; administration medication errors and injection-site reactions. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Sections 12.1, pages 547-549)*
  - Laboratory analyses cover hematocrit, hemoglobin, RBC/WBC/differentials/platelets; BUN, creatinine, bilirubin, ALT/AST, alkaline phosphatase, electrolytes, calcium/phosphorus, uric acid, cholesterol/protein/glucose/triglycerides/albumin; and urinalysis specific gravity/pH. Low/normal/high baseline-to-final shifts and CTC Grade 3/4 worst-value shifts are produced. CTC-selected analytes are hemoglobin, lymphocytes, neutrophils, platelets, WBC, albumin, alkaline phosphatase, ALT, AST, bilirubin, calcium, cholesterol, creatinine, glucose, phosphorus, potassium, sodium and triglycerides. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Sections 12.2-12.3, pages 552-554)*
  - Liver analyses classify ALT, AST, alkaline phosphatase and bilirubin as <1.5x, >=1.5 to <3x, >=3 to <5x, >=5 to <8x, or >=8x ULN, with baseline-to-minimum/maximum/final shifts and listing thresholds. Ocular-safety shifts use IOP 0–9, 10–21, 22–25, 26–30, >30 mmHg and nuclear/cortical/posterior-subcapsular lens-opacity grades <1.0, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 2.5, 3.0, >3.0; a two-step lens-opacity increase is <1.0 to >=2.5, 1.0 to >=3.0, or 1.5 to >3.0 in >=1 eye. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Sections 12.4-12.6, pages 555-556)*
- **Grading Scales and Causality:** Investigator relationship categories include probably/possibly related; unknown severity/relationship remains unknown unless another occurrence is severe or at least possibly related. CTC Version 3.0 Grades 3–4 define potentially clinically significant selected laboratory findings; liver listings use ALT/AST/alkaline phosphatase >=2.5x ULN or bilirubin >=1.5x ULN. *(Source: SAP Amendment 3 Sections 12.1, 12.3 and 12.4, pages 545-551 and 553-555)*
