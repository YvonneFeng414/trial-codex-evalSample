# Statistical Analysis Plan

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Background and Rationale
Empagliflozin (BI 10773) is evaluated in this Phase III programme to establish long-term cardiovascular safety in adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM), insufficient glycaemic control, and increased cardiovascular (CV) risk; the study is integral to ongoing CV safety assessment and regulatory submission. *(Source: CTP §§2.1–2.2, PDF-marker pages 25–26)*

### 1.2 Objectives

**Primary Objectives**

- Demonstrate non-inferiority of pooled empagliflozin 10 mg and 25 mg once daily versus placebo for time to first 3-point major adverse cardiovascular event (3P-MACE), using a hazard-ratio (HR) margin of 1.3. *(Source: CTP §2.2, PDF-marker page 25; CTP §7.2, pages 67–69)*

**Secondary Objectives**

- Assess the key secondary adjudicated 4-point composite (3P-MACE plus hospitalisation for unstable angina) within the confirmatory hierarchy. *(Source: CTP §§5.2.1,7.2, PDF-marker pages 45,67–69)*
- Assess occurrence/time to silent MI, heart-failure hospitalisation, new albuminuria, new macroalbuminuria, new/worsening nephropathy and the composite microvascular outcome. *(Source: CTP §5.2.1, PDF-marker pages 45–46)*
- Assess further CV events: CV death, fatal/nonfatal MI and stroke, unstable-angina hospitalisation, all-cause mortality, TIA and coronary revascularisation. *(Source: CTP §5.2.1, PDF-marker pages 45–46)*
- Assess diabetes-related efficacy: change from baseline in HbA1c, FPG, weight, waist and BP; HbA1c/SBP/weight composite and rescue-therapy use. *(Source: CTP §§5.1.1,5.2.1, PDF-marker pages 44–46; CTP §7.2, pages 67–69)*

**Exploratory Objectives**

- Describe PK trough concentrations, safety (AEs, hypoglycaemia, AESIs, laboratory data, ECG, vital signs), and optional pharmacogenomics. *(Source: CTP §§5.2–5.5, PDF-marker pages 45–61)*
- Conduct TSAP/Publication-SAP further analyses, including recurrent CV events, renal/microvascular endpoints, rescue therapy, risk factors, and correlations of SBP/HbA1c with UACR. *(Source: CTP §§5.2–5.5, PDF-marker pages 45–61; TSAP §§5,7.6, pages 192–203,240–245; Publication SAP §§5,7.4–7.6, pages 271–291)*

## 2. Study Methods

### 2.1 Trial Design

- **Design Type:** Phase III, international, multicentre, randomised, parallel-group, double-blind, placebo-controlled, add-on-to-standard-care, event-driven CV-safety study; screening, 2-week open-label placebo run-in, double-blind treatment until the required primary events, then 30-day follow-up. *(Source: CTP Synopsis, PDF-marker pages 6–10; CTP §§3.1, 6.2, pages 27, 62–66)*
- **Allocation Ratio:** 1:1:1. *(Source: CTP §§4.1.2, 7.1, PDF-marker pages 38, 67)*
- **Intervention Summary:** Oral once-daily study drug in addition to background therapy.
  - Empagliflozin (BI 10773) 10 mg.
  - Empagliflozin (BI 10773) 25 mg.
  - Matched placebo. *(Source: CTP §§3.1, 4.1.1–4.1.4, PDF-marker pages 27, 37–39)*

All patients have a 2-week open-label placebo run-in before randomisation. During the first 12 post-randomisation weeks, pre-consent background antidiabetic therapy should remain unchanged where possible (rescue is permitted by criteria); after week 12, it may be adjusted for clinical care. Planned treatment is approximately 6 to 8 years (up to approximately 420 weeks), conditional on accrual of the required events. *(Source: CTP §§3.1, 4.1.4, PDF-marker pages 27, 39; CTP §7.6, pages 74–75)*

### 2.2 Randomization

- **Method:** Third-party phone/web IVRS/IWRS assigns treatment from a computer-generated pseudorandom, reproducible and non-predictable sequence. *(Source: CTP §§4.1.2, 7.5, PDF-marker pages 38, 72)*
- **Stratification Factors:** Screening HbA1c <8.5%/≥8.5%; randomisation BMI <30/≥30 kg/m²; North America, Latin America, Europe, Africa or Asia; screening eGFR ≥90, 60–89, or 30–59 mL/min. *(Source: CTP §§4.1.2, 7.1, 7.5, PDF-marker pages 38, 67, 72)*
- **Minimization or Blocking:** Blocked, balanced randomisation within strata; no minimisation is reported. *(Source: CTP §7.5, PDF-marker page 72)*

For the TSAP primary model, North America includes Australia and New Zealand. Baseline-characteristic tables compare planned IVRS strata with actual CRF strata; analyses use actual CRF information, with IXRS values only as a stated fallback for missing HbA1c/eGFR. *(Source: TSAP §§7.1,7.4.1, PDF-marker pages 236–237)*

### 2.3 Sample Size

- **Planned Sample Size:** 7000 patients: 2333 empagliflozin 10 mg, 2333 empagliflozin 25 mg, and 2334 placebo. *(Source: CTP Synopsis, PDF-marker page 6; CTP §3.1, page 27)*
- **Outcome Used for Calculation:** Time to first 3P-MACE. *(Source: CTP §7.6, PDF-marker pages 73–75)*
- **Outcome Type:** Time-to-event HR non-inferiority endpoint. *(Source: CTP §§7.1, 7.6, PDF-marker pages 67, 73–75)*
- **Assumed Control-Arm Value:** No fixed placebo event rate is used for the event requirement; scenarios use yearly event rates 1.0%, 1.5%, and 2.0%. *(Source: CTP §7.6, PDF-marker pages 73–75)*
- **Assumed Treatment-Arm Value:** Identical hazards for pooled empagliflozin and placebo for the non-inferiority event calculation. *(Source: CTP §7.6, PDF-marker page 73)*
- **Target Difference / Effect Size:** HR margin 1.3; 691 events provide at least 80% power to detect HR 0.785 (21.5% risk reduction). *(Source: CTP §7.6, PDF-marker pages 73–75)*
- **Variability Assumption:** Not applicable to the time-to-event event calculation. *(Source: CTP §7.6, PDF-marker pages 73–75)*
- **Alpha (Significance Level):** One-sided .025; .0001 is spent at interim and .0249 remains for final analysis. *(Source: CTP §§7.3.4, 7.6, PDF-marker pages 71, 73–75)*
- **Power:** The NI event calculation targets 90% power at margin 1.3 (690 events without interim adjustment); separately, 691 events provide at least 80% power to detect HR 0.785. *(Source: CTP §7.6, PDF-marker pages 73–75)*
- **Statistical Test Assumed:** Log-rank test in EAST version 5; stated equivalent to Cox proportional-hazards score test. *(Source: CTP §7.6, PDF-marker pages 73–75)*
- **Required Number of Events:** Minimum 691 adjudicated primary events (690 before interim adjustment). *(Source: CTP §§7.3.1, 7.6, PDF-marker pages 69, 73–75)*
- **Non-Inferiority / Equivalence Margin:** HR 1.3. *(Source: CTP §7.6, PDF-marker pages 73–75)*
- **Dropout / Non-Adherence Inflation:** No numeric sample-size inflation factor for dropout or non-adherence is reported. *(Source: pages 1–297 reviewed)*
- **Design Effect / Clustering:** Not applicable; individually randomised trial. *(Source: CTP §§3.1, 7.5, PDF-marker pages 27, 72)*
- **Basis for Assumptions:** 24-month accrual at 3500/year, 1.5% yearly event rate and 7000 patients yield just under 8 years; duration scenarios also use 1.0% and 2.0% annual event rates and 2- or 2.5-year accrual. *(Source: CTP §7.6, PDF-marker pages 74–75)*
- **Sample Size Re-Estimation / Amendments:** Blinded overall event-rate review may change recruitment goal; if assumptions/accrual require more recruitment or change, a protocol amendment is required. *(Source: CTP §3.3, PDF-marker page 31; CTP §7.6, page 75)*
- **Software Used for Calculation:** EAST Software, version 5. *(Source: CTP §7.6, PDF-marker pages 73–75)*
- **Sample Size Rationale:** The event-driven trial stops when at least 691 adjudicated primary events occur, not when 691 key-secondary events occur. *(Source: CTP §7.6, PDF-marker page 75)*

The calculation pools the two active arms (2:1 active:placebo). The event table gives 516/690 events for 80%/90% power; event count is independent of accrual, follow-up and annual event rate, whereas the number randomised/duration depends on them. *(Source: CTP §7.6, PDF-marker pages 73–75)*

### 2.4 Hypothesis Framework
Pooled empagliflozin versus placebo follows a fixed four-step hierarchy: NI for primary 3P-MACE (H0 HR≥1.3), NI for key-secondary 4P-MACE (H0 HR≥1.3), superiority for primary (H0 HR≥1), then superiority for key secondary (H0 HR≥1). Each is one-sided alpha .025; proceed only if the preceding 95% CI upper bound is below 1.3 (NI) or 1.0 (superiority). *(Source: CTP §7.2, PDF-marker pages 67–69)*

### 2.5 Statistical Interim Analyses

- **Interim Analyses Planned:** An interim analysis at approximately 80 confirmed primary events supports the NDA and a CV meta-analysis; an independent team conducts it to preserve trial-team blinding. Independent DMC unblinded reviews occur about 3–4 times yearly. *(Source: CTP §7.3.4, PDF-marker page 71)*
- **Stopping Guidelines:** The fixed hierarchy stops when a required criterion fails; DMC may recommend continue, modify or stop based on progress, safety and critical efficacy endpoints. *(Source: CTP §7.2, PDF-marker pages 67–69; CTP §3.1.1, page 30)*
- **Alpha Spending or Adjustment:** Haybittle–Peto boundary; .0001 alpha spent at interim and .0249 at final to maintain one-sided .025 experiment-wise alpha. *(Source: CTP §7.3.4, PDF-marker page 71; TSAP §4, page 177)*

The DMC, supported by an independent statistician external to BI, is the sole body with unblinded results; no BI employee has access. Interim/meta-analysis results cause no protocol change. Final analyses are those in the TSAP; interim detail is in the ISE, ISS, CV meta-analysis SAPs and Interim Analyses Logistics Plan. *(Source: CTP §7.3.4, PDF-marker pages 71–72; TSAP §§7,9.5, pages 235,262–263)*

### 2.6 Timing of Analyses

- **Final Analysis Timing:** Final analyses occur after at least 691 adjudicated primary events; patients are followed to trial termination, and the end is last patient out. *(Source: CTP §§3.1, 7.3.1, 7.6, PDF-marker pages 27, 69, 75)*
- **Outcome Assessment Schedule:** Post-randomisation visits are weeks 4, 8, 12, 16, 28, 40 and 52, then every 14 weeks; efficacy variables are assessed at weeks 12, 52, annually and end of study, with weight/waist also at follow-up. Final/EOS is followed by Visit 98 30 days later. *(Source: CTP Flow Chart and §§5.1,6.2, PDF-marker pages 11–13, 44–46, 63–66)*

HbA1c, FPG, weight, waist circumference and BP are assessed at weeks 12, 52, annually and end of study; weight and waist are additionally assessed at 30-day follow-up. The final/EOS visit includes drug collection/compliance, physical examination, laboratory assessment and ECG; follow-up includes BP/pulse, weight/waist, blood/urine safety labs, AEs/endpoints and concomitant therapy. *(Source: CTP §§5.1.1,6.2.3, PDF-marker pages 44–46,64–66)*

## 3. Statistical Principles

### 3.1 Significance and Confidence Intervals

- **Alpha Level:** Confirmatory tests are one-sided .025, with final remaining alpha .0249; exploratory other secondary/further tests and CIs are two-sided .05. *(Source: CTP §§7.2–7.3.4, PDF-marker pages 67–71; TSAP §7.5.2, page 239)*
- **Multiplicity Adjustment:** Fixed sequential gatekeeping controls the confirmatory primary/key-secondary sequence. Other secondary and further endpoint analyses outside this sequence are exploratory and receive no multiplicity correction. *(Source: CTP §7.2, PDF-marker pages 67–69; TSAP §7.5.2, page 239)*
- **Confidence Intervals:** Primary HR uses 95% CI and a CI based on remaining alpha (1−2×alpha); NI/superiority use the upper confidence bound. *(Source: CTP §7.2, PDF-marker pages 67–69; TSAP §7.4.1, page 237)*

### 3.2 Protocol Deviations

- **Adherence Definition:** Double-blind-treatment compliance should be 80%–120%; off study drug >7 consecutive days is recorded. TSAP overall compliance is weighted reported compliance divided by duration to last medication-return visit. *(Source: CTP §4.3, PDF-marker page 149; TSAP §7.3, page 236)*
- **Adherence Assessment:** Medication accountability/compliance is checked at each treatment visit; comments and stop/termination eCRFs are required for defined off-drug periods. *(Source: CTP §§4.1.4, 6.2.2, PDF-marker pages 39, 63; CTP amendment, page 149)*
- **Protocol Deviation Definition:** An important protocol violation affects patient rights/safety or can non-negligibly influence the primary outcome contrary to objectives. *(Source: TSAP §6.2, PDF-marker page 205)*
- **Protocol Deviation Reporting:** IPVs, including whether they exclude from PPS, are frequency summarised in the CTR; a medication-code-break listing is provided. *(Source: TSAP §6.2, PDF-marker pages 205–209)*

All deviations are documented. Missing consent excludes data from all sets; randomised/no dose excludes TS, OS, FAS, TS-FU, PPS and FAS-MDPP4; missing baseline HbA1c excludes FAS; wrong drug for >20% of duration excludes PPS; and serious site noncompliance prompts separately documented decisions on data use. *(Source: TSAP §§6.2–6.3, PDF-marker pages 205–211; CTP §8.7, page 79)*

### 3.3 Analysis Populations

- **Intention-to-Treat:** FAS comprises randomised, treated patients with baseline HbA1c and is the ITT basis for efficacy; treatment assignment is as randomised. The primary CV analysis is on TS and includes all events to trial termination. *(Source: CTP §§7.2–7.3, PDF-marker pages 68–69; TSAP §§6.1,6.3, pages 205,209–211)*
- **Per Protocol:** PPS comprises treated patients without the listed IPVs; not performed if PPS is <1% fewer than TS. *(Source: TSAP §6.3, PDF-marker page 210)*
- **Other Populations:** SCR: consent plus ≥1 screening procedure; RS: screened/randomised regardless of dosing; TS: randomised with ≥1 dose; OS: ≥30 cumulative dosing days with 30-day post-last-dose/end-trial observation rule; TS-FU: follow-up 28–50 days post-last dose; PKS: TS with PK sample; FAS-MDPP4: FAS on baseline metformin+DPP4 inhibitor with/without one extra oral agent. *(Source: TSAP §6.3, PDF-marker pages 209–211)*

No separate RS safety or efficacy analysis is planned because RS is expected to equal TS. PPS is not used for further efficacy endpoints. *(Source: TSAP §6.3 and Table 6.3:1, PDF-marker pages 210–211)*

## 4. Trial Population

### 4.1 Screening Data
About 14,000 patients are planned for screening at approximately 650 centres in approximately 40 countries to randomise 7000. Eligibility is assessed by medical history, examination and clinical laboratory testing; a site log includes all consented patients regardless of treatment. *(Source: CTP §3.3, PDF-marker pages 31–32)*

### 4.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary
Eligible patients have T2DM before consent, diet/exercise, drug-naïve (no antidiabetic treatment for 12 weeks) or stable background therapy (including insulin dose within 10% for 12 weeks), screening HbA1c 7.0%–10.0% (7.0%–9.0% if drug-naïve), age ≥18 years (Japan ≥20; India 18–65), BMI ≤45 kg/m², consent, and qualifying high CV risk: prior MI; specified multivessel/single-vessel CAD or unstable angina; prior stroke; or PAD. Women of child-bearing potential require acceptable contraception and periodic pregnancy testing. *(Source: CTP §§3.3.1–3.3.2, PDF-marker pages 32–33)*

Exclusions include confirmed run-in fasting glucose >240 mg/dL on a second day, liver enzymes/alkaline phosphatase >3×ULN, planned cardiac procedure within 3 months, eGFR <30, malabsorption surgery, dyscrasia/haemolysis, cancer within 5 years (except basal-cell carcinoma), contraindicated background therapy, unstable weight intervention, systemic steroids/uncontrolled endocrinopathy, pregnancy/nursing or unsuitable contraception, alcohol/drug abuse or poor compliance, recent/ongoing investigational trial, unsafe condition, and acute coronary syndrome/stroke/TIA within 2 months; Canada and South Africa have stated local exclusions. *(Source: CTP §3.3.3, PDF-marker pages 33–34)*

### 4.3 Recruitment
Recruitment is competitive and stops when sufficient patients have been screened; no more than 50% should originate from one geographic region. At least six patients should be randomised per centre, sites may be added if enrolment delays, and >60 patients/site needs Trial Clinical Monitor permission. *(Source: CTP §3.3, PDF-marker page 31)*

### 4.4 Withdrawal and Follow-up

- **Withdrawal Levels:** Patients may withdraw consent; pregnancy mandates stopping investigational drug and follow-up to birth/termination; eligibility violation/noncompliance or safety/interacting medications may cause temporary or permanent drug discontinuation. *(Source: CTP §3.3.4.1, PDF-marker page 35)*
- **Withdrawal Data Collection:** Screening failures need no further follow-up. After randomisation, patients stopping treatment remain on the scheduled follow-up to study end for survival, stroke/MI and clinical status when feasible; sites make documented LTFU attempts about every six months. Consent withdrawal stops further medical-information collection, though EOS/follow-up procedures are requested for safety. *(Source: CTP §§3.3.4.1,6.2.2–6.2.3, PDF-marker pages 35,63–66)*
- **Reasons for Withdrawal:** Withdrawal reasons are recorded in eCRFs and discussed in the final report; post-randomisation withdrawals are not replaced. *(Source: CTP §§3.3.4.1,6.2.3, PDF-marker pages 35,65)*

Pregnancy exposure is reported promptly and followed to outcome. Patients stopping study drug continue scheduled observation; those withdrawing consent receive no further medical-information requests, although EOS/follow-up is requested for safety. *(Source: CTP §§3.3.4.1,5.2.2.2, PDF-marker pages 35,52)*

### 4.5 Baseline Characteristics

- **Baseline Variables:** Sex, race, ethnicity, region, age, height, BMI, smoking, alcohol, hypertension history, diabetes duration, eGFR, antidiabetic therapy, CV risk history, HbA1c, FPG, weight, waist and BP. *(Source: TSAP §7.1, PDF-marker pages 235–236)*
- **Summary Methods:** TS descriptive tables; continuous in-text summaries include N, mean and SD, end-of-text additionally min, quartiles, median and max; categorical tables give N (%) and display missing only when present. *(Source: TSAP §7, PDF-marker page 235)*

CV baseline factors explicitly include ischaemic/haemorrhagic stroke, MI, CABG, multivessel CAD and PAD. Planned-versus-actual randomisation strata, background drug classes/doses and prespecified categories (age, BMI, HbA1c, eGFR, albuminuria, BP and region) are displayed. *(Source: TSAP §§6.4,7.1–7.2, PDF-marker pages 212–221,235–236)*

## 5. Analysis

### 5.1 Primary Outcomes
**Outcome: Time to first adjudicated 3P-MACE** *(Source: CTP §§5.2.1,7.1, PDF-marker pages 45,67)*
- **Definition:** First occurrence of CV death (including fatal stroke and fatal MI), nonfatal MI excluding silent MI, or nonfatal stroke, adjudicated by the CEC. *(Source: CTP §5.2.1, PDF-marker page 45; CTP §7.3.1, page 70)*
- **Measurement Units:** Days from randomisation: event date minus randomisation date plus 1. *(Source: CTP §7.3.1, PDF-marker page 69)*
- **Timing of Measurement:** All events to trial termination are included; event-free patients are censored at individual trial completion. *(Source: CTP §7.2, PDF-marker page 68)*
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Pooled 10/25-mg group versus placebo HR; first event only. *(Source: CTP §§7.1–7.3.1, PDF-marker pages 67–70)*

All adjudicated and confirmed events are used. The primary TS analysis uses time=(event date−randomisation date)+1; censored time=(individual trial-completion date−randomisation date)+1. *(Source: CTP §7.3.1, PDF-marker pages 69–70)*

### 5.2 Secondary Outcomes
**Outcome: Time to first adjudicated key-secondary 4P-MACE** *(Source: CTP §§5.2.1,7.1, PDF-marker pages 45,67)*
- **Definition:** CV death including fatal stroke/MI, nonfatal MI excluding silent MI, nonfatal stroke, or hospitalisation for unstable angina. *(Source: CTP §5.2.1, PDF-marker page 45)*
- **Measurement Units:** Days from randomisation to first event. *(Source: CTP §7.3.1, PDF-marker page 69)*
- **Timing of Measurement:** Analysed in the gatekeeping sequence after primary NI. *(Source: CTP §7.2, PDF-marker pages 67–69)*
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Pooled-active/placebo HR; individual-dose comparisons are additional analyses. *(Source: TSAP §7.5.1, PDF-marker page 239)*

**Outcome: Silent myocardial infarction** *(Source: CTP §5.2.1, PDF-marker page 45)*
- **Definition:** New qualifying ECG Q-wave/QS/R-wave pattern without preceding clinical MI or unstable angina; fatal/nonfatal MI analyses may include it as specified. *(Source: CTP Appendix 10.4, PDF-marker page 84; TSAP §5.2.2, pages 194–196)*
- **Measurement Units:** Event occurrence and days to first event. *(Source: TSAP §5, PDF-marker pages 192–196)*
- **Timing of Measurement:** Baseline and post-baseline ECGs according to the flow chart. *(Source: CTP §§5.2.4,10.4, PDF-marker pages 58,84)*
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** New versus baseline/prior ECG; specified patients are not at risk for the ECG-derived endpoint. *(Source: TSAP §6.8, PDF-marker pages 231–234)*

**Outcome: Heart failure requiring hospitalisation** *(Source: CTP §5.2.1, PDF-marker page 45)*
- **Definition:** Occurrence and time to first adjudicated heart failure requiring hospitalisation. *(Source: CTP §5.2.1, PDF-marker page 45; TSAP §7.5.2, page 239)*
- **Measurement Units:** Event occurrence and days to first event. *(Source: TSAP §7.5.2, PDF-marker pages 239–240)*
- **Timing of Measurement:** TS analysis to trial completion; specified OS and competing-risk analyses use their stated censoring rules. *(Source: TSAP §7.5.2, PDF-marker pages 239–240)*
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Cox/KM and cumulative-incidence analyses; death is the competing event. *(Source: TSAP §7.5.2, PDF-marker pages 239–240)*

**Outcome: Albuminuria, macroalbuminuria, nephropathy and microvascular outcome** *(Source: CTP §5.2.1, PDF-marker pages 45–46)*
- **Definition:** New albuminuria uses UACR ≥30 mg/g; macroalbuminuria uses UACR >300 mg/g. New/worsening nephropathy includes macroalbuminuria, doubling serum creatinine with MDRD eGFR ≤45 mL/min/1.73m², continuous renal replacement therapy or renal death. The microvascular composite comprises retinal photocoagulation, vitreous haemorrhage, diabetes-related blindness or nephropathy. *(Source: TSAP §5.2.2, PDF-marker pages 194–196; Publication SAP §§5.3.3.1,7.5.2.3, pages 272–273,287)*
- **Measurement Units:** UACR mg/g, eGFR mL/min/1.73m², serum creatinine and event occurrence/time. *(Source: CTP §5.2.3, PDF-marker pages 56–57)*
- **Timing of Measurement:** Urine/laboratory measures follow the flow chart; primary protocol renal outcomes use trial follow-up, with stated OS analyses. *(Source: CTP §§5.2.1,5.2.3, PDF-marker pages 45–57; Publication SAP page 287)*
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Central-laboratory spot UACR and MDRD eGFR; publication analyses add sustained ≥50%/≥57% eGFR reductions, sustained cystatin-C/UACR changes and version-2 microvascular composites. *(Source: CTP §5.2.3, PDF-marker pages 56–57; Publication SAP §§5.3.3.1,7.6.2.1, pages 272–273,289–291)*

**Outcome: Further CV, diabetes-related efficacy, rescue, PK and safety endpoints** *(Source: CTP §§5.1.1,5.2.1,5.5, PDF-marker pages 44–46,58–59)*
- **Definition:** Further CV endpoints are CV death, fatal/nonfatal MI and stroke, unstable-angina and HF hospitalisation, all-cause mortality, TIA and revascularisation. Further efficacy endpoints are change from baseline in HbA1c, FPG, weight, waist and BP, the HbA1c/SBP/weight composite, and BP-response endpoints. Other endpoints include rescue therapy, empagliflozin trough concentration, AEs/hypoglycaemia, labs, ECG and vital signs. *(Source: CTP §§5.1.1,5.2.1,5.5, PDF-marker pages 44–46,58–59; TSAP §5, pages 192–203)*
- **Measurement Units:** HbA1c %, FPG mg/dL, weight kg, waist cm, BP mmHg, concentration, event occurrence/time and relevant laboratory/ECG units. *(Source: CTP §§5.1.2,5.2.3–5.2.5, PDF-marker pages 44–59)*
- **Timing of Measurement:** Weeks 12,52, annually/end study (and weight/waist at follow-up); trough PK at visits 6 and 10 in the PK subset. *(Source: CTP §§5.1.1,5.5.1–5.5.2, PDF-marker pages 44–46,58–60)*
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Change-from-baseline, time-to-event/first event, composite and recurrent-event derivations are stated in TSAP/Publication SAP. *(Source: TSAP §§5,7.6, PDF-marker pages 192–203,240–245; Publication SAP §5, pages 271–274)*

### 5.3 Analysis Methods

- **Primary Analysis Methods:** On TS, display Kaplan–Meier curves and estimate HRs with Cox regression for pooled active versus placebo, adjusted for treatment, continuous age, sex, baseline BMI category, baseline HbA1c category, baseline eGFR category and region; Breslow handles ties. *(Source: TSAP §7.4.1, PDF-marker page 237)*
- **Covariate Adjustment:** If HbA1c/eGFR laboratory data are missing, use investigator-entered IXRS stratification category. Further efficacy ANCOVA/MMRM adjust baseline endpoint, HbA1c, BMI, eGFR and region. *(Source: TSAP §§7.4.1,7.6.2, PDF-marker pages 237,241–243)*
- **Assumption Checks:** Explore proportional hazards with log(-log survival) versus log time, treatment×log(time), and Schoenfeld residual plots. *(Source: TSAP §7.4.1, PDF-marker page 237)*
- **Alternative Methods if Assumptions Fail:** Identify strata with proportionality and use stratified Cox; investigate piecewise Cox. *(Source: TSAP §7.4.2.1, PDF-marker page 238)*
- **Sensitivity Analyses:** Repeat primary on OS and PPS; individual doses; add naïve/experienced status; competing-risk cumulative incidence and Cox analyses; alternative 7-/30-day censoring for selected CV outcomes. *(Source: TSAP §§7.4.2,7.5–7.6, PDF-marker pages 237–245; Publication SAP §§7.4–7.6, pages 275–291)*
- **Subgroup Analyses:** Primary/key secondary TS Cox interaction models and forest/KM plots across prespecified demographics, CV risk, HbA1c, BMI, renal function, geography and concomitant therapies; apply stated minimum event thresholds. *(Source: TSAP §§6.4,7.4.3, PDF-marker pages 211–221,238)*

Centre is excluded from models; region is included. Missing subgroup categories are not analysed and small categories can be pooled. Time-to-event subgroup modelling requires 21 total events across three groups (14 for two-group comparison); otherwise frequencies/incidence may be supplied. Tertiary efficacy uses a linear model on FAS; other secondary/further analyses are exploratory. *(Source: CTP §7.3.2, PDF-marker page 70; TSAP §§6.4–6.5,7.4.3, pages 211–221,238)*

### 5.4 Missing Data

- **Missing Data Assumptions:** No imputation for safety or primary CV analysis; pursue survival/primary outcome through trial end after premature treatment discontinuation. *(Source: CTP §7.4, PDF-marker page 72)*
- **Handling Methods:** Original-result analysis retains observed data without visit windowing except baseline/last/follow-up. OC uses on-treatment/pre-rescue observations; OC-AD includes post-treatment/rescue data. LOCF/interpolation applies continuous further efficacy only through the planned visit achievable by all randomised patients; NCF applies the HbA1c/SBP/weight composite and binary BP endpoints. Post-rescue values are missing for OC/LOCF. No safety data are imputed; lipid values are analysed by MMRM OC/OC-AD rather than LOCF. Missing/partial AE, death, treatment, concomitant-treatment, completion, hypoglycaemia and PK dates/times follow TSAP/BI derivation rules. *(Source: TSAP §§6.6–6.7, PDF-marker pages 222–230)*

### 5.5 Additional Analyses
Further analyses include ANCOVA and REML MMRM with unstructured covariance (AR(1), then compound symmetry if nonconvergent), eGFR random-coefficient slopes, rescue KM/log-rank analysis, PK trough summaries, recurrent-event analyses, renal/microvascular and BP analyses, and publication-SAP correlations/recurrent outcomes. *(Source: TSAP §§7.6.2–7.6.5, PDF-marker pages 241–245; Publication SAP §§5,7, pages 271–291)*

Publication-SAP supportive work additionally includes rescue-adjusted primary analyses, hypoglycaemia–3P-MACE relationship, risk-factor, recurrent-event and censoring investigations; OS renal/microvascular analyses; fat-index/arterial-stiffness and BP analyses; country-specific (Mexico, India, Japan, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Korea) and East-Asian presentations. *(Source: TSAP §§7.4–7.6,9.1–9.2, PDF-marker pages 237–245,257–259; Publication SAP §§7.4–7.6, pages 275–291)*

### 5.6 Harms

- **Adverse Event Definitions:** An AE is any untoward medical occurrence after consent; serious criteria, worsening-condition rules, AESI definitions and expedited reporting are protocol specified. Hypoglycaemia categories use symptoms, glucose thresholds (≤70 and <54 mg/dL), and need for assistance. *(Source: CTP §§5.2.2.1–5.2.2.2, PDF-marker pages 47–52; CTP §5.2.3, page 57)*
- **Safety Analysis Methods:** TS safety analyses are descriptive by randomised treatment. AEs use MedDRA and are treatment-emergent from first dose through last dose+7 days; summaries are patient frequency/proportions by SOC/PT, seriousness, intensity, relationship, discontinuation and AESI, with time-adjusted incidence per 100 patient-years. Labs, vital signs and ECG receive descriptive analyses; selected continuous measures use MMRM. *(Source: CTP §7.3.3, PDF-marker pages 70–71; TSAP §§7.8–7.8.5, pages 245–255)*
- **Grading Scales and Causality:** Investigator assesses clinical relevance and causal relationship; clinically relevant ECG/lab/vital-sign changes are AEs. Hepatic AESI uses ALT/AST and bilirubin thresholds, with 30-day follow-up in TSAP; CEC and independent hepatic/cancer adjudication apply blinded charters. *(Source: CTP §§5.2.2–5.2.4, PDF-marker pages 47–58; TSAP §§5.4,7.8.2, pages 200–203,252–255)*

All serious/nonserious AEs are collected from consent through 30-day follow-up; treatment-emergent analysis assigns first-dose through last-dose+7 days, while laboratory and other endpoints use their specific 1-/3-/7-day windows. AE occurrences are collapsed into episodes then condensed records; other significant AEs include nonserious events causing discontinuation/reduction or marked laboratory abnormality/significant added therapy. AESIs include decreased renal function, hepatic injury, UTI/genital infection, volume depletion, cancer, hypersensitivity, fracture, hypoglycaemia and ketoacidosis searches. *(Source: CTP §§5.2.2–5.2.5, PDF-marker pages 47–59,150–157; TSAP §§6.7,7.8.1, pages 226–228,245–251)*

Hypoglycaemia is asymptomatic ≤70 mg/dL, documented symptomatic 54–70 mg/dL, documented symptomatic <54 mg/dL without external assistance, or severe with assistance. Hepatic thresholds include ALT/AST ≥3×ULN with bilirubin ≥2×ULN and isolated elevations (≥3,5,10,20×ULN); decreased renal function is creatinine ≥2× baseline and >ULN. Investigators assess intensity/relatedness and follow central-lab alerts/reporting procedures. *(Source: CTP §§5.2.2.1,5.2.3, PDF-marker pages 47–57; TSAP §§5.4,7.8.1.4, pages 200–203,248–249)*
