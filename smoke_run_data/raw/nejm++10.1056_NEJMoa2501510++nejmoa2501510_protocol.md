# Statistical Analysis Plan

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Background and Rationale
C3G and IC-MPGN are complement-driven glomerular diseases that may occur in native kidneys or recur after transplant. Pegcetacoplan inhibits C3 and C3b, with the intended effect of limiting inappropriate complement activation and glomerular C3-deposit injury; reduction in proteinuria is the expected early renal signal. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §§4-4.2, pages 97-99)*

### 1.2 Objectives

**Primary Objectives**

- Assess twice-weekly SC pegcetacoplan versus placebo in primary C3G/IC-MPGN on the basis of proteinuria reduction. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §5.1.1, page 102)*

**Secondary Objectives**

- Assess the effect of pegcetacoplan on eGFR. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §5.1.2, page 102)*
- Assess the effect of pegcetacoplan on additional C3G/IC-MPGN disease-related parameters. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §5.1.2, page 102)*
- Evaluate safety of pegcetacoplan over 52 weeks of treatment. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §5.1.2, page 102)*

**Exploratory Objectives**

- Characterize additional clinical, laboratory, and histologic findings in response to pegcetacoplan. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §5.1.3, page 102)*

## 2. Study Methods

### 2.1 Trial Design

- **Design Type:** Phase 3, randomized, multicenter, placebo-controlled, double-blind study: 10-week screening, 26-week randomized controlled period (RCP), 26-week open-label period (OLP), then 8-week follow-up for participants not entering the long-term extension. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §§1,6-6.1, pages 82-84, 106-110)*
- **Allocation Ratio:** 1:1 pegcetacoplan to placebo in the RCP. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §§6.1.2,8.5.1, pages 110,125)*
- **Intervention Summary:** SC infusion twice weekly; RCP is blinded and OLP is open label, with all participants receiving pegcetacoplan in OLP. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §§6.1,8.1, pages 106-107,122)*
  - Pegcetacoplan group: pegcetacoplan in RCP and OLP.
  - Placebo group: matching placebo in RCP then pegcetacoplan in OLP.
  - Adults and adolescents >=50 kg: 1080 mg/20 mL twice weekly; adolescents 35 to <50 kg: 648 mg first, then 810 mg/15 mL twice weekly; adolescents 30 to <35 kg: 540 mg for first 2 doses, then 648 mg/12 mL twice weekly. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §8.1, page 122)*
  - Participants, sponsor, investigators, and site staff remain blinded to RCP allocation through completion of all week-26 assessments and RCP database lock; central evaluations, including central pathology, are blinded. Only non-site drug-supply/logistics personnel are unblinded. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §8.5.2, page 125)*

### 2.2 Randomization

- **Method:** Centrally performed computer-generated randomization schedule; the standalone SAP specifies stratified permuted blocks. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §8.5.1, page 125; standalone SAP §3.2, page 212)*
- **Stratification Factors:** Posttransplant recurrence versus nontransplant; then baseline renal biopsy available (screening or historic within 28 weeks) versus unavailable. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §8.5.1, page 125)*
- **Minimization or Blocking:** Stratified permuted blocks; minimization is not reported. *(Source: standalone SAP §3.2, page 212; Protocol Amendment 4 and standalone SAP, pages 77-194 and 198-267 reviewed)*

### 2.3 Sample Size

- **Planned Sample Size:** Approximately 80-100 randomized, 40-50 per arm; at least 78 native-kidney participants and at least 63 with native-kidney C3G. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.1, page 145)*
- **Outcome Used for Calculation:** Week-26 uPCR reduction/log ratio to baseline. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.1, page 145)*
- **Outcome Type:** Continuous log-transformed uPCR ratio. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.1, page 145)*
- **Assumed Control-Arm Value:** 20% uPCR reduction; mean log ratio -0.22. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.1, page 145)*
- **Assumed Treatment-Arm Value:** 60% uPCR reduction; mean log ratio -0.92. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.1, page 145)*
- **Target Difference / Effect Size:** Difference in assumed mean log ratios, -0.92 versus -0.22. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.1, page 145)*
- **Variability Assumption:** SD 0.88 on log scale. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.1, page 145)*
- **Alpha (Significance Level):** One-sided 0.025 for the calculation. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.1, page 145)*
- **Power:** At least 90% for 70 total participants before attrition inflation. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.1, page 145)*
- **Statistical Test Assumed:** Amendment 4 does not name a calculation test; the original protocol planned a two-sample t-test calculation. This is distinct from the final MMRM efficacy analysis. *(Source: original protocol §13.1, page 7; Protocol Amendment 4 §§12.1,12.3.1, pages 145-146)*
- **Required Number of Events:** Not applicable; no event-driven design. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.1, page 145)*
- **Non-Inferiority / Equivalence Margin:** Not applicable; superiority comparison versus placebo. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §§6.1,12.1, pages 106,145)*
- **Dropout / Non-Adherence Inflation:** 10% attrition for potential missing assessments and COVID-19 impact. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.1, page 145)*
- **Design Effect / Clustering:** Not applicable; individually randomized, non-clustered design. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §8.5.1, page 125)*
- **Basis for Assumptions:** Preliminary Study APL2-201 data. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.1, page 145)*
- **Sample Size Re-Estimation / Amendments:** The original protocol planned conditional-power re-estimation at approximately 50% completion, without Type I error adjustment and capped at 120; Amendment 4 does not retain this plan and records final enrollment of 124 after enrollment closure. *(Source: original protocol §13.1, pages 6-7; Protocol Amendment 4 amendment summary, pages 80-81)*
- **Software Used for Calculation:** Not reported for the final Amendment 4 calculation or the original conditional-power re-estimation. *(Source: original protocol §13.1, page 7; Protocol Amendment 4 and standalone SAP, pages 77-194 and 198-350 reviewed)*
- **Sample Size Rationale:** 70 provides targeted power under specified log-uPCR assumptions; 10% inflation supports enrollment targets. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.1, page 145)*

### 2.4 Hypothesis Framework
The primary null is no between-group difference in week-26 log-transformed uPCR ratio to baseline; its alternative is a difference. The comparative key/additional hypotheses separately test no difference versus a difference in composite renal response, >=50% uPCR response, eGFR change, histologic activity-score change, C3c response, proteinuria <1 g/day, albumin normalization, C3 normalization, FACIT-Fatigue change, and KDQOL change. The final SAP uses the ITT population meeting inclusion/exclusion criteria; treatment regimes are twice-weekly pegcetacoplan or placebo for 26 weeks. Primary/key/additional estimands specify ProhibiRescue (prohibited medication/rescue), RenalReplace (dialysis/transplant), and DisctTrt (permanent discontinuation) strategies. Primary, key secondary, and additional secondary endpoints are tested in a fixed sequence to control trial-wise type I error. *(Source: standalone SAP §§6.2-6.5, pages 219-230; final SAP Version 2.0 §§4,6, pages 286-290)*

### 2.5 Statistical Interim Analyses

- **Interim Analyses Planned:** Amendment 4 provides an administrative/regulatory analysis after all complete RCP and data are cleaned. The standalone SAP states no formal interim analysis is planned; these statements address distinct activities. An independent DMC regularly reviews accumulated safety/efficacy under its DMC SAP. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §§12.8-12.9, page 152; standalone SAP §§11-12, pages 249-250)*
- **Stopping Guidelines:** Not reported in protocol. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 and standalone SAP, pages 77-194 and 198-267 reviewed)*
- **Alpha Spending or Adjustment:** No adjustment is necessary for the administrative/regulatory analysis. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.8, page 152)*

### 2.6 Timing of Analyses

- **Final Analysis Timing:** Formal SAP finalized before database lock. RCP analysis follows completion or discontinuation of all participants, entry, review, cleaning and finalization of corresponding data, lock of the week-26 analysis database, and unblinding. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §§8.5.2,12, pages 125,145; standalone SAP §3.5, pages 213-214)*
- **Outcome Assessment Schedule:** Primary and secondary endpoints are week 26; they are evaluated at week 52 as exploratory. Primary uPCR uses an equal-weighted average over weeks 24,25,26; 24-hour urine collections are at weeks 24 and 48. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §§5.2.1,12.3.1, pages 102-104,146)*

## 3. Statistical Principles

### 3.1 Significance and Confidence Intervals

- **Alpha Level:** Primary analysis is two-sided 0.05. *(Source: Standalone SAP §6.2, page 219)*
- **Multiplicity Adjustment:** Fixed sequence: test key then additional secondary endpoints in their Section 5.2.1 order only after primary rejection; stop once a null is not rejected. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.3.2, page 147; Standalone SAP §6.2, page 219)*
- **Confidence Intervals:** 95% CIs accompany treatment estimates for primary and comparative secondary analyses. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §§12.3.1-12.3.2, pages 146-150)*

### 3.2 Protocol Deviations

- **Adherence Definition:** Participants bring empty/unused packaging and diaries to every visit; accountability is recorded in interactive response technology. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §8.4, page 125)*
- **Adherence Assessment:** Study-drug exposure and compliance are summarized; compliance is 100 times the number of doses received divided by planned doses, categorized as <80%, 80-<90%, 90-100%, and >100%. Missed doses are restarted as soon as possible on the original schedule. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §§8.1,8.4, pages 122,125; standalone SAP §§5.6-5.7, pages 217-218)*
- **Protocol Deviation Definition:** PP excludes ITT participants with eligibility violations and/or protocol deviations that could influence efficacy assessment. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.2.4, page 145)*
- **Protocol Deviation Reporting:** Monitor records/reports deviations; PP exclusion decisions are made and documented before database lock. The standalone SAP classifies deviations major/minor under the Protocol Deviation Handling Plan and requires ITT summaries/listings. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §§12.2.4,13.1, pages 145,153; standalone SAP §5.8, page 218)*

### 3.3 Analysis Populations

- **Intention-to-Treat:** All randomized participants, analyzed as assigned regardless of received treatment; all efficacy analyses use ITT. Final SAP Version 2.0 removed the mITT set used in the superseded 2023 SAP. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.2.3, page 145; final SAP Version 2.0 revision history and §§4,6, pages 267,286-290)*
- **Per Protocol:** ITT participants without eligibility violations/deviations that could influence efficacy; exclusions decided/documented pre-lock. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.2.4, page 145)*
- **Other Populations:** Screened: all written consent, disposition only. Safety: >=1 RCP study-drug dose, analyzed as received. Amendment 4 describes PK/PD as ITT recipients with evaluable postdose measures; the standalone SAP operationally defines PK/PD sets from Safety Set recipients with >=1 quantifiable postdose concentration/PD endpoint. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §§12.2.1-12.2.6, pages 145-146; standalone SAP §4, page 214)*

## 4. Trial Population

### 4.1 Screening Data
Screening confirms eligibility through two stages; it includes 24-hour urine, three triplicate FMU sets, baseline biopsy rules, retrospective eGFR up to 3 years, vaccination, and medical-history/laboratory/pathology review. The screened set is all consented participants and is disposition-only. Screening failures may remain in screening until eligible; formal rescreening after leaving screening requires reconsent and a new ID, while specified prior assessments may validate rescreening. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §§6.1.1,12.2.1, pages 109-110,145; standalone SAP §§4.1,5.1, pages 214-215)*

### 4.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary
Eligible participants have primary C3G/IC-MPGN, active renal disease, proteinuria >=1 g/day and FMU uPCR >=1000 mg/g, eGFR >=30 mL/min/1.73m2, stable renal regimen, required vaccination status, reproductive safeguards, and capacity to consent/assent and administer drug/caregiver support. Adults are eligible; where approved adolescents aged 12-17 weighing >=30 kg may enroll. Material exclusions include prior pegcetacoplan, improving disease, relevant secondary disease/infection/malignancy, prohibited complement therapies, pregnancy/breastfeeding, severe infection, and hereditary fructose intolerance. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 synopsis, pages 84-86; Protocol §7.1-7.2, pages 116-120)*

### 4.3 Recruitment
Enrollment was planned at 80-100, with native-kidney/C3G/adolescent targets and disease-type monitoring; Amendment 4 states that all eligible screened participants were enrolled at closure, for final enrollment of 124. Recruitment methods are not reported in protocol. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §§6, amendment summary, pages 106,81; Protocol Amendment 4 and standalone SAP, pages 77-194 and 198-267 reviewed)*

### 4.4 Withdrawal and Follow-up

- **Withdrawal Levels:** Treatment discontinuation, study withdrawal, and loss to follow-up are distinguished; withdrawn participants cannot reenter. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §7.3.1, page 120)*
- **Withdrawal Data Collection:** RCP treatment discontinuers are encouraged to complete remaining RCP visits; OLP discontinuers enter follow-up; TEAEs prompting discontinuation/withdrawal are followed to resolution, stabilization, or lack of clinical significance. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §§7.3.1-7.3.2, pages 120-121)*
- **Reasons for Withdrawal:** Safety/AE/laboratory/illness, consent withdrawal, nonadherence, LTFU, sponsor/regulatory termination, and pregnancy; date/reasons are recorded. LTFU requires >=3 documented contact attempts, including one written attempt. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §§7.3.2-7.3.3, pages 120-121)*

### 4.5 Baseline Characteristics

- **Baseline Variables:** Age, sex, race, ethnicity, region, height/weight/BMI; C3G/IC-MPGN type, native/posttransplant status, transplant history, diagnosis/transplant timing, medical history, prior/concomitant medicines/procedures, study-drug exposure; baseline eGFR, uPCR, serum C3/albumin and other laboratory values; and renal-biopsy activity/C3c findings. Derived variables include time since diagnosis and transplant. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.7, page 152; standalone SAP §§5.2-5.6, pages 215-217)*
- **Summary Methods:** Summarized by treatment group and specified analysis set, including adult/adolescent and disease-type summaries; WHO and MedDRA dictionaries code concomitant medications and medical histories, respectively. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.7, page 152; standalone SAP §§5.2-5.3, pages 215-216)*

## 5. Analysis

### 5.1 Primary Outcomes
**Outcome: Week-26 log-transformed uPCR ratio to baseline** *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §5.2.1, page 103)*
- **Definition:** Ratio of week-26 to baseline first-morning uPCR, analyzed on natural-log scale; week-26 is equal-weighted weeks 24,25,26. Baseline is average of >=6 of 9 FMU samples from screening through day 1 (include days -2,-1 and predose day 1); if <6, average all collected values. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.3.1, page 146; Standalone SAP §6.4.1, page 222)*
- **Measurement Units:** uPCR (mg/g); log ratio is analysis scale, with geometric means/ratios returned to original scale for presentation. *(Source: Standalone SAP §§6.4-6.4.1, pages 222-223)*
- **Timing of Measurement:** Week 26, using weeks 24-26; triplicate FMU at each visit plus additional week-25 triplicate. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §§6.1.2,12.3.1, pages 110,146)*
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Natural-log transform because uPCR is expected highly skewed; equal-weighted contrast. *(Source: Standalone SAP §§6.4-6.4.1, pages 222-223)*

The main analysis is ITT; participants with <6 baseline samples remain in ITT but are excluded from PP. ProhibiRescue uses a hypothetical strategy, RenalReplace a composite strategy, and DisctTrt treatment policy. *(Source: final SAP Version 2.0 §§4,6, pages 286-290; standalone SAP §§6.3-6.4.1, pages 220-223)*

### 5.2 Secondary Outcomes
**Outcome: Key secondary endpoints at week 26** *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §5.2.1, pages 103-104)*
- **Definition:** In protocol order: composite renal response (eGFR reduction <=15% and uPCR reduction >=50%); >=50% uPCR response; adult evaluable-biopsy change in C3G histologic-index activity score (all data, including adolescents, are summarized); adult evaluable-biopsy >=2-order reduction in C3c staining; eGFR change from baseline. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §§5.2.1,12.3.2.1, pages 103,147-149)*
- **Measurement Units:** Binary responder proportions/odds ratios; histologic activity score and C3c staining; eGFR mL/min/1.73m2. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.3.2.1, pages 147-149)*
- **Timing of Measurement:** Week 26; primary/key/additional endpoints are evaluated at week 52 as exploratory endpoints. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §5.2.1, pages 102-104)*
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** uPCR response uses baseline and week-26 averages from >=6/9 FMU samples; eGFR baseline is last nonmissing predose assessment. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.3.2.1; standalone SAP §6.5.1, pages 148,225)*

**Outcome: Additional secondary endpoints at week 26** *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §5.2.1, pages 103-104)*
- **Definition:** Proteinuria <1 g/day; albumin normalization for baseline below LLN; serum-C3 normalization for baseline below LLN; changes in FACIT-Fatigue and KDQOL. The final protocol calls these week-26 endpoints; the earlier standalone SAP estimand table labels proteinuria <1 g/day at week 24. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §§5.2.1,12.3.2.2, pages 103-104,149-150; standalone SAP §6.3, page 221)*
- **Measurement Units:** Binary responder proportions/odds ratios; FACIT-Fatigue and KDQOL scores. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.3.2.2, pages 149-150)*
- **Timing of Measurement:** Week 26, with week-52 exploratory evaluation. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §5.2.1, pages 102-104)*
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Albumin/C3 baseline and week-26 values average up to two measurements; baseline-below-LLN participants only; >=LLN at week 26 is success. Proteinuria <1g/day follows composite-renal logistic approach; albumin/C3 logistic models adjust respectively for baseline albumin/C3, disease type and stratification. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.3.2.2, pages 149-150)*

### 5.3 Analysis Methods

- **Primary Analysis Methods:** ITT primary efficacy analysis uses MMRM with treatment, visit, disease type, stratification factors, treatment-by-visit, baseline log-uPCR; unstructured covariance initially, alternate structures if no convergence; MI results combine using Rubin’s rule. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.3.1, page 146; final SAP Version 2.0 §§4,6, pages 286-290)*
- **Covariate Adjustment:** Composite response adjusts baseline eGFR, baseline log-uPCR, disease type, stratification; uPCR response adjusts baseline uPCR, disease type, stratification; biopsy/C3c and continuous endpoints use the specified baseline endpoint, disease type and stratification factors. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §§12.3.2.1-12.3.2.2, pages 147-150)*
- **Assumption Checks:** Investigate unstructured covariance; if failure to converge, use other covariance structures specified in SAP. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §§12.3.1-12.3.2, pages 146,149)*
- **Alternative Methods if Assumptions Fail:** SAP’s slope mixed model falls back from unstructured to ARH(1), AR(1), then CS covariance; uses Kenward-Roger denominator degrees of freedom and sandwich SEs for non-unstructured covariance. Exact or penalized logistic approaches address sparse binary outcomes. *(Source: standalone SAP §6.1, pages 218-219)*
- **Sensitivity Analyses:** Primary: MAR MMRM and MNAR delta-adjusted tipping-point MI; PP repetition and slope analysis. Key endpoints also have prespecified PP, sensitivity, slope/supplemental and subgroup analyses as applicable. *(Source: standalone SAP §§6.4.2-6.5.5, pages 223-239)*
- **Subgroup Analyses:** Primary endpoint by age, sex, race, region, disease type, transplant history, baseline FMU uPCR/eGFR/C3, with low-count levels pooled as appropriate; forest plots report treatment effects/CIs/P values. *(Source: Standalone SAP §6.4.4, page 225)*

### 5.4 Missing Data

- **Missing Data Assumptions:** For primary endpoint, nonmonotone uPCR missingness is imputed with MCMC before monotone MI. ProhibiRescue is hypothetical/reference-based; RenalReplace is composite/worst-change plus random error; DisctTrt is treatment-policy/reference-based. MAR and MNAR tipping-point analyses assess robustness. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §§12.3.1, pages 146-147; standalone SAP §§6.3-6.4.2, pages 220-224)*
- **Handling Methods:** Primary: 100 imputed data sets, SAS seed 230815, MMRM each then Rubin pooling. Binary composite/uPCR/albumin/C3 endpoints treat each ICE and missing week-26 response as nonresponse. Continuous endpoints map ProhibiRescue to hypothetical, RenalReplace to composite, and DisctTrt to treatment policy. *(Source: standalone SAP §§6.3-6.4.1, pages 220-223)*

### 5.5 Additional Analyses
Exploratory endpoints include week-52 primary/key/additional evaluations; 24-hour-uPCR change, annual historical eGFR slope, >=30% and normalization proteinuria, time-to-50% response/time-to-normalization, macrophage count, PGIC, EQ-5D-5L, WPAI, and correlation of uPCR reduction with eGFR. Final SAP Version 2.0 removed normalization of hematuria. Amendment 4 removed the week-26 drusen endpoint; if ophthalmology is conducted it is baseline and weeks 42-52. PK concentrations are summarized over time in the PK set with BLQ conventions; PD values/changes/% changes use descriptive summaries and MMRM; immunogenicity is analyzed; population PK/exposure-response work follows a separate plan. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 amendment summary, page 80; Protocol Amendment 4 §§5.2.1-5.2.3,12.3.3,12.5-12.6, pages 103-105,150-152; final SAP Version 2.0 revision history, pages 267,271-272; standalone SAP §§6.7-10, pages 240-248)*

### 5.6 Harms

- **Adverse Event Definitions:** A TEAE is new or worsened after first dose. AE analysis is restricted to TEAEs; special-interest events include thrombocytopenia, severe infection, hypersensitivity/pneumonitis and severe acute kidney injury. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §§11.2.1,11.2.5, pages 140-144; standalone SAP §7.1, pages 242-243)*
- **Safety Analysis Methods:** Safety set; current MedDRA coding. Tabulate TEAEs by SOC/PT/treatment, exposure-adjusted incidence, infusion-related/treatment-related events, maximum severity/strongest causality, dose interruption/withdrawal/discontinuation/death and SAE categories; list onset, duration, relation, severity, action, treatment and outcome. Tabulate transplant rejection/graft loss/death; summarize labs, ADA, ECG and vital signs by treatment. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §12.4, pages 150-151; standalone SAP §7, pages 242-248)*
- **Grading Scales and Causality:** Investigator assesses relationship and severity: mild, moderate, severe definitions; severity differs from seriousness. All SAEs are reported within 24 hours. *(Source: Protocol Amendment 4 §§11.2.2.1-11.2.3, pages 141-143)*
