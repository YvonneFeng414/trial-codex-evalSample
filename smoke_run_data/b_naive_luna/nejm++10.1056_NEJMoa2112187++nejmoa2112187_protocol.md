# Clinical Study Protocol and Statistical Analysis Framework

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Background and Rationale

- **[Provided]** Patients with recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer progressing after platinum-based chemotherapy have poor outcomes and no widely accepted subsequent systemic standard. This pivotal phase 3 study compares cemiplimab with investigator’s-choice (IC) chemotherapy.

### 1.2 Objectives

- **[Provided]** **Primary objective (P1):** Compare overall survival (OS) for cemiplimab versus IC chemotherapy, first among patients with squamous cell carcinoma (SCC), then in the overall eligible population (SCC or adenocarcinoma/adenosquamous carcinoma).
- **[Provided]** **Primary outcome (P1):** OS for cemiplimab versus IC chemotherapy, first in SCC patients, then in the overall eligible population.
- **[Provided]** **Secondary objective (S1):** Compare progression-free survival (PFS), objective response rate (ORR) by RECIST 1.1, duration of response (DoR), adverse-event profiles, and EORTC QLQ-C30 global-health-status/quality-of-life (GHS/QoL) and physical-functioning mean change from baseline, in SCC and overall populations.
- **[Provided]** **Secondary outcomes (S1):** PFS, ORR by RECIST 1.1, DoR, adverse-event profiles, and EORTC QLQ-C30 GHS/QoL and physical-functioning mean change from baseline, in SCC and overall populations.
- **[Provided]** **Exploratory objective (S2):** Characterize serum cemiplimab pharmacokinetics (PK) and immunogenicity; explore associations of efficacy with pretreatment-tumor molecular features and peripheral-blood pharmacodynamic activity.
- **[Provided]** **Exploratory outcomes (S2):** Serum cemiplimab PK and immunogenicity; associations of efficacy with pretreatment-tumor molecular features and peripheral-blood pharmacodynamic activity.

## 2. Study Methods

### 2.1 Trial Design

- **[Provided]** This is an open-label, randomized, multicentre phase 3 trial with screening, treatment, follow-up, and subsequent survival follow-up. There is no treatment crossover.
- **[Provided]** Participants are randomized 1:1 to cemiplimab or IC chemotherapy. Treatment is planned for up to 96 weeks.
- **[Provided]** Cemiplimab is administered at 350 mg intravenously every 3 weeks.
- **[Provided]** IC chemotherapy is one of pemetrexed, topotecan, irinotecan, gemcitabine, or vinorelbine, with drug selection and dosing according to the following schedules:
  - **[Provided]** Pemetrexed: 500 mg/m² intravenously on day 1 every 21 days, with folate/vitamin B12 support.
  - **[Provided]** Topotecan: 1 mg/m² intravenously daily for 5 days every 21 days; the dose may increase to 1.25 mg/m² after the first course without significant toxicity.
  - **[Provided]** Irinotecan: 100 mg/m² intravenously weekly for 4 doses, followed by 10–14 days’ rest (at least 14 days in Japan); the dose may increase to 125 mg/m² after the first course without significant toxicity.
  - **[Provided]** Gemcitabine: 1000 mg/m² intravenously on days 1 and 8 every 21 days.
  - **[Provided]** Vinorelbine: 30 mg/m² intravenously on days 1 and 8 every 21 days.
- **[Provided]** Cemiplimab and IC treatment continue until 96 weeks, RECIST 1.1 progression, unacceptable toxicity, death, or consent withdrawal. Cemiplimab may continue beyond RECIST progression only under protocol criteria.
- **[Provided]** During follow-up, a patient with progressive disease (PD) may be retreated with the same originally assigned drug; IC selection and each drug’s dose and schedule are protocol specified.
- **[Provided]** Treatment starts within 5 days after randomization, or within 10 days for pemetrexed, and is organized in 6-week cycles.
- **[Clarification Required]** The protocol criteria for cemiplimab continuation beyond RECIST progression and retreatment after PD are not supplied. These criteria matter for consistent treatment decisions and interpretation of post-progression exposure.

### 2.2 Randomization

- **[Provided]** Treatment assignment is made through IWRS after the investigator deems the patient eligible.
- **[Provided]** Randomization is 1:1.
- **[Provided]** For the SCC analysis, stratification factors are geographic region (North America, Asia-Pacific, rest of world [ROW]).
- **[Provided]** For the overall-population analysis, stratification factors are region and histology (SCC versus adenocarcinoma; adenosquamous carcinoma is classified as adenocarcinoma).
- **[Provided]** After Amendment 5, only SCC patients were randomized. Before Amendment 5, patients were stratified by SCC versus adenocarcinoma/adenosquamous histology; this historical distinction is retained in the overall-population analysis.
- **[Provided]** Minimization or blocking procedures are not specified.
- **[Clarification Required]** The randomization sequence-generation and allocation-concealment procedures, and whether blocks or minimization were used, are not supplied. These details matter for documenting implementation and assessing whether assignment could have been anticipated.

### 2.3 Sample Size

- **[Provided]** Planned enrollment is 460 randomized SCC patients, with approximately 590 total patients projected.
- **[Provided]** Sample size is based on OS in SCC patients, a time-to-event outcome, with assumed median OS of 7 months for IC chemotherapy and 10 months for cemiplimab.
- **[Provided]** The target effect is a hazard ratio (HR) of 0.70, corresponding to an approximately 42.8% increase in median OS under exponential distributions.
- **[Provided]** The sample-size calculation assumes exponential OS distributions in both groups, a 10% annual dropout rate, and 340 OS events in SCC patients.
- **[Provided]** The design targets approximately 90% power for HR 0.70 with an overall one-sided alpha of 0.025. The assumed test is a group-sequential, stratified log-rank OS test with Lan-DeMets (O’Brien-Fleming) alpha spending.
- **[Provided]** A non-inferiority or equivalence margin is not applicable because the study tests superiority. Design effect or clustering is not applicable because this is an individually randomized trial.
- **[Provided]** The assumptions were informed by phase 2 reports of median OS of 6.5–8.1 months for IC chemotherapy and enrollment assumptions of 2 patients/month during months 1–5, 9/month during months 6–16, 20/month during months 17–23, then 22/month.
- **[Provided]** SAP v2.0 updated sample size, interim analysis, target population, and enrollment rate for protocol amendments through Amendment 6. A formal sample-size re-estimation procedure is not specified.
- **[Provided]** East version 6.4.1 was used for the sample-size calculation.
- **[Provided]** The rationale is that 460 SCC patients are expected to yield 340 events about 42 months after the first SCC randomization; overall-population power is projected to exceed 90% if HR = 0.70.
- **[Clarification Required]** The formal sample-size re-estimation procedure, if any, is not specified. This matters because any change to planned enrollment or event targets should be distinguishable from the original design assumptions.

### 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

- **[Provided]** The null hypothesis is that cemiplimab and IC chemotherapy have the same OS survival curve; the alternative hypothesis is that cemiplimab is superior.
- **[Provided]** OS is tested first in SCC and, only if significant, in the overall eligible population.
- **[Provided]** The overall one-sided alpha is 0.025, with a group-sequential stratified log-rank test and Lan-DeMets (O’Brien-Fleming) alpha spending assumed for the OS design.
- **[Clarification Required]** The precise allocation of type I error across the sequential SCC and overall-population tests, including how group-sequential spending applies to each, is not specified. This matters to the confirmatory interpretation of p-values and the claimed family-wise type I error control.

### 2.5 Statistical Interim Analyses

- **[Provided]** The sample-size assumptions specify a group-sequential, stratified log-rank OS test using Lan-DeMets (O’Brien-Fleming) alpha spending, and SAP v2.0 updated interim-analysis information.
- **[Clarification Required]** The number, timing, event information fractions, exact efficacy boundaries, and any futility rules for interim analyses are not supplied. These decisions matter for monitoring the trial and controlling type I error.
- **[Clarification Required]** The identity and responsibilities of any independent monitoring committee, and access to unblinded interim results, are not specified. This matters for oversight and protection of trial integrity.

### 2.6 Timing of Analyses

- **[Provided]** The final OS analysis cutoff is when 340 SCC OS events are observed. Events through the cutoff are included; patients otherwise are censored at the earlier of the last-known-alive date or the cutoff.
- **[Provided]** CT/MRI assessments occur on day 42 (±7 days) of cycles 1–4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, and 16. After treatment, imaging occurs every 90 days (±10) until PD for patients without prior PD.
- **[Provided]** EORTC QLQ-C30 is completed at screening and on day 1 of treatment cycles (it may be completed at another visit in the same cycle), before procedures, and at post-treatment follow-up visits 1 and 2.
- **[Provided]** Follow-up visit 1 is approximately 30 days (±10) after the last dose; visit 2 is approximately 90 days (±10) later. Survival ascertainment then occurs every 90 days (±10) until death or completion.
- **[Clarification Required]** Database-lock and data-readiness requirements are not specified. This matters for defining the operational point at which the final analysis dataset is considered complete and suitable for analysis.

## 3. Statistical Principles

### 3.1 Significance and Confidence Intervals

- **[Provided]** The OS design uses an overall one-sided alpha of 0.025 and approximately 90% power for HR 0.70 in the SCC population at 340 SCC OS events.
- **[Proposed]** Report effect estimates with confidence intervals and p-values alongside the prespecified hierarchical OS tests. **Rationale:** estimates and uncertainty provide information beyond a significance decision. **Assumption:** two-sided 95% confidence intervals are suitable for descriptive reporting alongside the one-sided 0.025 confirmatory test; the confirmatory decision remains governed by the prespecified sequential procedure.
- **[Clarification Required]** Confidence-interval methods and the reporting convention for secondary and exploratory analyses are not specified. This matters for consistent interpretation, particularly where multiple outcomes are assessed.

### 3.2 Protocol Deviations

- **[Proposed]** Record protocol deviations before unblinding for analysis, classify their nature and potential impact, and summarize them by randomized group. **Rationale:** transparent deviation reporting supports interpretation of the randomized comparison. **Assumption:** deviation categories and review procedures can be defined in the statistical analysis plan without changing the protocol’s eligibility or treatment rules.
- **[Proposed]** Do not exclude randomized participants from the primary randomized-group OS analysis solely because of a protocol deviation. **Rationale:** retaining randomized assignment preserves the randomized comparison. **Assumption:** the primary estimand is the effect of assignment to treatment, regardless of subsequent deviations.
- **[Clarification Required]** The criteria for identifying major deviations and any formal per-protocol analysis population are not supplied. This matters for reproducible population definitions and sensitivity analyses.

### 3.3 Analysis Populations

- **[Proposed]** Define the primary efficacy population as all randomized patients, analyzed according to assigned treatment, separately for the SCC population and the overall eligible population. **Rationale:** this retains the randomized comparison for the primary OS objective. **Assumption:** randomized patients are included regardless of treatment received or protocol deviations.
- **[Proposed]** Define a safety population as all randomized patients who receive at least one dose of study treatment, analyzed according to treatment received. **Rationale:** safety summaries should reflect treatment exposure. **Assumption:** at least one dose is the relevant exposure threshold.
- **[Proposed]** Analyze the SCC population as randomized SCC patients and the overall eligible population as randomized patients with SCC, adenocarcinoma, or adenosquamous carcinoma, retaining the amendment-era enrollment and histology classification described for randomization. **Rationale:** this matches the stated primary objectives and historical design. **Assumption:** histology and amendment status are available in the analysis dataset.
- **[Proposed]** Use available data for PK, immunogenicity, molecular-feature, and pharmacodynamic analyses, with analysis sets defined according to availability of the relevant measurements. **Rationale:** these exploratory objectives depend on specialized specimens or assays. **Assumption:** these measurements may not be available for every randomized patient.
- **[Clarification Required]** Formal definitions of the efficacy, safety, PK, immunogenicity, and biomarker analysis populations are not supplied. This matters because differing inclusion rules can change denominators and analysis interpretation.

### 4.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

- **[Provided]** Inclusion criteria are women aged 18 years or older with recurrent, persistent, or metastatic noncurable cervical SCC; adenocarcinoma/adenosquamous carcinoma was allowed before Amendment 5.
- **[Provided]** Patients must have progressed after platinum specifically for recurrent/persistent/metastatic cancer, not after curative chemoradiation.
- **[Provided]** Patients must have a RECIST 1.1 measurable lesion: at least 10 mm by CT/MRI/caliper or at least 20 mm by chest radiograph; lymph nodes must be greater than 15 mm in short axis.
- **[Provided]** ECOG performance status must be 0 or 1.
- **[Provided]** Laboratory inclusion thresholds are bilirubin ≤1.5× ULN, or ≤3× ULN with liver metastases; transaminases ≤3× ULN, or ≤5× ULN with liver metastases; alkaline phosphatase ≤2.5× ULN, or ≤5× ULN with liver/bone metastases; and creatinine ≤1.5× ULN or creatinine clearance >45 mL/min.
- **[Provided]** Hemoglobin must be ≥9 g/dL, absolute neutrophil count ≥1.5×10⁹/L, and platelets ≥75×10⁹/L.
- **[Provided]** Other inclusion criteria are life expectancy greater than 12 weeks; ability to comply with study requirements and complete questionnaires; informed consent; and required prior bevacizumab/paclitaxel status or exceptions.
- **[Provided]** Exclusion criteria include significant systemic-treatment autoimmune disease; prior PD-1/PD-L1 therapy; immune-modulator use within 4 weeks or recent associated toxicity; active/unstable CNS metastasis; more than 10 mg prednisone-equivalent immunosuppression within 4 weeks; active serious infection/HIV/HBV/HCV; pneumonitis within 5 years; anticancer therapy within 30 days or radiation within 14 days; relevant allergy; concurrent/recent malignancy; transplant; pregnancy/breastfeeding; and failure to use required contraception.
- **[Clarification Required]** Detailed rules for required prior bevacizumab/paclitaxel status or exceptions are not supplied. This matters for determining eligibility consistently.
- **[Clarification Required]** Operational definitions and any additional details for the summarized exclusion criteria are not supplied. This matters for consistent screening and eligibility adjudication.

## 5. Analysis

### 5.1 Primary Outcomes

- **[Provided]** The primary outcome is OS, compared between cemiplimab and IC chemotherapy first in SCC patients and then in the overall eligible population.
- **[Proposed]** Define OS as time from randomization to death from any cause, with patients not known to have died censored at the earlier of their last-known-alive date or the analysis cutoff. **Rationale:** this provides an operational time-to-event definition consistent with the supplied censoring rule. **Assumption:** randomization is the time origin and death from any cause is the event.
- **[Provided]** For the final OS analysis, events through the cutoff at 340 SCC OS events are included; otherwise, censoring is at the earlier of last-known-alive date or cutoff.
- **[Clarification Required]** The supplied final cutoff is event-driven for SCC; any separate cutoff or event target for the overall-population analysis is not specified. This matters for defining the overall analysis dataset.

### 5.2 Secondary Outcomes

- **[Provided]** Secondary outcomes are PFS, ORR by RECIST 1.1, DoR, adverse-event profiles, and EORTC QLQ-C30 GHS/QoL and physical-functioning mean change from baseline in SCC and overall populations.
- **[Proposed]** Define PFS as time from randomization to RECIST 1.1 progression or death, whichever occurs first. **Rationale:** this provides a standard operational definition for the stated progression-free survival endpoint. **Assumption:** death is an event regardless of whether it follows documented progression.
- **[Proposed]** Define ORR as the proportion of patients with a RECIST 1.1 complete or partial response. **Rationale:** complete and partial response provide an operational interpretation of objective response. **Assumption:** response is assessed using RECIST 1.1. **[Clarification Required]** Whether responses must be confirmed, the assessment rule for patients without post-baseline imaging, and the specific ORR analysis denominator are not specified. These decisions matter for response classification and rate estimates.
- **[Proposed]** Define DoR among responders as time from the first documented response to RECIST 1.1 progression or death. **Rationale:** this makes the duration endpoint operational. **Assumption:** response and progression dates are available. **[Clarification Required]** The handling of responders without subsequent progression or death is not specified. - **[Provided]** Imaging is scheduled by CT/MRI on day 42 (±7 days) of cycles 1–4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, and 16; after treatment, imaging is every 90 days (±10) until PD for patients without prior PD.

- **[Provided]** The SCC analysis is stratified by geographic region (North America, Asia-Pacific, ROW); the overall analysis is stratified by region and histology, with adenosquamous carcinoma classified as adenocarcinoma.

- **[Proposed]** Analyze PFS using time-to-event methods, ORR using between-group response proportions, and DoR using time-to-event methods among responders. **Rationale:** these methods align with the respective endpoint types. **Assumption:** endpoint definitions, analysis sets, censoring rules, and denominators are finalized before database analysis.
