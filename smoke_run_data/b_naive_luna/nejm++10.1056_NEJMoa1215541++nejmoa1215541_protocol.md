# Clinical Study Protocol and Statistical Analysis Framework

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Background and Rationale

- **[Provided]** This Phase III study evaluates once-daily dolutegravir (DTG; GSK1349572) plus abacavir/lamivudine (ABC/3TC) versus fixed-dose efavirenz/emtricitabine/tenofovir (Atripla) in antiretroviral therapy (ART)-naïve adults with HIV-1.
- **[Provided]** The study aims to establish non-inferior antiviral activity and characterize longer-term safety, tolerability, resistance, and health outcomes. DTG 50 mg once daily was selected based on Phase 2b dose-ranging data.

### 1.2 Objectives

- **[Provided]** **Primary objective (P1):** Demonstrate antiviral activity of once-daily DTG plus ABC/3TC fixed-dose combination (FDC) versus Atripla over 48 weeks in HIV-1-infected, ART-naïve subjects. The stated outcome is antiviral activity over 48 weeks.
- **[Provided]** **Secondary objective (S1):** Demonstrate antiviral activity through Week 96. The stated outcome is antiviral activity through Week 96.
- **[Provided]** **Secondary objective (S2):** Evaluate antiviral activity through Week 144. The stated outcome is antiviral activity through Week 144.
- **[Provided]** **Secondary objective (S3):** Compare tolerability, long-term safety, antiviral activity, and immunologic activity over time. The stated outcome comprises those measures over time.
- **[Provided]** **Secondary objective (S4):** Assess development of viral resistance in subjects experiencing virologic failure. The stated outcome is development of viral resistance in those subjects.
- **[Provided]** **Secondary objective (S5):** Assess change in symptom bother count. The stated outcome is change in symptom bother count.
- **[Provided]** **Secondary objective (S6):** Evaluate incidence of HIV-associated conditions over time. The stated outcome is incidence of HIV-associated conditions over time.
- **[Provided]** **Secondary objective (S7):** Explore the impact of gender, race, and/or HIV-1 subtype on response. The stated outcome is response by gender, race, and/or HIV-1 subtype.
- **[Provided]** **Secondary objective (S8):** Explore change in utility and health-related quality of life. The stated outcome is change in utility and health-related quality of life.
- **[Provided]** All stated objectives are designated primary or secondary; exploratory objectives are not reported. Exploratory analyses are described as analyses rather than objectives.
- **[Clarification Required]** The operational definitions and assessment instruments for several stated outcomes are not supplied. These need to be specified to ensure consistent measurement and interpretation, particularly for antiviral activity, symptom bother, HIV-associated conditions, utility, and health-related quality of life.

## 2. Study Methods

### 2.1 Trial Design

- **[Provided]** The study is a Phase III, multicentre, randomized, stratified, double-blind, double-dummy, active-controlled, parallel-group, fully powered non-inferiority study.
- **[Provided]** The double-blind randomized phase runs from Day 1 through Week 96. Amendment 05 provides an open-label randomized phase through Week 144 and a DTG continuation phase thereafter.
- **[Provided]** Approximately 800 subjects are to be randomized in a 1:1 allocation, approximately 400 per arm.
- **[Provided]** Once-daily dosing is double-dummy and preferably fasting or at bedtime:
  - **[Provided]** The DTG + ABC/3TC arm receives DTG 50 mg and ABC/3TC 600/300 mg tablets, plus Atripla placebo.
  - **[Provided]** The Atripla arm receives EFV/FTC/TDF 600/200/300 mg, plus DTG and ABC/3TC placebos.
- **[Clarification Required]** Details of treatment conduct after Week 144, including the DTG continuation phase’s eligibility, treatment, and follow-up procedures, are not supplied. These decisions matter for interpreting post-Week-144 exposure and outcomes.

### 2.2 Randomization

- **[Provided]** After entry criteria are confirmed, central computer-generated randomization uses GSK-validated RANDALL/RandAll software to generate the schedule.
- **[Provided]** Randomization is stratified by screening plasma HIV-1 RNA (≤100,000 versus >100,000 c/mL) and screening CD4+ count (≤200 versus >200 cells/mm3), forming four strata.
- **[Provided]** A fixed block size is used and remains confidential. Minimization is not specified.
- **[Clarification Required]** The fixed block size and operational allocation-concealment procedures are not given. The block size may appropriately remain confidential, but the concealment procedures are needed to assess implementation of randomization.

### 2.3 Sample Size

- **[Provided]** The recruitment target is approximately 800 randomized subjects, approximately 400 per arm; the required evaluable sample is 394 subjects per arm.
- **[Provided]** The calculation is based on the proportion with plasma HIV-1 RNA <50 c/mL at Week 48, a binary virologic response rate.
- **[Provided]** The assumed Atripla response rate is 75%; the same 75% rate is used for non-inferiority planning, and a distinct DTG response-rate assumption is not specified.
- **[Provided]** The non-inferiority margin is −10 percentage points for the DTG-minus-Atripla response-rate difference. The calculation uses one-sided alpha 2.5%, 90% power, and the lower bound of a two-sided 95% confidence interval (CI) compared with −10%.
- **[Provided]** No separate variance assumption is specified beyond the binomial response-rate calculation. Required events are not applicable because the design is powered on a binary response rate rather than an event-driven time-to-event outcome.
- **[Provided]** No numerical dropout/non-adherence inflation is specified. The 75% planning response incorporates historical context of approximately 15% drug-related and 14% non-drug-related failure.
- **[Provided]** The basis for assumptions includes historical Atripla-controlled and GSK trials, an approximate control response of 71%, a recent EFV-arm range of 71%–82%, and anticipated better non-drug compliance.
- **[Provided]** No sample-size re-estimation will be performed. A separate design effect is not applicable because this is an individual randomized parallel-group trial without a cluster design.
- **[Provided]** The stated rationale is that 394 evaluable subjects per arm provides 90% power with a 75% control response, a 10% non-inferiority margin, and one-sided 2.5% significance.
- **[Clarification Required]** Calculation software is not specified. This matters for reproducibility of the sample-size calculation.

### 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

- **[Provided]** Let \(p_{\mathrm{DTG}}\) and \(p_{\mathrm{Atripla}}\) denote the Week-48 response proportions. The null hypothesis is \(H_0: p_{\mathrm{DTG}} - p_{\mathrm{Atripla}} \leq -10\%\); the alternative is \(H_1: p_{\mathrm{DTG}} - p_{\mathrm{Atripla}} > -10\%\).
- **[Provided]** DTG is non-inferior if the lower bound of the two-sided 95% CI for the response-rate difference is greater than −10%.
- **[Provided]** If both ITT-E and PP analyses demonstrate non-inferiority, ITT-E superiority is tested two-sided at 5%; superiority is declared if the lower 95% CI bound exceeds 0.
- **[Provided]** Conditional on non-inferiority, key secondary superiority tests are generalized Wilcoxon for time to suppression, a repeated-measures mixed model for Week-48 CD4, and ANCOVA for Week-4 SDM bother, in a specified fallback sequence.
- **[Clarification Required]** The exact fallback order, hypotheses, and multiplicity-control rules for the key secondary tests are not supplied. These are needed to determine which tests may support confirmatory conclusions and how family-wise error is controlled.

### 2.5 Statistical Interim Analyses

- **[Provided]** No sample-size re-estimation will be performed.
- **[Clarification Required]** Whether any interim efficacy, futility, or safety analyses are planned is not specified. This matters for oversight, potential early decisions, and interpretation of the final significance level.

### 2.6 Timing of Analyses

- **[Provided]** The final analysis is to occur once all subjects have completed or discontinued; Week 48 is the primary analysis time point.
- **[Provided]** In the final amended protocol, HIV-1 RNA assessments occur at screening, Day 1, Weeks 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 40, and 48, and every 12 weeks thereafter through Week 144.
- **[Provided]** Amendment 05 documents Week-144 open-label and continuation conduct; actual dates allocate analysis windows.
- **[Clarification Required]** Timing for CD4/CD8, SDM, EQ-5D, and safety assessments is not specified. It is needed to define the longitudinal analyses and analysis windows for these outcomes.
- **[Clarification Required]** The data cutoff, database-lock requirements, and data-readiness requirements are not specified. These decisions are needed to establish reproducible analysis timing and the final analysis dataset.

## 3. Statistical Principles

### 3.1 Significance and Confidence Intervals

- **[Provided]** The non-inferiority assessment uses the lower bound of a two-sided 95% CI for the response-rate difference and compares it with the −10 percentage-point margin; the sample-size calculation uses one-sided alpha 2.5%.
- **[Provided]** Following non-inferiority in both ITT-E and PP analyses, ITT-E superiority is assessed two-sided at 5%, with superiority declared if the lower 95% CI bound exceeds 0.
- **[Proposed]** Report treatment-group estimates, between-group differences, and two-sided 95% CIs for the primary response outcome. **Rationale:** This presents the magnitude and precision needed to apply the stated decision rule. **Assumption:** The Week-48 response-rate difference is the primary inferential contrast.
- **[Clarification Required]** The CI construction method and handling of the randomization strata in the primary estimate are not specified. These choices can affect the estimate and its uncertainty and should be prespecified.

### 3.2 Protocol Deviations

- **[Proposed]** Classify deviations using prespecified categories, including major deviations relevant to eligibility, study treatment, outcome assessment, and analysis population membership; document decisions before treatment codes are unblinded where feasible. **Rationale:** Consistent classification supports a credible PP analysis and reduces outcome-informed exclusions. **Assumption:** A blinded review process can be implemented before unblinding.
- **[Clarification Required]** The criteria for major protocol deviations and their consequences for analysis-set membership are not supplied. These decisions matter because the PP population is part of the non-inferiority decision.

### 3.3 Analysis Populations

- **[Provided]** ITT-E and PP analyses are both required to demonstrate non-inferiority before ITT-E superiority testing.
- **[Proposed]** Define the ITT-E population using a prespecified rule based on randomization and treatment exposure, and analyze subjects according to randomized assignment. **Rationale:** Prespecified population rules reduce post-randomization selection bias. **Assumption:** The “E” designation represents an exposure requirement; its exact meaning must be confirmed.
- **[Proposed]** Define the PP population as subjects who meet eligibility requirements, receive the assigned study regimen, and have no prespecified major deviation likely to affect the primary endpoint; specify exclusions without reference to observed outcomes. **Rationale:** This provides an interpretable adherent population for the required non-inferiority assessment. **Assumption:** These general criteria are appropriate pending confirmation of study-specific rules.
- **[Clarification Required]** Exact ITT-E and PP definitions, including treatment exposure requirements and rules for post-randomization exclusions, are not supplied. They are essential for reproducing the primary analysis and the non-inferiority decision.

## 4. Trial Population

### 4.1 Screening Data

- **[Provided]** Screening HIV-1 RNA must be ≥1000 c/mL. Screening plasma HIV-1 RNA and CD4+ count also determine the two randomization stratification factors.
- **[Provided]** One re-screen with a new number and one repeat test per analyte or assessment are permitted. Genotype retesting is not permitted.
- **[Proposed]** Summarize the numbers screened, eligible, randomized, and not randomized, with reasons for non-enrollment where available. **Rationale:** A screening disposition summary describes recruitment and selection. **Assumption:** These data are collected during screening.
- **[Clarification Required]** The permitted time interval and procedures for re-screening and repeat testing are not specified. These details matter for consistent eligibility decisions.

### 4.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

- **[Provided]** **Inclusion criteria:**
  - **[Provided]** Able to understand and comply with study requirements, expected to complete the study as planned, and considered an appropriate candidate for oral investigational therapy.
  - **[Provided]** HIV-1-infected adults aged ≥18 years.
  - **[Provided]** Screening HIV-1 RNA ≥1000 c/mL.
  - **[Provided]** ART-naïve, defined as ≤10 prior ART days after diagnosis.
  - **[Provided]** HLA-B*5701 negative.
  - **[Provided]** Dated written informed consent.
  - **[Provided]** Women must be non-child-bearing—post-menopausal with 12 months of amenorrhea and age ≥45 years, or with documented sterilization—or have negative screening and Day-1 pregnancy tests and consistently use protocol-specified contraception from two weeks before investigational product (IP) through two weeks after it.
  - **[Provided]** Permitted contraception methods include abstinence, double barrier, a qualifying intrauterine device or other method with <1% failure, or hormonal contraception plus a barrier method.
  - **[Provided]** French enrollment additionally requires social-security affiliation or beneficiary status.
- **[Provided]** **Exclusion criteria:**
  - **[Provided]** Breastfeeding.
  - **[Provided]** Active CDC Category C disease, except cutaneous Kaposi sarcoma not requiring systemic therapy; historical or current CD4 <200 is not exclusionary.
  - **[Provided]** Hepatic impairment.
  - **[Provided]** HBsAg positivity or anticipated HCV therapy.
  - **[Provided]** Recent gastrointestinal bleed.
  - **[Provided]** Allergy or intolerance to study drugs or drug class.
  - **[Provided]** Recent or ongoing malignancy, except specified skin and Kaposi sarcoma exceptions.
  - **[Provided]** Recent HIV immunotherapeutic vaccine, radiation, cytotoxic chemotherapy, immunomodulator, HIV-active agent, or experimental drug/vaccine, including a France-specific restriction.
  - **[Provided]** Primary resistance.
  - **[Provided]** Verified Grade-4 or acute disqualifying laboratory abnormality.
  - **[Provided]** ALT/bilirubin thresholds apply, but numerical thresholds are not specified.
  - **[Provided]** CrCl <50 mL/min by Cockcroft–Gault.
- **[Clarification Required]** The definitions of “recent,” hepatic impairment, and the specified malignancy and skin/Kaposi exceptions are not supplied. These terms affect eligibility and require operational definitions.
- **[Clarification Required]** Numerical ALT/bilirubin thresholds, the definition of primary resistance, the applicable Grade-4 or acute laboratory criteria, and the France-specific restriction are not supplied. They matter for consistent screening and enrollment decisions.

### 4.3 Recruitment

- **[Provided]** The study is multicentre.
- **[Clarification Required]** Recruitment locations, recruitment period, and enrollment targets by site or region are not specified. These details are needed for operational planning and interpretation of recruitment patterns.

### 4.4 Withdrawal and Follow-up

- **[Provided]** The final analysis is planned once all subjects have completed or discontinued.
- **[Clarification Required]** Withdrawal categories, treatment-discontinuation procedures, follow-up after discontinuation, and the handling of withdrawal of consent are not specified. These decisions affect endpoint availability and analysis populations.

### 4.5 Baseline Characteristics

- **[Provided]** Screening HIV-1 RNA and CD4+ count are used as stratification factors, with cut points of ≤100,000 versus >100,000 c/mL and ≤200 versus >200 cells/mm3, respectively.

## 5. Analysis

### 5.1 Primary Outcomes

- **[Provided]** The primary objective is to demonstrate antiviral activity of once-daily DTG plus ABC/3TC FDC versus Atripla over 48 weeks.
- **[Provided]** The sample-size calculation uses the proportion of subjects with plasma HIV-1 RNA <50 c/mL at Week 48.
- **[Clarification Required]** Whether the Week-48 proportion with HIV-1 RNA <50 c/mL is the formal primary endpoint, and how response is determined when a Week-48 measurement is absent or outside the assessment window, are not explicitly specified. These decisions define the primary analysis outcome.

### 5.2 Secondary Outcomes

- **[Provided]** Secondary outcomes correspond to antiviral activity through Weeks 96 and 144; tolerability, long-term safety, antiviral and immunologic activity over time; viral resistance in subjects with virologic failure; change in symptom bother count; incidence of HIV-associated conditions over time; response by gender, race, and/or HIV-1 subtype; and change in utility and health-related quality of life.
- **[Provided]** The conditional key secondary superiority tests include time to suppression by generalized Wilcoxon, Week-48 CD4 by repeated-measures mixed model, and Week-4 SDM bother by ANCOVA.
- **[Clarification Required]** Endpoint definitions, time points, assessment windows, and analysis contrasts for most secondary outcomes are not specified. They are required to operationalize the objectives and interpret the results.
- **[Clarification Required]** The criterion for virologic failure and the timing and methods for resistance assessment are not specified. They are needed to define the population in which resistance is evaluated.
- **[Provided]** EQ-5D is named among the assessments whose timing is not specified.
- **[Clarification Required]** The specific utility and health-related quality-of-life measures, including how EQ-5D is to be used, are not fully defined. This matters for selecting scoring and analysis methods.

### 5.3 Analysis Methods

- **[Proposed]** For the primary binary response outcome, estimate the DTG-minus-Atripla difference in response proportions with a two-sided 95% CI and apply the stated non-inferiority criterion. **Rationale:** This directly estimates the contrast used in the stated hypothesis. **Assumption:** The response is defined as a binary Week-48 outcome using HIV-1 RNA <50 c/mL.
- **[Proposed]** Prespecify an analysis that accounts for the randomization strata, with an unadjusted comparison as a sensitivity analysis. **Rationale:** This respects the stratified randomization while allowing assessment of robustness. **Assumption:** Screening stratum information is available for all randomized subjects.
- **[Clarification Required]** The primary CI method, stratification-adjustment method, and treatment of subjects with missing or unevaluable Week-48 status are not specified. These decisions can affect the non-inferiority conclusion.
- **[Provided]** Non-inferiority must be demonstrated in both ITT-E and PP analyses before ITT-E superiority testing.
- **[Provided]** The stated superiority testing uses the lower bound of the 95% CI for the response-rate difference to determine whether it exceeds 0.
- **[Provided]** The conditional secondary tests are generalized Wilcoxon for time to suppression, repeated-measures mixed model for Week-48 CD4, and ANCOVA for Week-4 SDM bother, in a specified fallback sequence.
- **[Clarification Required]** The time-to-suppression event definition, censoring rules, and details of the repeated-measures mixed model and ANCOVA are not specified. These are needed for reproducible analyses.
- **[Proposed]** Analyze longitudinal continuous outcomes using a prespecified model appropriate to the collected repeated measurements and report treatment differences with confidence intervals. **Rationale:** Longitudinal modeling can use repeated observations and quantify changes over time. **Assumption:** Repeated outcome measurements are collected at defined visits.
- **[Proposed]** Summarize virologic resistance findings among subjects meeting the prespecified virologic-failure definition descriptively, by treatment group. **Rationale:** This describes observed resistance without implying an unsupported confirmatory comparison. **Assumption:** Resistance testing results are available for a defined subset of virologic failures.
- **[Proposed]** Summarize HIV-associated conditions and harms by treatment group and over time using descriptive counts and appropriate rates when exposure time is available. **Rationale:** This supports the stated incidence and safety objectives. **Assumption:** Dates of events and relevant follow-up or exposure time are collected.

### 5.4 Missing Data

- **[Clarification Required]** The primary endpoint’s missing-data rule, including how treatment discontinuation, prohibited or rescue therapy, and missing Week-48 measurements affect response classification, is not supplied. These decisions can change response rates and the non-inferiority result.
- **[Proposed]** Prespecify a primary missing-data approach and sensitivity analyses that examine plausible alternative assumptions, including a tipping-point analysis for the binary primary outcome. **Rationale:** Non-inferiority conclusions can be sensitive to missing outcomes, so robustness should be assessed. **Assumption:** Missing outcomes may be informative and cannot be assumed to have a single known value.
- **[Proposed]** For repeated-measures models, describe the likelihood-based analysis assumptions and assess robustness to departures from those assumptions where feasible. **Rationale:** This makes the implications of incomplete longitudinal data transparent. **Assumption:** The selected model is appropriate for the outcome distribution and visit structure.

### 5.5 Additional Analyses

- **[Provided]** The study explores response by gender, race, and/or HIV-1 subtype.
- **[Proposed]** Present subgroup-specific response estimates and treatment-by-subgroup interaction estimates with confidence intervals; interpret these as exploratory rather than definitive evidence of differential treatment effect. **Rationale:** Interaction estimates address whether response may vary across subgroups. **Assumption:** Subgroup data are collected and subgroup sizes permit estimation.
- **[Clarification Required]** The subgroup definitions, categories, and any minimum data requirements are not specified. These choices are needed for consistent subgroup analyses.
- **[Proposed]** Conduct sensitivity analyses for the primary outcome using alternative reasonable analysis-population and missing-data assumptions, in addition to the required ITT-E and PP analyses. **Rationale:** Concordance across analyses supports evaluation of non-inferiority robustness. **Assumption:** Alternative rules can be specified before unblinding without changing the stated primary decision rule.
