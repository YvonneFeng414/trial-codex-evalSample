# Clinical Study Protocol and Statistical Analysis Framework

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Background and Rationale

- **[Provided]** Pediatric type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM) has limited glucose-lowering options. This Phase 3 study evaluates whether once-weekly dulaglutide added to diet and exercise, with or without metformin and/or basal insulin, improves glycemic control versus placebo.
- **[Provided]** HbA1c was selected as an accepted endpoint for pediatric T2DM glycemic trials.

### 1.2 Objectives

- **[Provided]** **Primary objective (P1):** Test superiority of pooled dulaglutide 0.75 mg and 1.5 mg subcutaneously (SC) once weekly versus placebo for change in central-laboratory HbA1c from baseline to Week 26 in children and adolescents with inadequately controlled T2DM.
- **[Provided]** **Primary outcome:** Change in central-laboratory HbA1c from baseline to Week 26, pooled dulaglutide versus placebo.
- **[Provided]** **Secondary efficacy objective (S1):** Compare individual dulaglutide 0.75-mg and 1.5-mg doses and, where specified, pooled dulaglutide with placebo for HbA1c change, fasting blood glucose (FBG) change, HbA1c <7.0%, and body mass index (BMI) change at Week 26; assess HbA1c, FBG, and BMI changes and HbA1c <7.0% at Week 52.
- **[Provided]** **Secondary safety and PK/PD objective (S2):** Assess safety at Week 26 versus placebo and at Week 52 within dulaglutide groups, covering self-reported hypoglycemia/rescue; adjudicated pancreatitis/pancreatic enzymes; thyroid treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs)/calcitonin; and anti-drug antibodies (ADA), allergic/hypersensitivity, and injection-site reactions. Characterize pharmacokinetics (PK) and exposure relationships with safety and efficacy.
- **[Provided]** **Exploratory objective (S3):** At Weeks 26 and 52 unless stated otherwise, assess HbA1c ≤6.5%; HbA1c <7.0% and HbA1c ≤6.5% without severe, documented symptomatic (<70 mg/dL), or probable hypoglycemia; weight, BMI standard deviation score (SDS), height/height SDS, waist, morning prolactin/IGF-1/estradiol/testosterone in males/luteinizing hormone (LH)/cortisol, lipids, Tanner stage, EQ-5D-Y visual analogue scale (VAS) and domain levels, and basal-insulin-dose change. Assess HbA1c change at Week 13 and insulin resistance, beta-cell function, and adiponectin at Weeks 13 and 26.

## 2. Study Methods

### 2.1 Trial Design

- **[Provided]** The study is a Phase 3, multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-arm superiority trial, followed by a 26-week open-label extension and 30-day safety follow-up.
- **[Provided]** Participants are allocated 1:1:1 at baseline (Visit 2) to dulaglutide 0.75 mg/week, dulaglutide 1.5 mg/week, or placebo/week during the double-blind period.
- **[Provided]** Dulaglutide 0.75 mg/week and 1.5 mg/week continue during the open-label period. Participants assigned placebo switch to dulaglutide 0.75 mg/week at Week 26.
- **[Provided]** Week-26 reporting labels are All Dula (pooled 1.5/0.75 mg), Dula 1.5, Dula 0.75, and Placebo; placebo is the reference.
- **[Provided]** Post-Week-26 sequence labels are All Dula (0.75/0.75 and 1.5/1.5 pooled), Dula 1.5/1.5, Dula 0.75/0.75, and Placebo/Dula 0.75. No between-group inference is made after Week 26.
- **[Clarification Required]** The study-treatment volumes and adjuvant content are not specified. These details matter for treatment administration and complete intervention documentation.
- **[Clarification Required]** The operational definition of rescue treatment and its relationship to study treatment discontinuation are not specified. This matters because rescue affects the efficacy estimand and is also a safety outcome.

### 2.2 Randomization

- **[Provided]** A computer-generated random sequence is implemented through an interactive web response system (IWRS) after eligibility confirmation at Visit 2.
- **[Provided]** Randomization is stratified by screening HbA1c (<8.0% or ≥8.0%), metformin use (yes/no), and insulin use (yes/no).
- **[Clarification Required]** Blocking or minimization procedures are not specified. This matters for documenting the allocation procedure and supporting reproduction of the randomization.
- **[Clarification Required]** Allocation concealment procedures beyond IWRS implementation are not specified. This matters for evaluating the protection of the allocation process.

### 2.3 Sample Size

- **[Provided]** The initial plan was approximately 150 enrolled participants (50 per arm) to obtain 120 Week-26 completers, with approximately 750 screened based on an estimated 80% screen-failure rate.
- **[Provided]** The sample-size calculation was based on the continuous percentage-point change in HbA1c from baseline to Week 26, comparing pooled dulaglutide with placebo. Absolute control- and treatment-arm values were not specified; the calculation reports a between-arm difference.
- **[Provided]** The initial target difference was −0.65 percentage points in mean HbA1c change for pooled dulaglutide versus placebo, with an assumed standard deviation (SD) of 1.0 percentage point, two-sided alpha 0.05, at least 90% pooled power and 80% power for each dose, and 20% dropout.
- **[Provided]** A sample-size re-estimation (SSR) scenario using SD 1.3 percentage points indicated that up to 189 enrolled participants (63 per arm) would give at least 80% pooled power and 69% power for each dose.
- **[Provided]** The Statistical Analysis Committee (SAC) could recommend a variability-only SSR after approximately 100 participants were enrolled, bounded at 150–189 participants; Lilly would decide whether to implement it. The blinded Week-26 SD was estimated using a restricted maximum likelihood (REML) mixed model for repeated measures (MMRM) containing baseline HbA1c, insulin/metformin, treatment, visit, and treatment-by-visit.
- **[Provided]** The 2019 SSR used a SAC-estimated HbA1c SD of 1.4 percentage points, an assumed difference of −0.8 percentage points, and 10% Week-26 HbA1c missingness; it confirmed an enrollment of approximately 150.
- **[Provided]** SSR does not require alpha adjustment. The powered endpoint is continuous HbA1c change, not time-to-event; a required number of events is not applicable. A non-inferiority/equivalence margin is not applicable because the trial is a superiority trial. A design effect for clustering is not applicable because the trial is individually randomized and no clustering is described.
- **[Provided]** Sample-size assumptions were based on limited pediatric GLP-1 receptor agonist experience, broad baseline HbA1c values, and inclusion of metformin and/or basal-insulin users. The stated rationale was to preserve power while limiting placebo exposure and accommodating uncertainty in pediatric endpoint variability.
- **[Clarification Required]** The software used for the sample-size calculation is not specified. This matters for reproducibility of the calculation.

### 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

- **[Provided]** Two primary estimands compare pooled dulaglutide with placebo for Week-26 HbA1c: an efficacy estimand using the intention-to-treat (ITT) population with post-rescue data censored, and a treatment-regimen estimand using the ITT population regardless of rescue or adherence.
- **[Provided]** Each primary estimand has a full two-sided alpha of 0.05.
- **[Provided]** After H1 pooled-HbA1c success, a graphical procedure transfers alpha among H2–H3 individual-dose HbA1c hypotheses, H4–H6 HbA1c <7% hypotheses, H7–H9 FBG hypotheses, and H10–H12 BMI hypotheses, with pooled, 1.5-mg, and 0.75-mg comparisons versus placebo.
- **[Provided]** The scheme is run separately for treatment-regimen washout-MI, efficacy, and EMA-restricted efficacy populations and may be revised using blinded Week-26 correlations before lock.
- **[Clarification Required]** The rule for declaring overall primary success when there are two primary estimands is not specified. This matters for interpretation of the trial’s primary conclusion.
- **[Clarification Required]** The graphical procedure’s starting weights, transfer rules, and exact mapping of hypotheses to tests are not specified. This matters for implementing and reproducing multiplicity control.
- **[Clarification Required]** The definitions of the washout-MI and EMA-restricted efficacy populations are not specified. This matters for determining who contributes to the tests and how the results are interpreted.

### 2.5 Statistical Interim Analyses

- **[Provided]** The planned interim procedure is a blinded, variability-only SSR after approximately 100 participants are enrolled. The SAC could recommend the SSR, and Lilly would decide implementation, within the enrollment bounds of 150–189. SSR does not require alpha adjustment.
- **[Clarification Required]** Any interim efficacy analyses, additional safety reviews, or independent monitoring arrangements are not specified. These decisions matter for operational oversight and, for efficacy looks, potential type I error control.

### 2.6 Timing of Analyses

- **[Provided]** The primary database lock occurs after all randomized participants complete Week 26/Visit 9. The sponsor unblinds after primary lock to analyze and report primary data.
- **[Provided]** The final database lock occurs after all participants complete the 4-week safety follow-up/Visit 801.
- **[Provided]** Baseline is Visit 2.
- **[Provided]** Week 13 is fasting, with pre-dose and 1-to-12-hour post-dose PK.
- **[Provided]** Week 26 is fasting and includes pre-dose PK, central laboratories, electrocardiogram (ECG), and EQ-5D-Y, with a ±6-day visit window.
- **[Provided]** At Week 39, PK is permitted ≤2 days post-dose.
- **[Provided]** Week 52 is fasting and includes PK, ECG, and EQ-5D-Y, with a ±7-day visit window.
- **[Provided]** Visit 801/Week 56 occurs approximately 30 days later and includes safety follow-up and PK.
- **[Provided]** EQ-5D-Y is assessed at baseline, Weeks 26 and 52, or early termination.

## 3. Statistical Principles

### 3.1 Significance and Confidence Intervals

- **[Provided]** The primary significance level is two-sided 0.05. The hypothesis framework assigns a full two-sided 0.05 to each primary estimand and specifies a graphical alpha-transfer procedure for the stated secondary hypotheses.
- **[Proposed]** Report treatment contrasts with two-sided 95% confidence intervals and exact p-values where applicable. **Rationale:** These intervals align with the stated two-sided 0.05 level and communicate effect size and precision. **Assumption:** Confidence intervals are intended to accompany the primary and inferential secondary estimates.
- **[Clarification Required]** The intended overall type I error interpretation across the two primary estimands is not specified. This matters when drawing a single confirmatory conclusion from the study.

### 3.2 Protocol Deviations

- **[Proposed]** Document protocol deviations before treatment-code unblinding, classify their impact using prespecified rules, and retain randomized participants in ITT analyses as applicable. **Rationale:** Prespecified handling limits post-randomization selection bias. **Assumption:** A blinded deviation-review process can be implemented.
- **[Clarification Required]** The definitions of major and minor deviations, the review process, and the consequences of deviations for analysis populations are not specified. These decisions matter for consistent population assignment and interpretation.

### 3.3 Analysis Populations

- **[Provided]** The hypothesis framework identifies ITT analyses for both primary estimands. For the efficacy estimand, post-rescue data are censored; for the treatment-regimen estimand, analyses include participants regardless of rescue or adherence.
- **[Proposed]** Define the ITT population as all randomized participants analyzed according to assigned treatment. **Rationale:** This preserves the randomized comparison. **Assumption:** The study’s intended ITT definition includes all randomized participants.
- **[Proposed]** Define the safety population as randomized participants who receive at least one dose of study treatment, summarized according to treatment received. **Rationale:** This supports assessment of treatment-emergent harms. **Assumption:** At least one dose is the intended exposure threshold.
- **[Proposed]** Define a PK population as participants with at least one dose and evaluable PK data, with analyses using actual treatment received and recorded sampling times. **Rationale:** PK interpretation depends on exposure and sample timing. **Assumption:** Evaluability criteria can be prespecified before database lock.
- **[Clarification Required]** No per-protocol population definition is supplied, and the operational definitions of the safety and PK populations are not supplied. These decisions matter for reproducible analysis-set membership.
- **[Clarification Required]** The operational criteria for the EMA-restricted efficacy population are not specified. This matters because it is included in the hypothesis-testing framework.

### 4.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

- **[Provided]** **Inclusion criteria:** Male or female, age 10 to <18 years, with IDF-ISPAD T2DM; lifestyle measures, metformin, and basal insulin stable for ≥8 weeks before screening; metformin ≥1000 mg/day or the highest tolerated/local-approved dose; screening HbA1c >6.5% to ≤11.0%, or ≤9.0% if newly diagnosed/lifestyle only; BMI >85th percentile; weight ≥50 kg; and participant assent/parent consent.
- **[Provided]** **Exclusion criteria:** Type 1 diabetes or antibodies; ketoacidosis/hyperosmolar syndrome; gastrointestinal, pancreatic, thyroid, renal, or cardiovascular risks; Hb variants; pregnancy/breastfeeding or ineffective contraception; unstable psychiatric treatment; prohibited medicines/other trials; other antihyperglycemics requiring ≥3-month washout; and bolus insulin requiring ≥6-week washout except ≤14-day acute-condition rescue.

## 5. Analysis

### 5.1 Primary Outcomes

- **[Provided]** The primary outcome is change in central-laboratory HbA1c from baseline to Week 26, comparing pooled dulaglutide 0.75 mg and 1.5 mg with placebo.
- **[Provided]** The primary outcome is evaluated under two estimands: efficacy, using ITT with post-rescue data censored; and treatment regimen, using ITT regardless of rescue or adherence.
- **[Proposed]** Report the Week-26 mean change and the pooled dulaglutide-minus-placebo difference, with negative differences indicating greater HbA1c reduction with dulaglutide. **Rationale:** This directly expresses the stated continuous endpoint and treatment contrast. **Assumption:** The contrast is defined as dulaglutide minus placebo.

### 5.2 Secondary Outcomes

- **[Provided]** At Week 26, secondary efficacy outcomes are individual-dose HbA1c change, FBG change, HbA1c <7.0%, and BMI change versus placebo, including pooled dulaglutide where specified.
- **[Provided]** At Week 52, secondary efficacy outcomes are HbA1c, FBG, and BMI changes and HbA1c <7.0%.
- **[Provided]** At Week 26 versus placebo and Week 52 within dulaglutide groups, safety outcomes include self-reported hypoglycemia/rescue; adjudicated pancreatitis/pancreatic enzymes; thyroid TEAEs/calcitonin; ADA; allergic/hypersensitivity reactions; injection-site reactions; and PK and exposure relationships with safety and efficacy.
- **[Provided]** Exploratory outcomes at Weeks 26 and 52 unless otherwise stated are HbA1c ≤6.5%; HbA1c <7.0% and HbA1c ≤6.5% without severe, documented symptomatic (<70 mg/dL), or probable hypoglycemia; weight, BMI SDS, height/height SDS, waist, morning prolactin/IGF-1/estradiol/testosterone in males/LH/cortisol, lipids, Tanner stage, EQ-5D-Y VAS and domain levels, and basal-insulin-dose change.
- **[Provided]** Additional exploratory outcomes are HbA1c change at Week 13 and insulin resistance, beta-cell function, and adiponectin at Weeks 13 and 26.
- **[Provided]** No between-group inference is made after Week 26.
- **[Clarification Required]** The exact definitions and ascertainment rules for the hypoglycemia categories, rescue, insulin resistance, beta-cell function, and basal-insulin-dose change are not specified. These matter for consistent endpoint derivation.
- **[Clarification Required]** The methods and reference standards for BMI SDS and height SDS, and the derivation of EQ-5D-Y domain levels, are not specified. These matter for interpreting and reproducing the outcomes.

### 5.3 Analysis Methods

- **[Proposed]** Analyze the primary continuous HbA1c outcome using an MMRM with treatment group represented by the two dulaglutide doses and placebo, visit, treatment-by-visit interaction, baseline HbA1c, and insulin and metformin use. Estimate the Week-26 contrast of pooled dulaglutide versus placebo. **Rationale:** This uses repeated HbA1c measurements and adjusts for key design factors. **Assumption:** Repeated HbA1c measurements through Week 26 are available and the pooled contrast is the intended primary comparison.
- **[Proposed]** For the efficacy estimand, exclude post-rescue HbA1c measurements from the efficacy analysis; for the treatment-regimen estimand, include available measurements regardless of rescue or adherence. **Rationale:** This operationalizes the two stated estimands. **Assumption:** Post-rescue observations are the data to be censored for the efficacy estimand.
- **[Clarification Required]** The primary model’s covariance structure, small-sample degrees-of-freedom method, and any fallback if the model fails to converge are not specified. These matter for reproducible inference.
- **[Proposed]** Analyze continuous Week-26 secondary outcomes using models appropriate to their repeated measurements and report dose-specific and pooled comparisons where specified. Analyze binary Week-26 outcomes such as HbA1c <7.0% using a regression model and report estimated proportions and treatment contrasts. **Rationale:** These methods match continuous and binary outcome scales. **Assumption:** Model-based contrasts are suitable for the randomized comparisons.
- **[Proposed]** Summarize Week-52 outcomes by treatment sequence without between-group inferential tests. **Rationale:** This follows the specified restriction on post-Week-26 inference. **Assumption:** Descriptive reporting is the intended use of Week-52 group summaries.
- **[Proposed]** Summarize exploratory outcomes descriptively and treat any additional exploratory comparisons as nonconfirmatory unless incorporated into the specified testing framework before lock. **Rationale:** This distinguishes exploratory assessment from the stated multiplicity-controlled hypotheses. **Assumption:** No other confirmatory testing family is intended.
- **[Clarification Required]** The exact statistical models, contrasts, and missing-data rules for secondary and exploratory outcomes are not specified. These matter for consistent estimation and interpretation.

### 5.4 Missing Data

- **[Provided]** The initial sample-size plan assumed 20% dropout. The 2019 SSR used an assumption of 10% Week-26 HbA1c missingness.
- **[Proposed]** Use the prespecified repeated-measures model under a missing-at-random assumption for the primary analysis, and report missingness by treatment group and visit. **Rationale:** This uses observed repeated outcome data and makes the missing-data assumption explicit. **Assumption:** Missingness is adequately explained by observed information included in the model.
- **[Proposed]** Conduct sensitivity analyses under plausible departures from missing at random, such as multiple-imputation scenarios with prespecified shifts or tipping-point analyses. **Rationale:** These assess how robust the primary conclusion is to unobserved outcomes. **Assumption:** Sensitivity scenarios can be selected before unblinding.
- **[Clarification Required]** The precise handling of missing Week-26 outcomes, post-rescue values, and missing data due to treatment discontinuation or other intercurrent events is not specified.
