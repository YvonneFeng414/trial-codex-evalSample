# Clinical Study Protocol and Statistical Analysis Framework

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Background and Rationale

- **[Provided]** FOXTROT is a phase IIa study of BAY 1213790, a fully human IgG1 anti-factor XIa antibody that binds FXIa near its catalytic center, in patients undergoing elective primary unilateral total knee arthroplasty (TKA). TKA carries substantial VTE risk, while established thromboprophylaxis entails bleeding risk. The study assesses selective FXIa inhibition for VTE prevention with a potentially favorable bleeding profile. Dose selection (0.3–1.8 mg/kg) was informed by preclinical work, Phase 1 population PK/PD, and systems pharmacology.

### 1.2 Objectives

- **[Provided]** **Primary objective (P1):** Assess safety and efficacy of different BAY 1213790 doses versus enoxaparin in elective primary unilateral TKA. The objective-level outcome is safety and efficacy of different BAY 1213790 doses versus enoxaparin.
- **[Provided]** **Secondary objective (S1):** Compare safety and efficacy of BAY 1213790 with apixaban. The objective-level outcome is safety and efficacy of BAY 1213790 compared with apixaban.
- **[Provided]** **Exploratory objective (S2):** Assess BAY 1213790 dose-response for efficacy and safety. The objective-level outcome is BAY 1213790 dose-response for efficacy and safety.
- **[Provided]** **Exploratory objective (S3):** Characterize BAY 1213790 PK/PD and their relationship. The objective-level outcome is BAY 1213790 PK/PD and their relationship.
- **[Provided]** **Other objective (S4):** Further characterize BAY 1213790 by evaluating additional PD markers, and investigate thrombosis mechanisms using additional PD markers. The objective-level outcome is additional PD markers for BAY 1213790 characterization and investigation of thrombosis mechanisms.

## 2. Study Methods

### 2.1 Trial Design

- **[Provided]** This is a randomized, active-comparator-controlled, multicenter phase IIa study. Treatment assignment is open-label; BAY 1213790 dose is observer-blinded; CIAC adjudication is blinded.
- **[Provided]** Part A administers BAY 1213790 after surgery. Sequential Part B administers BAY 1213790 before surgery, with no parallel recruitment. Part B begins only after Part A completion and review of at least 75% of Part A primary assessments.
- **[Provided]** In Part A, BAY 1213790 doses are 0.3, 0.6, or 1.2 mg/kg IV once after adequate hemostasis on the day after TKA, preferably in the morning and no more than 24 hours after wound closure. A 1.8-mg/kg arm may open.
- **[Provided]** In Part B, BAY 1213790 doses are 0.3 or 1.2 mg/kg IV once in the second half of pre-surgery Day 1. The IDMC/SC may select 0.3, 0.6, 1.2, or 1.8 mg/kg, or add an arm.
- **[Provided]** In both parts, enoxaparin 40 mg SC daily begins the pre-TKA evening or 6–8 hours after TKA and continues for at least 10 days to venography. Apixaban 2.5 mg orally twice daily begins after TKA, no more than 24 hours from wound closure, and continues for at least 10 days to venography.
- **[Provided]** The planned pooled allocation ratio is 5:1:1 for BAY 1213790:enoxaparin:apixaban, adaptable after arm closure or opening. BAY recipients are allocated 1:1:1 among Part A doses and 1:1 in Part B, subject to adjustment after arm closure or opening.
- **[Clarification Required]** The criteria, authority, and timing for opening or closing arms and adjusting allocation are not fully specified. These decisions affect enrollment, allocation, and interpretation of comparisons.

### 2.2 Randomization

- **[Provided]** IxRS randomizes participants to BAY 1213790, enoxaparin, or apixaban, followed by IxRS randomization of BAY recipients to a dose arm.
- **[Provided]** Stratification factors, minimization, and blocking are not specified.
- **[Clarification Required]** Whether randomization uses stratification, blocking, or other restrictions must be confirmed before implementation because these choices affect allocation procedures and potentially the analysis.

### 2.3 Sample Size

- **[Provided]** The initial target is 700 participants: 500 BAY 1213790 recipients across five intended arms and 100 each receiving enoxaparin or apixaban. The maximum is 900 participants.
- **[Provided]** The arm target is 100 participants per BAY and comparator arm. Comparator enrollment is at least 60 in Part A and at least 40 in Part B.
- **[Clarification Required]** Confirm whether the comparator minimums are pooled across both comparator groups or apply separately to each comparator. This affects the arm-level enrollment targets and allocation plan.
- **[Provided]** The sample-size calculation is based on the primary efficacy composite VTE endpoint at Day 12+3, treated as a binary event proportion. Assumed event proportions are 25% for enoxaparin and 10% and 4% for BAY 1213790 in the respective scenarios.
- **[Clarification Required]** The input does not map the 10% and 4% BAY event-rate scenarios to particular doses or treatment-time arms. This mapping matters for interpreting the scenario-specific power calculations.
- **[Provided]** The effect measure is the risk difference, with a 5% non-inferiority margin and one-sided 5% significance level. Power scenarios are: BAY 10% versus enoxaparin 25%, 81% superiority and 97% non-inferiority; BAY 4% versus enoxaparin 25%, 99% superiority and 99% non-inferiority.
- **[Provided]** Apixaban calculations are descriptive context: superiority/non-inferiority power is 0%/29% in one scenario and 43%/88% in another.
- **[Provided]** The assumed tests are one-sided non-inferiority and superiority tests using the lower bound of a two-sided 90% normal-approximation confidence interval for the risk difference. The calculation is for binary event proportions; variability assumptions, event-driven targets, and design effects are not applicable.
- **[Provided]** Approximately 20% unavailable or non-evaluable venography due to quality or dropout is assumed, yielding 80 evaluable participants per arm. The study is individually randomized and non-clustered.
- **[Provided]** The basis for the sample-size assumptions is the event-rate scenarios and the 20% non-evaluable venography assumption.
- **[Provided]** After approximately 75% of Part A Visit 7 assessments, pooled unavailable venography above 30% may trigger a sample-size increase to regain 80% superiority power. Arm or dose enrollment may also change on IDMC/SC advice, within the 900-participant maximum.
- **[Clarification Required]** The target comparison or event-rate scenario for the 80% superiority-power re-estimation is not specified. This matters because different comparison assumptions can yield different required sample sizes.
- **[Provided]** The calculation rationale is to characterize dose safety and efficacy in phase II comparisons; examples demonstrate non-inferiority and superiority precision versus enoxaparin.
- **[Provided]** Software for the sample-size calculation is not specified.

### 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

- **[Provided]** For each BAY dose/time arm versus enoxaparin, test non-inferiority first and superiority second, each one-sided at global α=5%. Use a fixed sequence and stop after the first null hypothesis that is not rejected.
- **[Provided]** The stated non-inferiority rule requires the lower 90% confidence interval bound for BAY minus enoxaparin to be below 5%; the stated superiority rule requires it to be below 0.
- **[Clarification Required]** Confirm the risk-difference sign convention and the stated “below” decision rules, particularly for non-inferiority. With BAY minus enoxaparin as the contrast, the direction of the bound and margin determines which results meet the stated decision rule.
- **[Provided]** No hypothesis testing is planned versus apixaban; report descriptive proportions and 90% confidence intervals.
- **[Clarification Required]** Multiplicity control across the different BAY dose/time-arm comparisons is not specified. This matters for interpretation of the overall type I error across comparisons.

### 2.5 Statistical Interim Analyses

IDMC/SC advice may also lead to arm or dose enrollment changes, within the 900-participant maximum.
- **[Provided]** Part B begins only after Part A completion and review of at least 75% of Part A primary assessments.
- **[Clarification Required]** Formal interim efficacy or futility testing, stopping boundaries, and any associated alpha-spending rules are not specified. These decisions matter if interim hypothesis tests are planned.
