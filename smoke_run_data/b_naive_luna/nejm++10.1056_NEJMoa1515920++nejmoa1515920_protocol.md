# Clinical Study Protocol and Statistical Analysis Framework

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Background and Rationale

- **[Provided]** Empagliflozin (BI 10773) is evaluated in this Phase III programme to establish long-term cardiovascular safety in adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM), insufficient glycaemic control, and increased cardiovascular (CV) risk.
- **[Provided]** The study is integral to ongoing CV safety assessment and regulatory submission.

### 1.2 Objectives

- **[Provided]** **Primary objective (P1):** Demonstrate non-inferiority of pooled empagliflozin 10 mg and 25 mg once daily versus placebo for time to first 3-point major adverse cardiovascular event (3P-MACE), using a hazard-ratio (HR) non-inferiority margin of 1.3.
- **[Provided]** **Primary outcome:** Time to first 3P-MACE, pooled empagliflozin 10 mg and 25 mg once daily versus placebo; HR non-inferiority margin 1.3.
- **[Provided]** **Secondary objective (S1):** Assess the key secondary adjudicated 4-point composite (3P-MACE plus hospitalisation for unstable angina) within the confirmatory hierarchy.
- **[Provided]** **Secondary outcome (S1):** Adjudicated 4-point composite of 3P-MACE plus hospitalisation for unstable angina.
- **[Provided]** **Secondary objective (S2):** Assess occurrence/time to silent MI, heart-failure hospitalisation, new albuminuria, new macroalbuminuria, new/worsening nephropathy, and the composite microvascular outcome.
- **[Provided]** **Secondary outcome (S2):** Occurrence/time to silent MI, heart-failure hospitalisation, new albuminuria, new macroalbuminuria, new/worsening nephropathy, and the composite microvascular outcome.
- **[Provided]** **Secondary objective (S3):** Assess further CV events: CV death, fatal/nonfatal MI and stroke, unstable-angina hospitalisation, all-cause mortality, transient ischaemic attack (TIA), and coronary revascularisation.
- **[Provided]** **Secondary outcome (S3):** CV death, fatal/nonfatal MI and stroke, unstable-angina hospitalisation, all-cause mortality, TIA, and coronary revascularisation.
- **[Provided]** **Secondary objective (S4):** Assess diabetes-related efficacy: change from baseline in HbA1c, fasting plasma glucose (FPG), weight, waist circumference, and blood pressure (BP); the HbA1c/SBP/weight composite; and rescue-therapy use.
- **[Provided]** **Secondary outcome (S4):** Change from baseline in HbA1c, FPG, weight, waist circumference, and BP; HbA1c/SBP/weight composite; and rescue-therapy use.
- **[Provided]** **Exploratory objective (S5):** Describe pharmacokinetic (PK) trough concentrations, safety (adverse events [AEs], hypoglycaemia, adverse events of special interest [AESIs], laboratory data, electrocardiogram [ECG], vital signs), and optional pharmacogenomics.
- **[Provided]** **Exploratory outcome (S5):** PK trough concentrations; AEs, hypoglycaemia, AESIs, laboratory data, ECG, and vital signs; optional pharmacogenomics.
- **[Provided]** **Exploratory objective (S6):** Conduct TSAP/Publication-SAP further analyses, including recurrent CV events, renal/microvascular endpoints, rescue therapy, risk factors, and correlations of systolic BP (SBP)/HbA1c with urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (UACR).
- **[Provided]** **Exploratory outcome (S6):** Recurrent CV events, renal/microvascular endpoints, rescue therapy, risk factors, and correlations of SBP/HbA1c with UACR.

## 2. Study Methods

### 2.1 Trial Design

- **[Provided]** This is a Phase III, international, multicentre, randomised, parallel-group, double-blind, placebo-controlled, add-on-to-standard-care, event-driven CV-safety study.
- **[Provided]** Screening is followed by a 2-week open-label placebo run-in, double-blind treatment until the required primary events, and 30-day follow-up.
- **[Provided]** Planned treatment is approximately 6 to 8 years, up to approximately 420 weeks, conditional on accrual of the required events.
- **[Provided]** Allocation is 1:1:1 to empagliflozin 10 mg, empagliflozin 25 mg, or matched placebo.
- **[Provided]** Study drug is administered orally once daily in addition to background therapy.
- **[Provided]** All patients undergo a 2-week open-label placebo run-in before randomisation.
- **[Provided]** During the first 12 post-randomisation weeks, pre-consent background antidiabetic therapy should remain unchanged where possible; rescue is permitted by criteria.
- **[Provided]** After week 12, background therapy may be adjusted for clinical care.
- **[Clarification Required]** The specific rescue-therapy criteria and permitted rescue treatments are not supplied; these are needed to implement treatment consistently and interpret rescue-therapy outcomes.
- **[Clarification Required]** The study-specific definitions of 3P-MACE, its component events, event dates, and adjudication procedures are not supplied; these are needed to classify endpoints and determine when the primary-event target is reached.

### 2.2 Randomization

- **[Provided]** A third-party phone/web IVRS/IWRS assigns treatment from a computer-generated pseudorandom, reproducible, non-predictable sequence.
- **[Provided]** Randomisation is blocked and balanced within strata; no minimisation is reported.
- **[Provided]** The allocation ratio is 1:1:1.
- **[Provided]** Stratification factors are screening HbA1c <8.5%/≥8.5%; randomisation BMI <30/≥30 kg/m²; region (North America, Latin America, Europe, Africa, or Asia); and screening eGFR ≥90, 60–89, or 30–59 mL/min.
- **[Provided]** For the TSAP primary model, North America includes Australia and New Zealand.
- **[Provided]** Baseline-characteristic tables compare planned IVRS strata with actual CRF strata.
- **[Provided]** Analyses use actual CRF information, with IXRS values only as a stated fallback for missing HbA1c/eGFR.
- **[Clarification Required]** The exact blocking sizes, any additional concealment procedures, and the operational definitions of the region strata are not supplied; these matter for reproducibility and assessment of allocation implementation.

### 2.3 Sample Size

- **[Provided]** The planned total sample size is 7000 patients: 2333 assigned to empagliflozin 10 mg, 2333 to empagliflozin 25 mg, and 2334 to placebo.
- **[Provided]** The sample-size calculation pools the two active arms, giving a 2:1 active-to-placebo allocation for that calculation.
- **[Provided]** The calculation outcome is time to first 3P-MACE, a time-to-event HR non-inferiority endpoint.
- **[Provided]** Identical hazards are assumed for pooled empagliflozin and placebo for the non-inferiority event calculation.
- **[Provided]** No fixed placebo event rate is used for the event requirement; duration scenarios use yearly event rates of 1.0%, 1.5%, and 2.0%.
- **[Provided]** The non-inferiority margin is HR 1.3.
- **[Provided]** Without interim adjustment, 690 events provide 90% power for non-inferiority; the event table gives 516 and 690 events for 80% and 90% power, respectively.
- **[Provided]** The interim-adjusted minimum is 691 adjudicated primary events.
- **[Provided]** Separately, 691 events provide at least 80% power to detect HR 0.785, corresponding to a 21.5% risk reduction.
- **[Provided]** The stated significance level is one-sided .025; .0001 is spent at interim and .0249 remains for final analysis.
- **[Provided]** The assumed calculation test is the log-rank test in EAST Software, version 5, stated to be equivalent to the Cox proportional-hazards score test.
- **[Provided]** A variability assumption is not applicable to the time-to-event event calculation.
- **[Provided]** No numeric dropout/non-adherence inflation factor is reported.
- **[Provided]** Design effect/clustering is not applicable because the trial is individually randomised.
- **[Provided]** Duration assumptions include 24-month accrual at 3500 patients per year, a 1.5% yearly event rate, and 7000 patients, yielding just under 8 years.
- **[Provided]** Duration scenarios also use 1.0% and 2.0% annual event rates and 2- or 2.5-year accrual.
- **[Provided]** A blinded overall event-rate review may change the recruitment goal. If assumptions or accrual require more recruitment or change, a protocol amendment is required.
- **[Provided]** The stopping basis is at least 691 adjudicated primary events, not 691 key-secondary events.
- **[Provided]** The event count is independent of accrual, follow-up, and annual event rate; the number randomised and duration depend on them.
- **[Clarification Required]** The method and timing for the blinded event-rate review, and the process for implementing any resulting recruitment change, are not specified; these affect recruitment oversight and documentation.
- **[Clarification Required]** No numeric allowance for dropout or non-adherence is reported; the effect of attrition on the planned number of evaluable events and trial duration therefore cannot be quantified from the supplied information.

### 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

- **[Provided]** The confirmatory comparison is pooled empagliflozin versus placebo and follows a fixed four-step hierarchy.
- **[Provided]** Step 1 tests non-inferiority for primary 3P-MACE: H0 HR≥1.3.
- **[Provided]** Step 2 tests non-inferiority for key-secondary 4P-MACE: H0 HR≥1.3.
- **[Provided]** Step 3 tests superiority for primary 3P-MACE: H0 HR≥1.
- **[Provided]** Step 4 tests superiority for key-secondary 4P-MACE: H0 HR≥1.
- **[Provided]** Each hypothesis is tested at one-sided alpha .025.
- **[Provided]** Proceed to the next hierarchy step only if the preceding 95% confidence interval (CI) upper bound is below 1.3 for non-inferiority or 1.0 for superiority.
- **[Proposed]** Define the HR for confirmatory interpretation as pooled empagliflozin relative to placebo. Rationale: this direction aligns the stated HR thresholds with the stated non-inferiority and superiority comparisons. Assumption: the supplied HR thresholds use active treatment as the numerator.
- **[Clarification Required]** The relationship between the hierarchy’s one-sided .025 testing level and the stated interim spending of .0001/final .0249 is not specified; the final critical level and hierarchy decision rules must be reconciled to control the intended type I error.

### 2.5 Statistical Interim Analyses

- **[Provided]** The sample-size section states that .0001 alpha is spent at interim and .0249 remains for final analysis.
- **[Provided]** A blinded overall event-rate review may change the recruitment goal.
- **[Clarification Required]** The timing, data scope, and purpose of any formal interim analysis are not specified; these are needed to establish which data are reviewed and when.
- **[Clarification Required]** The interim test, stopping boundaries, decision rules, and access to unblinded results are not specified; these are needed to protect trial integrity and implement the stated alpha spending.
- **[Clarification Required]** It is not specified whether the formal interim analysis assesses efficacy, safety, or both; this affects oversight procedures and interpretation of the stated alpha expenditure.

### 2.6 Timing of Analyses

- **[Provided]** Final analysis is planned after at least 691 adjudicated primary events.
- **[Provided]** Patients are followed to trial termination; the end is last patient out.
- **[Provided]** Data cutoff, database lock, and other data-readiness requirements are not specified.
- **[Provided]** Formal interim-analysis timing is not specified.
- **[Provided]** Post-randomisation visits occur at weeks 4, 8, 12, 16, 28, 40, and 52, then every 14 weeks.
- **[Provided]** Efficacy assessments (HbA1c, FPG, weight, waist circumference, and BP) are scheduled at weeks 12 and 52, annually, and at end of study.
- **[Provided]** Weight and waist circumference are additionally assessed at 30-day follow-up.
- **[Provided]** The final/end-of-study (EOS) visit includes drug collection/compliance, physical examination, laboratory assessment, and ECG.
- **[Provided]** Visit 98 occurs 30 days after final/EOS and includes BP/pulse, weight/waist, blood/urine safety laboratories, AEs/endpoints, and concomitant therapy.
- **[Clarification Required]** The analysis data cutoff, database-lock criteria, and any data-cleaning or adjudication completion requirements are not supplied; these determine when the final analysis can be performed.

## 3. Statistical Principles

### 3.1 Significance and Confidence Intervals

- **[Provided]** The stated hierarchy uses one-sided alpha .025 for each test and the preceding 95% CI upper bound for each proceed decision.
- **[Provided]** The sample-size section states .0001 alpha is spent at interim and .0249 remains for final analysis.
- **[Proposed]** Report HR estimates with two-sided 95% CIs and one-sided p-values for the confirmatory tests. Rationale: the supplied hierarchy specifies 95% upper bounds and one-sided testing. Assumption: the reported 95% CI is the interval intended for the stated upper-bound decision.
- **[Clarification Required]** The precise final-analysis confidence-interval level and critical value after interim alpha spending are not specified; these must be defined to ensure the CI rule matches the allocated alpha.
- **[Clarification Required]** The treatment of missing endpoint data, multiple non-confirmatory tests, and any additional multiplicity adjustment is not specified; this affects interpretation of inferential results.

### 3.2 Protocol Deviations

- **[Provided]** No protocol-deviation classification or analysis handling rules are supplied.

- **[Proposed]** Document deviations without excluding patients from the primary randomised comparison solely because of post-randomisation treatment changes. Rationale: this preserves the randomised comparison. Assumption: the primary estimand is based on assignment to treatment, with additional analyses addressing relevant deviations.
- **[Clarification Required]** The study-specific deviation rules and their effect on analysis populations are not provided; these are necessary to determine which patients or observations contribute to each analysis.

### 3.3 Analysis Populations

- **[Provided]** The planned randomised groups are empagliflozin 10 mg, empagliflozin 25 mg, and placebo.
- **[Provided]** The primary objective pools the two empagliflozin doses for comparison with placebo.
- **[Clarification Required]** No analysis populations are specified; the included patients and treatment assignment used for confirmatory, per-protocol, and safety analyses therefore remain undetermined.
- **[Proposed]** Use a full analysis set comprising all randomised patients, analysed according to randomised treatment, for the primary confirmatory comparison. Rationale: this preserves randomisation. Assumption: no study-specific post-randomisation exclusion rule is required.

- **[Proposed]** Use a safety population comprising randomised patients who received at least one dose, analysed by treatment received. Rationale: safety summaries should reflect exposure. Assumption: exposure and treatment received can be reliably determined.

## 4. Trial Population

### 4.1 Screening Data

- **[Provided]** The run-in exclusion criterion includes fasting glucose >240 mg/dL confirmed on a second day.
- **[Provided]** Screening HbA1c eligibility ranges are 7.0%–10.0%, or 7.0%–9.0% for drug-naïve patients.
- **[Clarification Required]** The full screening-data collection schedule, source data, and rules for repeat or invalid assessments are not specified; these affect eligibility determination and analysis of screening failures.

### 4.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

- **[Provided]** Inclusion requires T2DM before consent and diet/exercise.
- **[Provided]** Eligible antidiabetic-treatment history is either drug-naïve with no antidiabetic treatment for 12 weeks or stable background therapy, including insulin dose within 10% for 12 weeks.
- **[Provided]** Screening HbA1c must be 7.0%–10.0%, or 7.0%–9.0% if drug-naïve.
- **[Provided]** Minimum age is ≥18 years, except Japan ≥20 years and India 18–65 years.
- **[Provided]** BMI must be ≤45 kg/m².
- **[Provided]** Informed consent is required.
- **[Provided]** Qualifying high CV risk includes prior MI; specified multivessel/single-vessel coronary artery disease (CAD) or unstable angina; prior stroke; or peripheral arterial disease (PAD).
- **[Provided]** Women of child-bearing potential require acceptable contraception and periodic pregnancy testing.
- **[Provided]** Exclusion criteria include run-in fasting glucose >240 mg/dL confirmed on a second day; liver enzymes/alkaline phosphatase >3× upper limit of normal (ULN); planned cardiac procedure within 3 months; eGFR <30; malabsorption surgery; dyscrasia/haemolysis; cancer within 5 years except basal-cell carcinoma; contraindicated background therapy; unstable weight intervention; systemic steroids/uncontrolled endocrinopathy; pregnancy/nursing or unsuitable contraception; alcohol/drug abuse or poor compliance; recent/ongoing investigational trial; unsafe condition; and acute coronary syndrome/stroke/TIA within 2 months.
- **[Provided]** Canada and South Africa have stated local exclusions; their details are not specified.
- **[Clarification Required]** Definitions for qualifying CAD, unstable angina, PAD, stable background therapy, malabsorption surgery, contraindicated therapy, unstable weight intervention, recent investigational trial, and unsafe condition are not supplied; these are needed for consistent eligibility decisions.
- **[Clarification Required]** The specific local exclusions for Canada and South Africa are not supplied; they are needed to apply country-specific eligibility consistently.
- **[Clarification Required]** Contraception methods, pregnancy-testing schedule, and the operational definition of child-bearing potential are not specified; these are needed to implement the stated reproductive eligibility requirements.
