# Clinical Study Protocol and Statistical Analysis Framework

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Background and Rationale

- **[Provided]** C3G and IC-MPGN are complement-driven glomerular diseases that may occur in native kidneys or recur after transplant. Pegcetacoplan inhibits C3 and C3b to limit inappropriate complement activation and glomerular C3-deposit injury. Reduction in proteinuria is the expected early renal signal.

### 1.2 Objectives

- **[Provided]** **Primary objective (P1):** Assess twice-weekly subcutaneous (SC) pegcetacoplan versus placebo in primary C3G/IC-MPGN on the basis of proteinuria reduction. The primary outcome is proteinuria reduction with twice-weekly SC pegcetacoplan versus placebo.
- **[Provided]** **Secondary objective (S1):** Assess the effect of pegcetacoplan on eGFR; outcome: eGFR.
- **[Provided]** **Secondary objective (S2):** Assess the effect of pegcetacoplan on additional C3G/IC-MPGN disease-related parameters; outcome: additional C3G/IC-MPGN disease-related parameters.
- **[Provided]** **Secondary objective (S3):** Evaluate pegcetacoplan safety over 52 weeks of treatment; outcome: safety over 52 weeks of treatment.
- **[Provided]** **Exploratory objective (S4):** Characterize additional clinical, laboratory, and histologic findings in response to pegcetacoplan; outcome: additional clinical, laboratory, and histologic findings in response to pegcetacoplan.

## 2. Study Methods

### 2.1 Trial Design

- **[Provided]** This is a Phase 3, randomized, multicenter, placebo-controlled, double-blind study comprising a 10-week screening period, a 26-week randomized controlled period (RCP), a 26-week open-label period (OLP), and an 8-week follow-up for participants not entering the long-term extension.
- **[Provided]** Participants are allocated 1:1 to pegcetacoplan or placebo during the RCP. The pegcetacoplan group receives pegcetacoplan during the RCP and OLP; the placebo group receives matching placebo during the RCP and pegcetacoplan during the OLP.
- **[Provided]** Study drug is administered by SC infusion twice weekly. Adults and adolescents weighing ≥50 kg receive 1080 mg/20 mL twice weekly. Adolescents weighing 35 to <50 kg receive 648 mg first, then 810 mg/15 mL twice weekly. Adolescents weighing 30 to <35 kg receive 540 mg for the first 2 doses, then 648 mg/12 mL twice weekly.
- **[Provided]** The RCP is blinded. Participants, sponsor, investigators, and site staff remain blinded to RCP allocation through completion of all week-26 assessments and RCP database lock. Central evaluations, including central pathology, are blinded. Only non-site drug-supply/logistics personnel are unblinded.
- **[Clarification Required]** Clarify the exact administration schedule represented by “648 mg first” for adolescents weighing 35 to <50 kg. This matters for consistent dosing, implementation, and safety reporting.
- **[Clarification Required]** Clarify which participants may enter the long-term extension and the follow-up procedures for those who do. The supplied design specifies an 8-week follow-up only for participants not entering the extension, but does not specify extension-entry criteria or procedures.

### 2.2 Randomization

- **[Provided]** Randomization is performed centrally using a computer-generated schedule with stratified permuted blocks. The allocation ratio is 1:1.
- **[Provided]** Stratification factors are posttransplant recurrence versus nontransplant, followed by availability versus unavailability of a baseline renal biopsy from screening or historically within 28 weeks.
- **[Provided]** Minimization is not specified.
- **[Clarification Required]** Specify block sizes or whether block sizes vary, and clarify how the two stratification factors are implemented in the randomization system. These details matter for reproducibility and for any analysis accounting for stratification.

### 2.3 Sample Size

- **[Provided]** The planned sample size was approximately 80–100 randomized participants, 40–50 per arm, including at least 78 native-kidney participants and at least 63 participants with native-kidney C3G.
- **[Provided]** Under the Amendment 4 calculation, the outcome was week-26 uPCR reduction/log ratio to baseline, analyzed as a continuous log-transformed uPCR ratio. The assumed control-arm reduction was 20% (mean log ratio −0.22), and the assumed treatment-arm reduction was 60% (mean log ratio −0.92), for a target difference of −0.92 versus −0.22.
- **[Provided]** The Amendment 4 calculation assumed an SD of 0.88 on the log scale, one-sided alpha of 0.025, and at least 90% power with 70 total participants before attrition inflation. A 10% inflation was applied for potential missing assessments and COVID-19 impact. The assumptions were based on preliminary Study APL2-201 data.
- **[Provided]** The Amendment 4 rationale was that 70 participants provide targeted power under the specified log-uPCR assumptions and that 10% inflation supports enrollment targets. The calculation test and calculation software were not specified.
- **[Provided]** The original protocol specified a two-sample t-test and planned conditional-power re-estimation at approximately 50% completion, without Type I error adjustment and capped at 120 participants. The software for that re-estimation was not specified.
- **[Provided]** The conditional-power re-estimation plan was not retained. The design was not event-driven; a non-inferiority/equivalence margin and design effect/clustering were not applicable.
- **[Provided]** Final enrollment after enrollment closure was 124.
- **[Derived]** Final enrollment of 124 is above the approximate planned range of 80–100.
- **[Clarification Required]** Clarify whether the final enrollment of 124 refers to randomized participants or includes participants who were enrolled but not randomized. This matters for reconciling enrollment with the randomized analysis population and the planned arm sizes.
- **[Clarification Required]** Provide the final numbers randomized per arm and the achieved counts of native-kidney and native-kidney C3G participants. The supplied final enrollment total does not provide these counts, which are needed to describe the study population and assess the planned population targets.

### 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

- **[Provided]** The primary null hypothesis is no between-group difference in the week-26 log-transformed uPCR ratio to baseline; the alternative is a difference.
- **[Provided]** Comparative key/additional hypotheses separately test no difference versus a difference in composite renal response, ≥50% uPCR response, eGFR change, histologic activity-score change, C3c response, proteinuria <1 g/day, albumin normalization, C3 normalization, FACIT-Fatigue change, and KDQOL change.
- **[Provided]** Primary, key secondary, and additional secondary endpoints are tested in a fixed sequence to control trial-wise Type I error.
- **[Clarification Required]** Specify the fixed testing sequence, including the placement of each listed endpoint and the consequences of a failed test. The sequence is necessary to determine which secondary comparisons retain confirmatory Type I error control.
- **[Clarification Required]** Specify whether the primary alternative is directional or nondirectional. The hypothesis is stated as “a difference,” while the sample-size calculation specifies a one-sided alpha; the distinction affects the confirmatory test and interpretation.

- **[Provided]** Primary and secondary endpoints are assessed at week 26 and evaluated at week 52 as exploratory. The primary uPCR uses an equal-weighted average over weeks 24, 25, and 26. Twenty-four-hour urine collections occur at weeks 24 and 48.
