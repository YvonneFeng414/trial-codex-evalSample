# Statistical Analysis Plan

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Background and Rationale
GRIPHON evaluates selexipag (ACT-293987), a non-prostanoid IP-receptor agonist, in pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH), with morbidity/mortality, exercise capacity, and safety evaluated against placebo.

### 1.2 Objectives

**Primary Objectives**

- Demonstrate the effect of selexipag versus placebo on time to first CEC-confirmed morbidity/mortality event in PAH.

**Secondary Objectives**

- Evaluate selexipag versus placebo for exercise capacity and other secondary/exploratory efficacy endpoints in PAH.
- Evaluate safety and tolerability of selexipag in PAH (AC-065A302 and AC-065A303).

**Exploratory Objectives**

Not reported as separately labelled objectives; the protocol nevertheless enumerates exploratory efficacy endpoints and specifies exploratory dose-response, pharmacoeconomic, PK, and safety analyses.

## 2. Study Methods

### 2.1 Trial Design

- **Design Type:** Prospective, multicenter, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled, parallel-group Phase 3, event-driven study AC-065A302/GRIPHON; AC-065A303 is an open-label uncontrolled extension for long-term safety/tolerability.
- **Allocation Ratio:** Placebo:selexipag 1:1; 1,150 planned patients (575 per group).
- **Intervention Summary:** Day-1 evening dose is 200 µg twice daily selexipag or matching placebo; weekly 200-µg twice-daily increments reach an individual maximum tolerated dose up to 1,600 µg twice daily, with reductions for tolerability. From Week 12 the dose is held to Week 26; later increases are allowed at scheduled visits. Treatment continues until 331 CEC-confirmed events (maximum 4.3 years).
  - **Selexipag:** 200 µg twice daily initially; titrated to individual tolerated dose ≤1600 µg twice daily.
  - **Placebo:** Matching ACT-293987 placebo, twice daily under the same titration schedule.

### 2.2 Randomization

- **Method:** Independent centralized IWRS/IVRS assigns a unique randomization number; the code remains confidential until unblinding.
- **Stratification Factors:** Site.
- **Minimization or Blocking:** Not reported in protocol.

### 2.3 Sample Size

**Outcome: time to first CEC-confirmed morbidity/mortality event**

- **Planned Sample Size:** 1,150 randomized patients, 575 per group.
- **Outcome Used for Calculation:** First CEC-confirmed morbidity/mortality event.
- **Outcome Type:** Time-to-event.
- **Assumed Control-Arm Value:** Constant event hazard 0.2231/year.
- **Assumed Treatment-Arm Value:** Constant event hazard 0.145/year.
- **Target Difference / Effect Size:** Hazard ratio 0.65.
- **Variability Assumption:** Variance under the alternative hypothesis; constant dropout hazard 0.051/year in both arms.
- **Alpha (Significance Level):** One-sided overall α=0.005 in the group-sequential design.
- **Power:** 90% to reject the null hypothesis.
- **Statistical Test Assumed:** One-sided unstratified log-rank test in a group-sequential setting.
- **Required Number of Events:** 331 first CEC-confirmed events.
- **Non-Inferiority / Equivalence Margin:** Not applicable: superiority comparison.
- **Dropout / Non-Adherence Inflation:** Dropout hazard 0.051/year; no separate numerical sample-size inflation reported.
- **Design Effect / Clustering:** Not applicable: individually randomized, non-clustered trial.
- **Basis for Assumptions:** Event and dropout hazards, target HR, group-sequential design, and variance under the alternative.
- **Sample Size Re-Estimation / Amendments:** Earlier Version 1 allowed a blinded adaptation of the sample size up to 1,150 patients. Version 7 specifies 1,150 planned patients and the 331-event design; this SAP uses the final Version-7 specifications.
- **Software Used for Calculation:** EAST 5.3, using its variance-under-alternative option.
- **Sample Size Rationale:** 331 events under the stated hazards achieve 90% power; recruitment of up to 1,150 1:1-randomized patients was expected to yield the target. At the interim, the planned probability of rejecting the null was approximately 19%; under HR=1, the probability of accepting the null at interim was approximately 50%.

### 2.4 Hypothesis Framework
For the primary endpoint, the one-sided null is that selexipag survival for remaining free of a first CEC-confirmed event is no greater than placebo at all times; the alternative is no worse at all times and greater at some time. The primary comparison is superiority.

### 2.5 Statistical Interim Analyses

- **Interim Analyses Planned:** The final Version-7 plan has one efficacy/safety interim analysis at approximately 202 CEC-confirmed first events (information fraction 202/331=0.61), performed by an independent Statistical Analysis Center and reviewed by an independent DMC. Earlier Version 1 stated that no interim efficacy analysis was planned; this is superseded by Version 7.
- **Stopping Guidelines:** Interim efficacy p≤0.00005/Z≥3.8906; nonbinding futility guidance p≥0.5/Z≤0. The DMC may recommend continuing despite an interim boundary; final boundaries are definitive.
- **Alpha Spending or Adjustment:** Interim cumulative one-sided alpha 0.00005; overall one-sided alpha 0.005. If continued, final alpha/critical value are calculated from observed interim information; efficacy boundaries are not altered by futility.

### 2.6 Timing of Analyses

- **Final Analysis Timing:** A302 includes Treatment and post-treatment observation data, excluding Treatment Extension; cut-off 27-Apr-2014. A303 safety data through 10-Mar-2014 are included.
- **Outcome Assessment Schedule:** Screening ≤28 days before Day 1; baseline/randomization Day 1; titration through Week 8; visits Week 12, Week 16, Week 26 then every 6 months; EOS and safety follow-up. Study Closure prompts EOS or PTOCV within four weeks; Treatment Extension runs from EOS to EOTE after unblinding. 6MWT/Borg are baseline and scheduled follow-ups; Week-26 endpoint assessments are performed before morning dose (trough).

## 3. Statistical Principles

### 3.1 Significance and Confidence Intervals

- **Alpha Level:** Primary testing uses one-sided group-sequential α=0.005.
- **Multiplicity Adjustment:** The primary group-sequential alpha-spending approach controls the primary analysis; the final SAP specifies conditional hierarchical testing for secondary endpoints, preserving the stated order before testing a subsequent secondary endpoint.
- **Confidence Intervals:** At interim/final primary analyses, unadjusted Cox HRs receive two-sided repeated 99% CIs; post-termination stage-wise ordering provides overall p value and group-sequentially adjusted two-sided 99% CI. Other specified analyses use their stated 95% or 99% CIs.

### 3.2 Protocol Deviations

- **Adherence Definition:** PPS requires ≥1 study-drug dose, no interruption totaling ≥50% of time between first/last dose, no off-treatment period ≥4 weeks, and valid pre-dose baseline 6MWT; data are retained only to the patient-specific PPS cut-off at prespecified deviations.
- **Adherence Assessment:** Drug log CRF, CRF data, and PV code-list document.
- **Protocol Deviation Definition:** “Protocol deviation” and “protocol violation” are interchangeable; the PV code-list document supplies definitions.
- **Protocol Deviation Reporting:** Significant deviations with greatest potential effect on efficacy/safety are summarized; patient disposition and PPS exclusions are also summarized.

### 3.3 Analysis Populations

- **Intention-to-Treat:** FAS comprises all randomized A302 patients, analyzed as randomized with all available efficacy data; it is the main set for efficacy endpoints.
- **Per Protocol:** PPS is the FAS satisfying age, PAH subgroup, dosing, adherence, valid baseline 6MWT, and no listed treatment/unblinding deviations through the PPS cut-off; analyzed as randomized and used as robustness analyses for primary/secondary efficacy.
- **Other Populations:** Safety set: randomized and ≥1 dose, analyzed as treated. PK set: FAS, ≥1 dose and ≥1 post-initiation PK sample. QoL set: FAS at sites with a suitable CAMPHOR language. Ophthalmology set: safety-set participants in that substudy.

## 4. Trial Population

### 4.1 Screening Data
Screening collects consent, inclusion/exclusion confirmation including hemodynamics, medical/PAH history, concomitant medications, WHO class, examination, weight/height, vital signs, ECG, 6MWT/Borg, pregnancy test where applicable, laboratory tests, NT-proBNP, and relevant subset assessments. Screening failures and reasons are summarized.

### 4.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary
Eligible patients are 18–75 years with symptomatic Group-1 idiopathic, heritable, drug/toxin-induced, connective-tissue-disease-associated, repaired-shunt congenital-heart-disease-associated, or HIV-associated PAH; documented RHC (mPAP ≥25 mmHg, PVR ≥400 dyn·s·cm−5, PCWP/LVEDP ≤15 mmHg); and screening 6MWD 50–450 m. Women of childbearing potential require contraception with failure rate <1%/year through one month after final dose. Exclusions include PH Groups 2–5; prostacyclin/analogue treatment within one month or planned during trial (except single acute vasodilator-testing administration); obstructive disease (FEV1/FVC<70% and FEV1<65% predicted) or restrictive disease (TLC<70% predicted); Child-Pugh B/C impairment; LVEF<45%; severe renal dysfunction; BMI<18.5; recent investigational drug; inability to comply/perform 6MWT; rehabilitation completed <8 weeks before screening or planned; impaired consent/adherence capacity; life expectancy <12 months; and pregnancy/lactation. ERAs/PDE5 inhibitors are permitted if stable ≥3 months before baseline through Week 26; prostacyclin/analogues and investigational drugs are prohibited.

### 4.3 Recruitment
Approximately 1,150 patients were planned at about 190 centers in approximately 44 countries; first patient first visit was planned Q4 2009 and recruitment completed by Q1 2013.

### 4.4 Withdrawal and Follow-up

- **Withdrawal Levels:** Individual treatment discontinuation triggers EOS; patients discontinuing study drug enter post-treatment observation until Study Closure, and may later be eligible for open-label extension where approved.
- **Withdrawal Data Collection:** The observation period is baseline to EOS/PTOCV; the post-treatment observation period begins after EOS for premature discontinuers. Clinic visits and telephone calls alternate every 3 months, with unscheduled visits for PAH deterioration; vital status is sought at closure for persons not consenting to observation. SAEs are reported to 30 days after drug discontinuation or first open-label selexipag dose, whichever comes first.
- **Reasons for Withdrawal:** Disposition and reasons for drug discontinuation are summarized; permanent discontinuation is mandated after a CEC-confirmed MM event.

### 4.5 Baseline Characteristics

- **Baseline Variables:** Sex, race, country/region, age, weight, height, BMI; PAH diagnosis time, RHC measures, etiology, WHO class, blood pressure/heart rate, 6MWD, Borg score, and blinded PAH expert review. Baseline is the last assessment on/before randomization.
- **Summary Methods:** Continuous variables are summarized descriptively; categorical variables by frequency/percentage, displayed by analysis set and treatment as specified.

## 5. Analysis

### 5.1 Primary Outcomes
**Outcome: time to first CEC-confirmed morbidity/mortality event**

- **Definition:** Composite of all-cause death; PAH-worsening hospitalization (non-elective stay ≥24 hours with protocol-defined signs/symptoms); PAH worsening requiring lung transplantation/balloon atrial septostomy; parenteral prostanoid or chronic oxygen for PAH worsening (continuous oxygen ≥24 hours/day, 7 days/week, intended long term); or disease progression. For baseline class II/III, progression requires ≥15% 6MWD decrease confirmed twice on different days within 2 weeks plus worse class; for baseline class III/IV, the 6MWD criterion plus need for additional PAH therapy. Disease progression is disregarded for baseline class I. Where confirmatory assessments are missing, the CEC qualifies/disqualifies the event and date using all available clinical data.
- **Measurement Units:** Days from randomization: CEC onset date minus randomization date plus 1.
- **Timing of Measurement:** First qualifying event through 7 days after final A302 treatment dose; events are adjudicated blinded by the independent CEC.
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Patients without an event are right-censored using the minimum relevant last-dose+7/EOS/last-contact/cut-off dates, according to treatment and PTOP consent. CEC events with onset through 16-Aug-2011 are censored at onset only in the main primary analysis and treated as events in supportive/subgroup analyses.

### 5.2 Secondary Outcomes
**Outcome: absolute change from baseline to Week 26 in 6MWD at trough**

- **Definition:** Week-26 minus baseline 6-minute walk distance; at trough, with no morning dose before assessment.
- **Measurement Units:** Metres.
- **Timing of Measurement:** Baseline and Week 26.
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Absolute change; specified single-imputation and observed-data/supportive analyses.

**Outcome: absence of worsening in modified NYHA/WHO functional class at Week 26**

- **Definition:** No worsening relative to baseline functional class; baseline class-IV patients excluded from this analysis.
- **Measurement Units:** Categorical modified NYHA/WHO class I–IV.
- **Timing of Measurement:** Baseline and Week 26.
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Binary absence-of-worsening endpoint.

**Outcome: Borg Dyspnea Index at Week 26**

- **Definition:** Patient-reported Borg dyspnea index.
- **Measurement Units:** Borg score points.
- **Timing of Measurement:** Baseline and Week 26, before the morning dose at applicable visits. The final SAP reclassified this Version-7 secondary endpoint as exploratory.
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Week-26 score; missing-data substitutions follow the protocol’s endpoint-specific rules.

**Outcome: time to CEC-confirmed PAH death or first CEC-confirmed hospitalization for PAH worsening**

- **Definition:** First of CEC-confirmed PAH death or CEC-confirmed first PAH-worsening hospitalization.
- **Measurement Units:** Days from randomization.
- **Timing of Measurement:** Up to 7 days after final treatment dose.
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** This endpoint was exploratory in Protocol Version 7 but reclassified as secondary in Final SAP Version 6.0; it is analyzed as time-to-event with endpoint-specific censoring.

**Outcome: time to death up to Study Closure**

- **Definition:** All-cause death.
- **Measurement Units:** Days from randomization to death/censoring.
- **Timing of Measurement:** Through Study Closure.
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Time-to-event with specified right-censoring.

**Outcome: CAMPHOR Breathlessness subscale change from baseline to Week 26**

- **Definition:** Change in Breathlessness subscale of the CAMPHOR Symptoms score, at selected centers.
- **Measurement Units:** CAMPHOR score points.
- **Timing of Measurement:** Baseline and Week 26.
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Absolute change from baseline.

**Outcome: CAMPHOR Symptoms-score change from baseline to Week 26**

- **Definition:** Change in the CAMPHOR Symptoms score at selected centers.
- **Measurement Units:** Score points (range 1–25).
- **Timing of Measurement:** Baseline and Week 26.
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Absolute change from baseline.

### 5.3 Analysis Methods

- **Primary Analysis Methods:** FAS, as randomized: one-sided unstratified log-rank group-sequential test; Kaplan–Meier estimates and unadjusted Cox proportional-hazards HR. Supportive analyses include unstratified/stratified Cox and log-rank tests, PPS, competing risks/multi-state methods, investigator-reported-event analysis, and analyses restricted to typical/consistent PAH. Secondary 6MWD uses nonparametric ANCOVA/Wilcoxon-Mann-Whitney; functional class uses CMH/Breslow-Day; time-to-event secondary endpoints use log-rank/Cox.
- **Covariate Adjustment:** Primary Cox model has treatment as sole covariate; prespecified supportive stratification uses region, baseline WHO class, baseline PAH medication, and/or etiology. 6MWD ANCOVA includes baseline 6MWD.
- **Assumption Checks:** Proportional-hazards assumption is examined using specified Cox-model methods.
- **Alternative Methods if Assumptions Fail:** Competing-risk/Gray-test and Andersen–Gill recurrent-event methods are specified where relevant; nonparametric methods are used for non-normally distributed outcomes.
- **Sensitivity Analyses:** Primary endpoint: alternate handling of events through 16-Aug-2011, potentially informative censoring, investigator-reported events, PPS, and restricted expert-review population. 6MWD: alternate single-imputation rules, LOCF and trough-restricted analysis; FC: no-imputation analysis.
- **Subgroup Analyses:** Baseline PAH therapy, WHO class, sex, race/ethnicity, age, PAH etiology, and region; plots/interaction-style comparisons use KM/log-rank/Cox or endpoint-appropriate methods.

### 5.4 Missing Data

- **Missing Data Assumptions:** Not reported in protocol as MAR or MNAR. Time-to-event endpoints use explicit right-censoring rules; missing data are assessed descriptively.
- **Handling Methods:** Primary and other time-to-event outcomes use endpoint-specific censoring and no imputation. For relevant Week-26 endpoints, a CEC event before assessment triggers worst-value substitutions (functional class/Borg/CAMPHOR score 5); specified algorithms also carry forward the last valid post-baseline value in applicable non-event cases. For Week-16 CAMPHOR, an intervening CEC event triggers worst-score substitution; otherwise the relevant available prior/last valid assessment rule is applied. 6MWD has sensitivity algorithms including LOCF; functional class has no-imputation sensitivity. Exploratory repeated continuous outcomes are observed-case descriptive analyses.

### 5.5 Additional Analyses
Exploratory endpoints retain the protocol’s a–s enumeration: (a) first CEC MM excluding progression to closure; (b) PAH death/first PAH hospitalization through treatment+7 days; (c) PAH death through treatment+7 days; (d) PAH death/first PAH hospitalization to closure; (e) first CEC MM to closure; (f) PAH death to closure; (g) recurrent CEC PAH hospitalizations; (h) recurrent MM-event time course; (i–j) Week-16 and EOS 6MWD; (k–l) Week-16 and EOS Borg; (m–n) Week-16/EOS functional class; (o) EOS NT-proBNP; and (p–s) Week-16/EOS CAMPHOR Breathlessness and Symptoms. Further analyses include dose-response (IMD was not randomized), pharmacoeconomic hospitalization rates/days, and PK summaries. Hospitalization counts use negative-binomial models/two-sided Wald testing; annualized hospital days use Wilcoxon-Mann-Whitney. PK concentrations below detection are set to zero for summaries and listed as received.

### 5.6 Harms

- **Adverse Event Definitions:** Treatment-emergent AEs/SAEs, ECG abnormalities, and marked laboratory abnormalities are assessed through 7 days after final dose; endpoint enumeration includes AEs leading to permanent discontinuation and dose reduction, special-interest events, vital-sign, bone-marker and retinal-tortuosity changes. Seriousness and expedited reporting are defined in protocol.
- **Safety Analysis Methods:** Safety set/as treated; incidence tables by MedDRA SOC/PT, maximum severity and investigator relationship; descriptive quantitative summaries for laboratory tests, ECG, vital signs and weight. A302 compares arms; A303/within-patient extension summaries are also specified.
- **Grading Scales and Causality:** Investigators grade intensity mild/moderate/severe and assess relationship to study drug; ongoing AEs are followed to resolution/stabilization/explanation and ongoing SAEs at EOS receive follow-up.

### 5.7 Software and References

- **Statistical Software:** EAST 5.3 for the group-sequential sample-size/power calculation. Software for final analyses is not reported in the supplied protocol/SAP beyond this named calculation software.
- **Other Referenced Documents:** GRIPHON CEC Charter (including Version 3 responsibilities), DMC Charter, PV code-list document, CAMPHOR questionnaire, AC-065A303 protocol, and final SAP D-14.302 are referenced.
