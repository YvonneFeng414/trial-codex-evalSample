# Statistical Analysis Plan

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Background and Rationale
ENVISAGE-TAVI AF is a phase 3b trial of edoxaban versus vitamin K antagonist (VKA) in people with atrial fibrillation (AF) requiring chronic oral anticoagulation after transcatheter aortic valve implantation (TAVI). The final SAP v3.0 applies where it adds to or differs from the final protocol.

### 1.2 Objectives

**Primary Objectives**

- Compare edoxaban-based versus VKA-based regimens for net adverse clinical events (NACE: all-cause death, myocardial infarction [MI], ischemic stroke, systemic thromboembolism [SEE], valve thrombosis, or ISTH major bleeding).
- Compare the regimens for ISTH major bleeding.

**Secondary Objectives**

- Compare alternative NACE definitions (TIMI, BARC, GUSTO), MACE, MACCE, cardiovascular mortality, stroke subtypes, SEE, MI, valve thrombosis, and repeat target-lesion revascularization.
- Compare ISTH, TIMI, BARC and GUSTO bleeding endpoints, intracranial/life-threatening/fatal bleeding, mortality, adverse events (AEs), laboratory parameters, ECG and vital signs.

**Exploratory Objectives**

- Compare cardiovascular hospital admissions (>=24 hours), length of stay, ICU days, PACT-Q treatment satisfaction, EQ-5D-5L quality of life, and optional biomarkers.

## 2. Study Methods

### 2.1 Trial Design

- **Design Type:** Multinational, multicentre, prospective, randomized, open-label, blinded endpoint evaluation (PROBE), parallel-group phase 3b trial; an independent CEC adjudicates events and an independent DSMB monitors safety.
- **Allocation Ratio:** 1:1 edoxaban-based regimen:VKA-based regimen.
- **Intervention Summary:** Randomized treatment starts within 24 hours of randomization and lasts up to 36 months (minimum 12 months, except last enrolled subjects minimum 6 months), followed by 30 days' observational follow-up.
  - **Edoxaban-based regimen:** Edoxaban 60 mg once daily, or 30 mg once daily when dose-reduction criteria in the locally approved label are met.
  - **VKA-based regimen:** Locally approved VKA, investigator dose-adjusted to INR 2.0-3.0; in Japan, 1.6-2.6 for age >=70 and 2.0-3.0 for age <70.
  - **Antiplatelet co-therapy:** Predeclared at randomization; no APT or SAPT up to 90 days without another indication; SAPT indefinitely after PCI, with DAPT only up to 3 months after each stent; ASA 75-100 mg/day or clopidogrel 75 mg/day preferred.

### 2.2 Randomization

- **Method:** IXRS assigns a unique identifier and treatment; an independent biostatistician generates the schedule under IXRS-vendor oversight. No crossover is permitted.
- **Stratification Factors:** TAVI procedure with stenting (yes/no) and need for edoxaban dose adjustment under local label (yes/no).
- **Minimization or Blocking:** The schedule contains a randomization block number; block size and minimization method are not reported in protocol.

### 2.3 Sample Size

**Outcome: NACE**

- **Planned Sample Size:** Approximately 1400 randomized participants, event-driven.
- **Outcome Used for Calculation:** NACE, a composite of all-cause death, MI, ischemic stroke, SEE, valve thrombosis, and ISTH major bleeding.
- **Outcome Type:** Time-to-first composite event/hazard ratio.
- **Assumed Control-Arm Value:** VKA annual NACE rate 14%.
- **Assumed Treatment-Arm Value:** Assumed observed hazard ratio 0.95.
- **Target Difference / Effect Size:** Noninferiority HR margin 1.38.
- **Variability Assumption:** Not reported in protocol; the event-driven calculation reports an annual rate and anticipated median follow-up of two years.
- **Alpha (Significance Level):** 0.05.
- **Power:** 80% for NACE noninferiority.
- **Statistical Test Assumed:** Noninferiority assessment of the HR using the upper bound of a two-sided 95% CI against 1.38.
- **Required Number of Events:** At least 320 NACE events across both arms.
- **Non-Inferiority / Equivalence Margin:** HR 1.38.
- **Dropout / Non-Adherence Inflation:** The sample size or follow-up duration may be adjusted for actual versus expected endpoint and dropout rates; numerical inflation is not reported in protocol.
- **Design Effect / Clustering:** Not applicable: individually randomized parallel-group design.
- **Basis for Assumptions:** Published literature and ENGAGE AF-TIMI 48; Executive Committee review for margin acceptability.
- **Sample Size Re-Estimation / Amendments:** Possible adjustment of sample size or follow-up based on observed endpoint and dropout rates.
- **Software Used for Calculation:** Not reported in protocol.
- **Sample Size Rationale:** With 14% annual control event rate and two-year median follow-up, approximately 1400 participants yield at least 320 NACE events for 80% power.

### 2.4 Hypothesis Framework
Four confirmatory hypotheses are tested sequentially: NACE noninferiority; ISTH major-bleeding noninferiority; ISTH major-bleeding superiority; and NACE superiority. Steps 1-2 require upper two-sided 95% CI HR <1.38, and steps 3-4 require upper bound <1.00; failure stops the sequence.

### 2.5 Statistical Interim Analyses

- **Interim Analyses Planned:** No formal statistical interim analyses. The independent DSMB is estimated to conduct five unblinded safety reviews.
- **Stopping Guidelines:** DSMB stopping/modified-protocol criteria are specified in the DSMB charter, not in this document.
- **Alpha Spending or Adjustment:** Not applicable: no formal interim analysis; confirmatory type-I error is controlled by the final hierarchical procedure.

### 2.6 Timing of Analyses

- **Final Analysis Timing:** Main endpoint analysis is ITT over the overall study period: randomization (or first dose for non-ITT sets) to Month 36/EOT, last visit, or death, whichever occurs first; incomplete follow-up is censored at last known status.
- **Outcome Assessment Schedule:** Baseline/randomization; Months 3, 6, 12, 18, 24, 30 and EOT/Month 36 (±14 days); post-treatment follow-up at one month (±7 days); then 6-monthly follow-up contacts. PACT-Q/EQ-5D-5L are baseline, M3, and M12 or EOT, whichever first.

## 3. Statistical Principles

### 3.1 Significance and Confidence Intervals

- **Alpha Level:** 0.05 for sample-size testing; the confirmatory hierarchy uses two-sided 95% CIs.
- **Multiplicity Adjustment:** Hierarchical gatekeeping across the four primary hypotheses; adjudicated secondary efficacy and safety analyses have no multiplicity adjustment and are descriptive-exploratory.
- **Confidence Intervals:** Two-sided 95% CIs for hazard ratios and event summaries.

### 3.2 Protocol Deviations

- **Adherence Definition:** Edoxaban compliance is returned/tablets expected x100, summarized <80% and >=80%; VKA compliance is evaluated through INR, including Rosendaal linear interpolation time in range.
- **Adherence Assessment:** Each visit/telephone assessment uses pill count, subject/physician report, medication log and, for VKA, INR; an additional assessment follows suspected endpoints.
- **Protocol Deviation Definition:** Major deviations include material eligibility violation, timing error, wrong treatment >=5 days, late first dose, prohibited co-medication, serious noncompliance and specified dosing/DAPT violations. Incorrect stratification is corrected but does not exclude PP.
- **Protocol Deviation Reporting:** Number/percentage with major deviations excluding PP and subject-level reason listing; the major-violation list is reviewed and finalized before database lock.

### 3.3 Analysis Populations

- **Intention-to-Treat:** All randomized participants, assigned randomized regimen regardless of receipt/error/dose change; randomization is reference date.
- **Per Protocol:** Randomized participants receiving >=1 assigned-regimen dose without a listed major protocol violation; first dose is reference date.
- **Other Populations:** Enrolled: signed ICF; randomized: randomized; mITT: randomized and >=1 assigned dose; safety: same eligibility as mITT but actual treatment if entirely incorrect; PACT-Q/EQ-5D-5L: mITT criteria plus evaluable baseline and >=1 post-baseline questionnaire.

## 4. Trial Population

### 4.1 Screening Data
Written informed consent and every eligibility criterion are required before randomization. Sites maintain a screening record, documenting each screen failure and reason.

### 4.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary
Eligible adults have successful TAVI (correct single-valve position; gradient <20 mmHg, peak velocity <3.0 m/s, regurgitation <=2; no overt stroke/uncontrolled bleeding) and pre-existing or new AF (>30 seconds ECG) requiring chronic OAC. Key exclusions include high bleeding risk, contraindication to either treatment, prolonged DAPT requirement, recent stroke/MI/major surgery, mechanical valve, severe renal/hepatic disease, instability, active malignancy, specified laboratory abnormalities, pregnancy, investigator-assessed inability to comply, prior randomization, substance dependence, or life expectancy <6 months.

### 4.3 Recruitment
Approximately 1400 participants are planned from more than 200 sites in North America, Europe and Asia; other countries may participate.

### 4.4 Withdrawal and Follow-up

- **Withdrawal Levels:** Study treatment may end at EOT, withdrawal, or discontinuation; all exposed participants are asked to consent to 6-monthly follow-up through study end.
- **Withdrawal Data Collection:** EOT records last dose/time and unused medication; early discontinuers are asked to complete post-treatment follow-up, with phone/medical-record follow-up if needed.
- **Reasons for Withdrawal:** Early study-medication withdrawal/discontinuation is listed by primary reason and summarized by month/treatment; Kaplan-Meier plots are planned.

### 4.5 Baseline Characteristics

- **Baseline Variables:**
  - Demographics: age and age categories (<65, 65-<75, 75-<80, 80-<85, 85-<90, >=90), sex, ethnicity, race, country and region; smoking status, cigarettes/day and duration/quit-time for current/former smokers.
  - Measurements/renal status: height, weight and <=45/<=60-kg categories, BMI and categories, body surface area, sitting BP, heart rate and temperature; local creatinine clearance and categories, recalculated CKD-EPI/MDRD eGFR and CKD stage.
  - Randomization/clinical status: dose-reduction factors (CrCl, weight, P-gp inhibitor), prior 30-day antiplatelet/anticoagulant use; AF category, labile INR, coronary/carotid/peripheral disease, MI/PCI/CABG, heart failure/NYHA/ejection fraction, stroke/SEE, diabetes, hypertension, lipids, alcohol, bleeding/ICH, valve/respiratory history, CHA2DS2-VASc, HAS-BLED, EuroSCORE I/II and STS.
  - Procedure/assessment variables: medical/surgical history, physical examination, 12-lead ECG result/findings, TAVI success/device/complications/gradient/velocity/regurgitation, PCI timing/access/lesion/stent details, and baseline five-domain EQ-5D-5L/VAS and seven-item PACT-Q1.
- **Summary Methods:** ITT by treatment; continuous variables use n, mean, SD, quartiles, median, minimum and maximum; categorical variables use frequencies and percentages. Baseline is the last nonmissing assessment on/before first study medication.

## 5. Analysis

### 5.1 Primary Outcomes

**Outcome: NACE (primary efficacy)**

- **Definition:** Time to first all-cause death, MI, ischemic stroke, SEE, valve thrombosis, or ISTH major bleeding, adjudicated by the blinded CEC.
- **Measurement Units:** Time to first event; summaries include participant counts/percentages, subject-year exposure and annual event rate.
- **Timing of Measurement:** Overall study period in ITT for primary analysis; event time runs randomization to first event, M36/EOT, last visit or death.
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Composite is derived as first qualifying component event; CEC adjudication prevails.

**Outcome: ISTH major bleeding (primary safety)**

- **Definition:** Time to first clinically overt bleed with hemoglobin fall >=2 g/dL, >=2-unit transfusion, critical-site/organ bleeding, or fatal outcome.
- **Measurement Units:** Time to first event; counts/percentages and two-sided 95% CIs are summarized.
- **Timing of Measurement:** Overall study period in ITT for primary analysis; supplementary safety/PP analyses use specified alternative periods.
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** CEC adjudication; ISTH major, CRNM and minor categories and bleeding location are derived.

### 5.2 Secondary Outcomes

**Outcome: Key secondary efficacy endpoints**

- **Definition:** Time to first alternative TIMI/BARC/GUSTO NACE, MACE, MACCE, cardiovascular death, stroke subtype/fatality, SEE, MI, valve thrombosis, or repeat target-lesion revascularization.
- **Measurement Units:** Time to first adjudicated event/composite event and HR.
- **Timing of Measurement:** Overall study period, ITT, with first event used for each endpoint/composite.
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Composite endpoints are first component occurrence; CEC interpretation prevails except repeat target-lesion revascularization.

**Outcome: Secondary safety endpoints**

- **Definition:** ISTH major, CRNM, minor and any bleeding; major/CRNM location, event counts, fatal/nonfatal major bleeding, ICH and life-threatening bleeding; TIMI major, major-and/or-minor, minor, minimal and any bleeding; BARC 3-or-5, 2/3/5, 3, 3a, 3b, 3c, 2, 1 and any bleeding; GUSTO severe/life-threatening, severe/life-threatening-or-moderate, moderate, mild and any bleeding. Additional secondary safety endpoints are all-cause/CV mortality, AEs, laboratory parameters, ECG, physical examination and vital signs.
- **Measurement Units:** Time to first adjudicated event or first component event for composites; routine safety is descriptive.
- **Timing of Measurement:** Primary and key-secondary adjudicated safety analyses are specified across ITT, Safety and PP sets for the overall, on-treatment and initial-dose-to-final-dose-plus-30-day periods. Secondary bleeding comparisons specifically use ITT and mITT for the overall and initial-dose-to-final-dose-plus-30-day periods. Routine safety is summarized on the Safety set.
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** ISTH/TIMI/BARC/GUSTO classifications and adjudicated locations/categories are derived.

### 5.3 Analysis Methods

- **Primary Analysis Methods:** Cox proportional-hazards model of time to first event includes treatment and both randomization stratifiers; reports edoxaban:VKA HR, p value and two-sided 95% CI, using maximum partial likelihood/Breslow ties (SAS PHREG). Kaplan-Meier curves and cumulative event rates are presented.
- **Covariate Adjustment:** Treatment group plus TAVI-with-stenting and dose-reduction stratifiers; a subgroup variable is omitted as a covariate when defining that subgroup.
- **Assumption Checks:** Proportional hazards assessed graphically with log(-log) and scaled Schoenfeld-residual plots.
- **Alternative Methods if Assumptions Fail:** Logistic regression with treatment and the two stratifiers if serious PH violations occur.
- **Sensitivity Analyses:** For NACE, repeat the ITT overall-study-period analysis in PP and use mITT for on-treatment and initial-dose-to-final-dose-plus-30-day analyses. For ISTH major bleeding, repeat in PP and use Safety or PP for supplemental periods; secondary bleeding uses ITT and mITT. These robustness comparisons are descriptive-exploratory.
- **Subgroup Analyses:** NACE and ISTH major bleeding: age (<85/>=85; <75/>=75), sex, race, North America/Europe/Asia, weight <=/>60 kg and <=/>50 kg, BMI <20/20-<30/>=30 kg/m2, Cockcroft-Gault CrCl (<15, 15-30, >30-<50, 50-<80, 80-<95, >=95 mL/min), dose-reduction requirement, CHA2DS2-VASc (0-1/2/>=3), HAS-BLED (<3/>=3), CHF/stroke/MI/hypertension/diabetes, AF type, PCI/CABG and centre VKA TTR (<60%/>=60%). Categories may be redefined for final subgroup counts; treatment-by-subgroup interaction and forest plots are used.

### 5.4 Missing Data

- **Missing Data Assumptions:** Analyses use observed data; no missing data are imputed except partial dates.
- **Handling Methods:** Participants without a qualifying event are censored at the end of the applicable analysis period or last known outcome status; incomplete follow-up uses last known status. Partial dates for treatment, medication and AEs use prespecified imputation rules. Missing AE severity is imputed mild before first dose and severe on/after first dose; missing relationship is unrelated before first dose and related on/after first dose. Character laboratory results are converted to coded analytic values while raw character values remain listed; missing laboratory units are assigned only after documented review and otherwise remain missing.

### 5.5 Additional Analyses
PACT-Q1 has seven expectation items scored 1-5; PACT-Q2 has convenience (11 items) and satisfaction (7 items) dimensions, rescaled to 0-100 with higher scores denoting greater convenience/satisfaction. EQ-5D-5L comprises mobility, self-care, usual activities, pain/discomfort and anxiety/depression, plus utility index and 0-100 VAS. Both are descriptive-exploratory; MMRM compares PACT-Q scores, while EQ MMRM includes treatment and both randomization stratifiers. Biomarker and HEOR/HCRU analyses are deferred to separate SAPs/reports; PK and PD are not applicable. COVID-19-related data are summarized separately.

### 5.6 Harms

- **Adverse Event Definitions:** A treatment-emergent AE (TEAE) begins or worsens on/after first study medication; an event more than 30 days after the last dose is not TEAE. Events are coded with MedDRA v19.1.
- **Safety Analysis Methods:** Safety-set TEAE summaries use MedDRA primary system organ class/preferred term and report all, drug-related, non-serious, serious, severe, fatal, interruption and permanent-withdrawal events; repeated preferred terms count once at greatest severity/strictest relationship. TEAEs occurring in >=1% and >=5% in either group are separately summarized; non-TEAEs are separately listed, and an AE-of-special-interest-excluded overview is provided. Labs, vital signs and ECG use scheduled/analysis windows and descriptive changes.
- **Grading Scales and Causality:** Bleeding is classified by ISTH, TIMI, BARC and GUSTO; investigator-assessed drug relationship and severity are summarized.

### 5.7 Software and References

- **Statistical Software:** SAS version 9.2 or higher for statistical analyses; SAS PHREG for primary Cox models.
- **Other Referenced Documents:** CSP versions/amendments through v5.0 (17-Apr-2020), eCRF v6/v8, CEC Charter v1.0, DSMB Charter v1.0, separate TLF shells, and separate biomarker/HEOR SAPs.
