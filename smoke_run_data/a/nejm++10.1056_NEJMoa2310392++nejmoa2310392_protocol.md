# Statistical Analysis Plan

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Background and Rationale

TIMELESS evaluates whether IV tenecteplase improves clinical outcomes versus placebo in imaging-selected acute ischemic stroke presenting 4.5–24 hours after last-known-well time. The rationale is to achieve earlier reperfusion in patients with salvageable tissue and ICA/MCA occlusion, including patients needing transfer for endovascular therapy.

### 1.2 Objectives

**Primary Objectives**

- Compare efficacy of tenecteplase with placebo using ordinal Day-90 modified Rankin Scale (mRS).

**Secondary Objectives**

- Evaluate efficacy using Day-90 functional independence, angiographic reperfusion, NIHSS, Barthel Index, Glasgow Outcome Scale, 24-hour reperfusion and 24-hour recanalization; evaluate safety using sICH, adverse events, mortality, and PH2.

**Exploratory Objectives**

- Evaluate final infarct volume/growth, mRS subgroups, thrombectomy-not-performed outcomes, 30-day readmission, one-pass EVT, Neuro-QoL, and Day-90 survival.

## 2. Study Methods

### 2.1 Trial Design

- **Design Type:** Phase III, prospective, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial in imaging-eligible 4.5–24-hour AIS with ICA or MCA (M1/M2) occlusion.
- **Allocation Ratio:** 1:1.
- **Intervention Summary:** Both groups receive guideline-based standard care; ICA/M1 patients may receive adjuvant EVT if guideline criteria are met.
  - Tenecteplase: 0.25 mg/kg IV, maximum 25 mg, one bolus over 5 seconds.
  - Placebo: one bolus injection over 5 seconds.

### 2.2 Randomization

- **Method:** IWRS assigns treatment kit and randomizes eligible consented patients; treat as soon as possible.
- **Stratification Factors:** Age <=70/>70 years; occlusion ICA/M1 versus M2; baseline NIHSS <=15/>15; ECC versus nECC.
- **Minimization or Blocking:** Permuted blocks provide approximately 1:1 balance within each stratum.

### 2.3 Sample Size

- **Planned Sample Size:** 456 patients, approximately 228 per group; 432 evaluable after 5% dropout.
- **Outcome Used for Calculation:** Day-90 ordinal mRS.
- **Outcome Type:** Seven-level ordinal mRS (0–6).
- **Assumed Control-Arm Value:** mRS 0–6 distribution: 13%, 13%, 12%, 14%, 18%, 12%, 18%.
- **Assumed Treatment-Arm Value:** mRS 0–6 distribution: 17%, 20%, 16%, 15%, 13%, 7%, 12%.
- **Target Difference / Effect Size:** Common odds ratio at least 1.76, tenecteplase versus placebo.
- **Variability Assumption:** Assumed ordinal mRS distributions; alternative historical scenarios gave >99% and >88% power.
- **Alpha (Significance Level):** Two-sided 0.049 after adjustment for one efficacy interim analysis.
- **Power:** At least 90%.
- **Statistical Test Assumed:** Wilcoxon–Mann–Whitney test for calculation.
- **Required Number of Events:** Not applicable: this is not an event-driven design.
- **Non-Inferiority / Equivalence Margin:** Not applicable: superiority design.
- **Dropout / Non-Adherence Inflation:** 5% dropout.
- **Design Effect / Clustering:** Not applicable: individually randomized trial; no cluster design.
- **Basis for Assumptions:** Assumed mRS distributions and alternative scenarios derived from prior stroke studies.
- **Sample Size Re-Estimation / Amendments:** Not reported in protocol.
- **Software Used for Calculation:** EAST 6.4.1.
- **Sample Size Rationale:** 456 provides 90% power to detect the specified mRS distribution difference after 5% dropout.

### 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

All efficacy endpoints are assessed for superiority. For the primary endpoint, H0 is common odds ratio=1 versus two-sided H1 not equal to 1; an odds ratio represents odds of lower (better) mRS for tenecteplase versus placebo.

### 2.5 Statistical Interim Analyses

- **Interim Analyses Planned:** Safety reviews after 25, 50, 100, and 200 patients complete 72–96-hour assessment; one efficacy analysis at 228 patients with Day-90 assessment.
- **Stopping Guidelines:** Non-binding sICH boundaries are 8/25, 12/50, 19/100, 32/200, and 36/228; iDMC may recommend stopping/amendment for safety or efficacy.
- **Alpha Spending or Adjustment:** Lan-DeMets alpha spending approximating O’Brien–Fleming; two-sided .003 interim and .049 final, preserving family-wise type-I error .05.

### 2.6 Timing of Analyses

- **Final Analysis Timing:** After all patients complete/discontinue, all data are in the database, and database lock.
- **Outcome Assessment Schedule:** 24-hour imaging/NIHSS window 18–30 hours; 72–96-hour NIHSS 60–96 and imaging 60–120 hours; Day 5 4–6 days for mRS/GOS/BI; Day 30 15–60 and Day 90 61–120 days for clinical outcomes.

## 3. Statistical Principles

### 3.1 Significance and Confidence Intervals

- **Alpha Level:** Primary .003 at interim or .049 final, two-sided.
- **Multiplicity Adjustment:** Gatekept sequential secondary tests after successful primary: functional independence, recanalization, reperfusion, angiographic reperfusion, NIHSS, BI, then GOS; stop sequence at failed test.
- **Confidence Intervals:** Two-sided 95% CIs; Wald CIs for model odds ratios and Hodges–Lehmann 95% CIs for location shifts.

### 3.2 Protocol Deviations

- **Adherence Definition:** Available efficacy data are analyzed regardless of protocol adherence; PP excludes key major deviations.
- **Adherence Assessment:** Key major deviation list finalized blinded before database lock; COVID-related versus unrelated PP exclusions distinguished.
- **Protocol Deviation Definition:** Key major protocol deviations are not individually defined in the protocol; list is finalized before lock.
- **Protocol Deviation Reporting:** Major deviations are listed and summarized by treatment; investigator documents/explains deviations and promptly reports those affecting safety/data integrity.

### 3.3 Analysis Populations

- **Intention-to-Treat:** All randomized patients who provided informed consent, analyzed as randomized.
- **Per Protocol:** ITT patients without key major protocol deviations.
- **Other Populations:** Safety population: randomized, consented, and receiving any study drug; analyzed as actually treated.

## 4. Trial Population

### 4.1 Screening Data

Screening documents consent, demographics, history, baseline conditions, weight, glucose/coagulation, pregnancy test, CBC/chemistry, baseline mRS/NIHSS and qualifying imaging. Patients need signed consent, age >=18, 4.5–24-hour AIS, ICA/M1/M2 occlusion and target mismatch; ICA enrollment is capped at 15%.

### 4.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

Eligible patients have pre-stroke mRS 0–2, NIHSS >=5 both baseline and immediately pre-randomization, qualifying CTA/MRA plus CTP/MRP target mismatch (core <70 mL, ratio >=1.8, mismatch >=15 mL), and capacity to comply. Key exclusions include bleeding/coagulopathy/anticoagulants, prior thrombolysis, relevant intracranial pathology, uncontrolled hypertension, glucose extremes, prior recent stroke/surgery, pregnancy, and prohibitive imaging findings.

### 4.3 Recruitment

Approximately 456 patients are enrolled at approximately 90 ECC/nECC sites, primarily in the United States and Canada.

### 4.4 Withdrawal and Follow-up

- **Withdrawal Levels:** Patient may withdraw voluntarily; investigator may withdraw patient; sponsor may terminate study or site.
- **Withdrawal Data Collection:** Make every effort to obtain information; document primary withdrawal reason in eCRF; study completion is Day 90 +/-14 days.
- **Reasons for Withdrawal:** Includes withdrawal of consent, study termination, or site closure; patients are not replaced.

### 4.5 Baseline Characteristics

- **Baseline Variables:** Age, sex, race, ethnicity, baseline mRS, baseline NIHSS, vital signs/laboratories, medical history and stroke characteristics.
- **Summary Methods:** Continuous variables: n, mean, SD, median, minimum, maximum; categorical: n/% including unknown/missing. Baseline is last pre-dose value, or pre-randomization if undosed.

## 5. Analysis

### 5.1 Primary Outcomes

**Outcome: Ordinal mRS at Day 90**

- **Definition:** Disability scale 0 (no symptoms) through 5 (severe disability); death scored 6.
- **Measurement Units:** Ordinal mRS category 0–6.
- **Timing of Measurement:** Day 90, analysis window 61–120 days after randomization.
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Death before a scheduled assessment is assigned mRS 6. Missing Day-90 mRS is LOCF from Day 30 or Day 5/discharge for primary analysis; sequential ordinal multiple imputation and tipping point are robustness analyses.

### 5.2 Secondary Outcomes

**Outcome: Functional independence**
- **Definition:** Day-90 mRS 0–2.
- **Measurement Units:** Proportion of patients.
- **Timing of Measurement:** Day 90.
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Uses primary mRS imputations.

**Outcome: Recanalization and reperfusion**
- **Definition:** Complete CTA/MRA recanalization; reperfusion is >90% reduction from baseline in Tmax>6-second lesion volume.
- **Measurement Units:** Proportion of patients.
- **Timing of Measurement:** 24 hours post-randomization; imaging window 18–30 hours.
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Central-reader imaging used.

**Outcome: Angiographic reperfusion, NIHSS, BI, and GOS**
- **Definition:** Angiographic reperfusion=TICI 2b/3 in endovascular patients; NIHSS 0–42 with higher scores worse; BI >=95; good recovery is rescaled GOS=1.
- **Measurement Units:** Angiographic/BI/GOS outcomes are proportions; NIHSS is ordinal total score.
- **Timing of Measurement:** Angiographic endpoint at procedure completion; NIHSS, BI and GOS secondary endpoints at Day 90.
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Death before Day 90 yields GOS 5 then scale is reversed; missing NIHSS items yield missing total and are not imputed.

### 5.3 Analysis Methods

- **Primary Analysis Methods:** Proportional odds model for Day-90 mRS, adjusted according to the variable-selection algorithm; WMW GenOR if model fitting fails or proportional-odds score test <=.05; unadjusted WMW supportive analysis.
- **Covariate Adjustment:** Candidate variables: age, occlusion type, baseline NIHSS, site, baseline thrombectomy planned, and protocol version; retain an algorithm-selected subset to avoid sparse cells.
- **Assumption Checks:** Overall proportional-odds score test; p>.05 acceptable.
- **Alternative Methods if Assumptions Fail:** Stratified WMW GenOR with 95% CI.
- **Sensitivity Analyses:** COVID hypothetical strategy, PP Day-90 LOCF, and MI tipping-point analysis varying delta.
- **Subgroup Analyses:** Primary mRS by occlusion, site, age, NIHSS, infarct volume, onset-to-randomization, ICA/M1/M2, protocol version, thrombectomy planned/performed, and drug-to-groin-puncture tertiles.

### 5.4 Missing Data

- **Missing Data Assumptions:** Non-mRS missing remains missing unless stated. mRS MI assumes MAR when delta=0; delta>0 evaluates MNAR departures.
- **Handling Methods:** No post-baseline mRS means exclusion from mRS analyses; death is mRS 6; LOCF is primary; sequential treatment-specific ordinal MI creates 100 datasets for robustness; Neuro-QoL requires >=4 or >=50% answered items, otherwise missing.

### 5.5 Additional Analyses

Exploratory analyses use logistic regression for thrombectomy/readmission/one-pass outcomes, van Elteren tests and Hodges–Lehmann estimates for infarct volume/growth, linear models for Neuro-QoL domains, and stratified log-rank/Kaplan–Meier for Day-90 survival if at least five deaths per group. Pharmacokinetic/pharmacodynamic analyses are not planned.

### 5.6 Harms

- **Adverse Event Definitions:** TEAE is a new AE or worsening condition on/after dosing; missing/partial onset dates follow specified TEAE rules.
- **Safety Analysis Methods:** Safety population, actual treatment, descriptive only/no hypothesis tests; summarize MedDRA-coded AEs, SAEs, related AEs, discontinuations, deaths, AESIs, sICH, PH2, labs and vital signs.
- **Grading Scales and Causality:** CTCAE v5.0 grades severity. sICH is >=4-point NIHSS worsening versus proximal NIHSS attributed to CT-preferred/MRI bleed within 36 hours; AE causality is investigator-assessed.

### 5.7 Software and References

- **Statistical Software:** EAST 6.4.1 for sample-size calculation; SAS procedures including PROC MIANALYZE are specified for analysis code.
- **Other Referenced Documents:** Final SAP V5 was based on Protocol V4 and finalized before database lock/unblinding; iDMC Charter, independent imaging review charter, and Neuro-QoL Scoring Manual v2.0 are referenced.
